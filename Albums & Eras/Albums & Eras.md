# Albums & Eras

The whole catalogue is one story. Here it is in three acts, in order: the flood, the machine, the circus. Each album stands fine on its own, but heard back to back they turn into one long argument between a heart and the voice that guards it.

## 🌊 Act I: Subject to Flooding

**[[Subject to Flooding]]** (2018). The flood and the wound. A debut made in the wilderness years that diagnoses the disease the rest of the catalogue spends years treating. Its we-not-me heart already holds the seed of everything after: the protector who will one day get a name, the borrowed older voices, the first naming of [[The Circle]].

## 🎰 Act II: 33

**[[33]]** (2022). Waking up inside the machine. The private wound of the debut turns outward and becomes an X-ray of the attention age: the scroll, the slot machine, the smiling screen. It asks the catalogue's central question at the album's literal center, then answers it with a founding myth at the close, all before any of it is named "Lucius." If [[Happy Face]] was your door, it lives here.

## 🎪 Act III: Lucius Lullaby

**[[Lucius Lullaby (album)|Lucius Lullaby]]** (2026). The protector gets the microphone. A sustained descent that holds its breath almost to the end, where the shadow finally gets top billing and the only thing that truly resolves is the survival of the feeling underneath. For the first time, the project shares its name with the voice it was built to contain.

## Between the acts

**[[Singles & EPs]]**. The connective tissue, and more than once the actual plot. The big turns kept happening off-album: the dream that births [[Sir Lucius]], and the 2023 to 2025 run of singles and EPs that quietly assembles the figure the 2026 album finally names.

## The theatrical spine

**[[The Interview]]**. Not an album. Watch it anyway. A seven-part video series in which [[Roy]] and his shadow [[Sir Lucius]] argue across a table over who really made the music. Dozens of songs land differently once you have watched it.
