# The Abyss

> [!gist] The center of the whole journey
> The Abyss is the gap between worlds, the one crossing every seeker has to make alone, and the place you get stuck if you don't. It's in the lyrics and tweeted *"the edge of The Abyss,"*. Esoteric systems teach that knowledge which never becomes understanding leaves you *"stuck in the abyss between worlds."* It's the It It is the leap of faith, the place you enter even when you can't fully see the landscape. 
> Within the catalogue, it is the Fool's leap over the edge where the heart leads and the mind surrenders, holding tightly to each other.  You don't go around it, there is no going around what is central to yourself; that just leads back to the loop.

**Where you've heard it**
- Roy's 2022 *"Good Friday"* tweet: *"At the moment I am in the dark. **At the edge of The Abyss.**"*
- His IG Live teaching: knowledge that never rises to understanding leaves you *"stuck in the **abyss between worlds**."*
- The **[[Sir Rabbit]]** poem's leap over *"the **Great Divide**"* — the Abyss made into a story.

*That's the whole idea. Open a drawer below only if you want the deeper reading, none of it is required.*

---

> [!map]- Roy's own word (why this one is grounded)
> This isn't a lens laid over the work; Roy names the Abyss himself, over and over. On **Good Friday 2022** he tweeted, in the first person, that he was *"in the dark. At the edge of The Abyss."* On his *"I'm 33"* board he writes it three ways: the Hero's-Journey Ordeal is labeled *"In the Abyss, Facing the Shadow Self"*; the *"Tree thinking"* pyramid runs *"Abyss In"* at the top down to *"Abyss Out"* at the base; and the 0–10 emanation ladder marks *"Abyss (knowledge is relation)"* between stages three and four. The Crown notebook he minted, *"The City of the Pyramids"* (Crowley's name for the realm *past* the Abyss, where the ego burns away), even spells out the toll: *"sacrifice lesser aspects of self for greater."*

> [!lore]- What the Abyss actually is (the gap between worlds)
> In the Tree of Life the Abyss is the break between the top three sephirot (the divine, effectively unreachable) and the lower seven (where we live). The only way across is **Da'at**, *knowledge* — the hidden "non-sephirah" gate that the body-map places at the **throat.** That's why this project keeps staging the crossing at the mouth: Da'at is *"the abyss becoming speech"* (see [[The Mouth]], [[The Voice]]), and the *El Roi* tattoo sits on [[Sir Lucius]]'s throat, right at the gate. Roy's own gloss says the same thing twice: on the board, *"knowledge is relation"*; on his IG Live, information that never rises to understanding leaves you *"stuck in the abyss between worlds."* The Abyss is where knowledge goes to die unless it turns into something lived.

> [!reading]- The leap: Sir Rabbit at the Great Divide
> The clearest *story* of the crossing is the [[Sir Rabbit]] poem (what used to be its own page, "The Divide"). Near the end the seeker reaches *"an edge below the Great Divide,"* and a golden-caped Spider tells him the way across is over the side and *through the sun.* Then the tell: the instant the rabbit panics (*"I'm not ready to fly!"*), the Spider hands the choice straight back (*"thats up to you it's not for my to pry"*) and will **not** push. That refusal is the crossing's whole law: a leap made *for* you isn't a crossing at all. He jumps while still afraid and lands *"back where he begun,"* same spot, not the same rabbit. It is the **Fool's leap** almost to the letter, and Roy drew the same picture in his own public caption for [[No. 1 Champion (la Conquistadora)]] (signed *El Roy*): *"I saw her divinity, but not the one who held it… I followed anyway, white rose in my left hand, and stepped off the ledge"* — the Rider-Waite Fool (card 0), the white rose, the cliff, the step taken on faith with recognition outrunning certainty.

> [!mask]- The false rescuer at the edge (Lucius)
> Every Abyss has someone standing at it, offering to spare you the drop. In [[The Interview]], Lucius says it outright: *"You naive little god. Without me, you'll **drown**."* That's the counterfeit crossing. He doesn't shove you over, and he doesn't leave the choice open the way the Spider does; he sells you a bridge that circles back to him, mastery, metrics, control, in place of the actual leap. The true guide leaves the uncertainty intact. Lucius offers you a way to skip it, and skipping it is the one thing that guarantees you never cross. (On the guardian who stands at every such gate, see [[The Dweller on the Threshold]].)

> [!warning]- Facing the shadow (the Ordeal)
> On Roy's Hero's-Journey wheel the Abyss *is* the Ordeal, labeled *"In the Abyss, Facing the Shadow Self,"* with a plunge-into-the-dark note beside it. So the crossing isn't only cosmic geometry; it's the shadow-confrontation, you meet what you buried ([[Sir Lucius]]) at the narrowest point of the road. The *City of the Pyramids* page names the price in Crowley's terms: the ego burns, *"lesser aspects of self sacrificed for greater,"* which is the same death-to-be-remade that [[The Circle]] means by going *"the inside of the inside."* (Framework held as framework: the Thelemic and Kabbalistic apparatus is the lens the work rhymes with, not a doctrine Roy has declared.)

> [!question]- Where it stays open
> Is the Abyss crossed once, or every day? *"Abyss In / Abyss Out"* could be a single descent-and-return or the rhythm of a whole life. And the sharpest question Roy's own words raise: if you get *"stuck in the abyss between worlds,"* is that only failure, or is it the necessary **middle** the songs keep naming, *"a man is like an in-between, animating Heaven and Hell"*? The gap you're trapped in and the gate you cross may be the same place, seen with and without fear.

> [!receipts]- Sources, and how sure we are
> **Grounded (Roy's public word):** the 2022 *"Good Friday"* tweet (*"at the edge of The Abyss"*); the IG-Live *"stuck in the abyss between worlds"* / Knowledge→Understanding→Wisdom teaching; the board's *"In the Abyss, Facing the Shadow Self,"* *"Abyss In / Abyss Out,"* and *"Abyss (knowledge is relation)"*; the *"City of the Pyramids"* notebook (Hall of Mirrors Crown room #67); Lucius's *"you'll drown"* in [[The Interview]]; the [[Sir Rabbit]] poem's *"Great Divide."* **Strong reading:** that these are one figure, the crossing between worlds, and that the Spider's refusal-to-push and Lucius's drowning-threat are the true and false faces of the same gate. **Framework:** Da'at and the throat, the Thelemic City of the Pyramids, the Dweller lineage. *None of this is the answer; it's one way of seeing it.*
