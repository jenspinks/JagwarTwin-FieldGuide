# Hermeticism - As Above So Below

> **One-line reading.** Hermeticism is the opposite of treating the world as a trap. It holds the cosmos as one coherent, legible law, the same pattern repeating at every scale, and Roy didn't just read up on it. He printed its central maxim on a music video years before anyone worked out where he got it.

## At a glance

- **In one line**: *as above, so below*. One design echoing through every level of reality, from the galaxy down to the atom, a cosmos you can read and climb instead of flee.
- **Where it shows up**: [[Down to You]] (the reversible heaven↔earth refrain; *"AS ABOVE SO BELOW"* printed on the 2021 video end-card) · [[Life Is Good]] (*"my head's an antenna"*) · [[Welcome to the Circus]] (*"Did Hermes paint his face?"*).
- **Connected ideas**: [[The Pattern]] · [[Source vs Reflection]] · [[Sir Lucius]] · [[Duality]] · [[Inversion]] · [[Mary]] · [[Gnosticism - The World as Trap]] · [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]].
- **Confidence**: Roy's *engagement* with Hermeticism is grounded fact. The books are on his shelf, the maxim is on his video, the seven principles are transcribed in his own hand. The song-by-song *mappings* are interpretation, a frame the work fits, not a proven blueprint.

## What it is

Hermeticism is a Western esoteric tradition rooted in late-antique texts attributed to a legendary sage, **Hermes Trismegistus**: the *Corpus Hermeticum*, or *Hermetica*. Its single most famous idea is a line from the **Emerald Tablet**: *as above, so below*. Whatever pattern holds at the largest scale, the cosmos, the divine, repeats at every smaller one, the world, the body, the mind. The universe isn't a deceptive copy you're meant to escape. It's a coherent, lawful order you can *read* and *climb*, one design echoing through every level.

Most of the tradition's modern reach comes from **The Kybalion** (1908), a popular primer that boils it down to **seven principles**:

1. **Mentalism**: *"The All is Mind."* Reality is, at bottom, mental; the universe is a thought.
2. **Correspondence**: *"as above, so below."* The same pattern recurs at every scale. (This is the famous one.)
3. **Vibration**: nothing rests; everything is motion, everything is a frequency.
4. **Polarity**: everything is dual; opposites are two ends of one thing.
5. **Rhythm**: everything swings, like a pendulum, between its poles.
6. **Cause and Effect**: every cause has its effect; nothing is mere chance.
7. **Gender**: a masculine and a feminine principle operate in everything.

The throughline is *coherence*: a legible cosmos, not a hostile one. And here's the part that lifts this out of armchair theory: Roy put **both** the *Hermetica* and *The Kybalion* on his public Instagram Book Club reading list. The books are literally on the shelf. He also posted a quote-card crediting Hermes Trismegistus by name (*"The center is everywhere"*), naming the tradition out loud instead of leaving us to infer it.

A word on how to use any of this: don't push for meaning. Put on an audiobook about Hermeticism, or Kabbalah, or both, then just play the records and let the meaning happen on its own. That's the honest way in, and it's how most of this page got found in the first place.

## How it works

Hermeticism is the most board-grounded of the three esoteric streams the project draws on, because Roy transcribed all seven Kybalion principles, verbatim and in full, onto his *"I'm 33 this year"* spreadsheet, on a tab cheerfully titled *"How to upload myself to the internet."* (Yes. The cosmology and the upload fantasy share a browser tab.) That hands us a rare luxury: we get to watch the principles move through the work one at a time, with the source sitting right there next to them.

**Mentalism**, *"The All is Mind,"* is the premise under the project's whole consciousness-and-upload imagery. If that sounds like a bridge too far, start somewhere you can measure: the placebo effect. The mind alone, we've seen it, named it, measured it, can change the material body. That's a small, sturdy door into a very big idea. The board's "Cybernetics" cell walks the rest of the way and lands on the cleanest statement of it going: *"All the world is mind. Mind woven into matter."* If reality is information at bottom, then a self is information too, and information can, in principle, be externalized. That single move is the engine of the entire upload cosmology (see [[The Upload]], [[The Digital Double]]).

**Correspondence**, *"as above, so below,"* is the principle Roy leans on hardest, so hard he gave it his own name: **The Pattern**. The board's Correspondence cell reads, in part, *"The formula is the same from a solar system down to the atom,"* then lists Adam, Hurricane, Galaxy, Fibonacci, and closes on *"The Pattern is present on all levels."* Small systems reflecting larger ones, inner states mirroring outer reality, the central maxim turned into a working term. It's also the logic the mirror runs on: [[Source vs Reflection]] is *as above, so below* narrowed to a single act of mediation, what's above faithfully reflected below.

**Vibration** is the principle with the most public anchors, which makes it the firmest ground on the page. Three of them. The board cell quotes Tesla straight, *"If you wish to understand the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration,"* and closes on *"Tune to desired frequency."* Roy restated the whole doctrine in his own voice in a 2020 Q&A: *"Everything is a vibrational frequency. Light, matter, sound, it's all the same thing at variable speeds."* And the released lyric *"My head's an antenna / I'm wide awake"* ([[Life Is Good]]) is the principle in first person, mind as a receiver tuned to a frequency. Three anchors lift this past coincidence into documented engagement. The principle also has two darker registers, both in released songs (see below).

**Polarity** is the project's whole obsession with **duality**: the Roy/Lucius split, the *one becomes two* shape of the entire story (see [[Duality]]). The Kybalion's teaching is that opposites are two ends of one thing, and the cleanest way to hold it is a thermostat. Hot isn't good or bad; extreme hot is bad, same with cold, and both are just aspects of one whole called temperature. Harmony is a matter of adjusting the degree, not eliminating a side. Which is why the work's resolution is never to defeat a pole but to re-order the pair, an embrace, not a funeral. It was never about either side *winning*. [[Life Is Good]] even stages the refusal outright: *"Keep a smile on my face, too happy to choose"* declines the this-side/that-side split.

The last three map more lightly, by resonance. **Rhythm** to the Circle-and-Loop recurrence (see [[The Circle]], itself a near-perfectly hermetic allegory, the macro cycle at cosmic scale, *as above*). **Cause and Effect** to the seed-and-karma material. **Gender** to the divine-feminine thread ([[Mary]]) and the board's own Left/Right-hemisphere cell, order and reason and masculine on one side, chaos and creativity and feminine on the other.

And then there's the figure who carries the tradition's *name* into the masks: **[[Sir Lucius]] as Hermes.** The 2026 *welcome to the circus (Live Underground)* card tacked a new line onto the chorus, *"BUT WHO ARE YOU WITHOUT THE MASK? DID HERMES PAINT HIS FACE? I DON'T KNOW, MAYBE,"* surfacing the Hermetic thread in public again, five years after the *Down to You* card. Hermes is the trickster, the psychopomp, the inventor of writing and letters, the namesake of both *Hermeticism* and *hermeneutics*, the art of decoding. It's a near-perfect fit for the face-painting ringmaster of a *"Live UNDERGROUND"* series, and the *"I don't know, maybe"* performs the cipher's own shrug. The card is the anchor. The full equation is a reading.

> [!reading]- Everything Everywhere All at Once (Down to You as the fullest annotation)
> The page has an honesty test waiting for it down in Source notes: a principle-reading only earns its keep if it would *fail* on some songs. So here is the strongest reply this page can make, and it's a single song. [[Down to You]] is the flagship, the one that wore *"AS ABOVE SO BELOW"* on its 2021 end-card, and it doesn't run one Kybalion principle. It runs a fistful at once.
>
> - **Mentalism** and **Correspondence** live in the title itself. *"It all comes down to you"* reads two ways at once: the divine descending *to you*, and *you* as the level where the whole pattern finally lands. Above answered below. It's an I-and-You machine, mirror made grammar.
> - **Polarity** is the refrain refusing to pick a direction: heaven to earth *and* earth to heaven, with the ad-lib *"up from below."* Descent and ascent are the same string, pulled from both ends.
> - **Cause and Effect** is *"comes down to you"* heard as responsibility: nothing here is chance, the weight lands exactly where the line points.
>
> Two more sit on top as esoteric-frame overlays, and these are the loosest threads of the bunch, held as reading and nothing firmer: a **base-to-gold transmutation** register (base needs-metal climbing to the gold of divine connection, the alchemist's ladder) and a **descent-of-Nous** overlay (spirit-mind falling into matter, where the merely logical mind can't grasp it and only contemplation can). Whether Roy reached for either is exactly the kind of thing worth not being sure about. But that's the whole point of parking it here: if one lens can catch *this* many of the seven moving through a single three-minute pop song, it's doing more than decorating. (Interpretation throughout, and the two overlays most of all.)

## Where it appears

- **[[Down to You]]**: the public floor. The 2021 *Down to You (Official Music Video)* end-card printed **"AS ABOVE SO BELOW"** as a sigil, flanking the seeker-question *"WHAT IS IT THAT YOU SEEK?"*, years before Roy's source board was decoded. The mutating refrain stages the maxim as a *reversible exchange*: *"Heaven in the sky to the Earth below / Heaven in your eye from the Earth below,"* with the ad-lib *"up from below."* Descent **and** ascent, the circuit completed, not a one-way drop. (The title *"it all comes down to you"* doubles as responsibility and as emanation, the divine descending *to you.*)
- **[[Life Is Good]]** (JT × angelbaby, 2023): *"My head's an antenna / I'm wide awake"* (Vibration, first person) and *"Keep a smile on my face, too happy to choose"* (Polarity, refusing the pole-pair).
- **[[Online]]** (*33*, 2022): *"And they know my every step / They infiltrate my brain."* The Kybalion's induction/telepathy clause, *"mind over mind,"* inverted from gift into violation; the Vibration doctrine run as surveillance-horror.
- **[[Loser]]** (*Subject to Flooding*, 2018): *"Jericho fell like a wave."* Frequency turned destructive, the wall that falls to a sound, vibration that dismantles containment.
- **[[Welcome to the Circus]]** (*Lucius Lullaby*, 2026): the Hermetic thread named (*"Did Hermes paint his face?"*) and inverted (*"Everything's upside down"*).
- **The "I'm 33" board**: the seven principles transcribed in full; the source of the term *The Pattern*; and a typed *"Tracks"* list (a likely-unreleased concept album running a Hermetic descent/return arc) whose titles recur as [[Hall of Mirrors]] unlock-codes.

> [!reading]- Bad Vibrations (Online and Loser turn the dial the wrong way)
> The board's Vibration cell carries a telepathy clause: thought and emotion come *"accompanied by vibrations… which tend to affect the minds of other persons by induction… telepathy… mind over mind."* On the board that's a gift, minds tuning to each other like radios. Two released songs pick up the same machinery and run it backwards.
>
> **[[Online]]** flips induction into surveillance. *"They infiltrate my brain"* is mind-over-mind as break-in, the telepathic field weaponized into a watcher. The comedy is load-bearing: every transcendent *"from above"* claim gets a pin stuck in it. *"All the shamans and the Brahmans say we come from space"* is undercut a beat later by *"Maybe Jupiter is stupider than people say,"* and the whole cosmic pile-up collapses to *"we're stuck just like a bug inside your database."* The *"Buy! Buy! Buy! / Bye! Bye! Bye!"* homophone makes buying and leaving the same sound, the jingle-as-sedation that lets the horror go down sweet. Vibration doctrine, run as paranoia, then shrugged off. Correspondence, not claimed intent: the song never names the principle.
>
> **[[Loser]]** takes the destructive register. *"Jericho fell like a wave"* is frequency turned to collapse, the wall that comes down to a trumpet blast. The grammar is plain: voices break walls, vibration dismantles containment. The board names the principle, the line lets it ring on the wall.

> [!mask]- Hermes Paints His Face (Lucius as trickster-messenger)
> The 2026 *"Did Hermes paint his face?"* card recasts the face-painting ringmaster as **Hermes**, and the fit is dense. Hermes is the **trickster**; the **psychopomp**, the guide who walks souls through the underworld, which is precisely the job description for a *"Live UNDERGROUND"* series; the **inventor of writing and letters**; the namesake of both **Hermeticism** and **hermeneutics**, the art of decoding. In the alchemical stream he's **Mercurius**, the duplex trickster-spirit, and that maps clean onto Lucius's three registers and his signature move, *tells the truth, but the truth deceives.* He's the guide and the trickster in one body, the messenger who manipulates as much as he leads to the right path. > [!mask]- The Upside Down (the maxim run backwards)
> [[Welcome to the Circus]] runs a *broken* correspondence, *as below, so above*: the material treated as supreme, the supreme dragged down into the muck. *"Spinning you 'round and 'round / Everything's upside down."* The Circus is the as-above-so-below axiom flipped on its head. It's the same move [[Inversion]] names: matter promoted into spirit's chair, the Qliphoth as a Tree-of-Death, the Hanged Man's *voluntary* inversion set against the Circus's *involuntary* one, with the scriptural signatures stamped on it (Isaiah 5:20, *"call evil good"*; Acts 17:6, *"turned the world upside down"*) and the Kabbalistic *tzimtzum* (*"contract to expand"*). A true *as above, so below* affirms the cosmos; its inversion is the counterfeit. (There's even a name floating around for the shadow that counterfeits the summit, *Counterfeit Capricorn*, though that thread's proper home is [[Inversion]] and [[The Light-Bearer]], not this page.)

> [!map]- Star, Moon, Sun (three cards, and who actually holds each)
> The Major Arcana closes on a deliberate light-sequence: **The Star (XVII)**, the morning-star, Venus before dawn, then **The Moon (XVIII)**, reflection and illusion, then **The Sun (XIX)**, the true light at last. It's tempting to hand the whole three-card run to [[Sir Lucius]], and that's the mistake worth not making, because the cards don't all belong to one figure.
>
> - **The Star is Lucius.** The morning-star herald: the light that announces the dawn, gets mistaken *for* the dawn, and is then outshone by the very sunrise it preceded. His clean map is Hod/Mercury (Mercurius); the Venus-morning-star is only a *demoted resonance* on him, the herald outshone by the king.
> - **The Moon is [[Roy]].** The moon doesn't make its own light, it reflects. The sun can be fully hidden and its light still reaches you off the moon's face, which is the whole consolation of the card: the light is there, it's just borrowed and aimed down. That reflected-light position, *and* the redemptive arc of a moon trying to *become* the sun, are Roy's, not Lucius's. Roy also holds Venus/Netzach, two different Venus registers kept strictly apart.
> - **The Sun** is where both poles are headed. Whether the Sun *is* [[SOL]] (Spanish for *sun*, the Song of Love that unsettles Lucius in the theatrical [[The Interview|Roy v Lucius series]]) is left genuinely open, a reading laid alongside the cards, not a settled identity. *Is SOL the sun?* stays a question mark on purpose, and this page says nothing about what is to come with it.
>
> Two guardrails. The map itself has one home, and it's [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]], not this page. And the *only* grounded tarot anchor for Lucius is the **Devil card (XV)**, which Roy named himself by pointing to Paul Foster Case. The Star-Moon-Sun run is an exploratory lens set next to that grounded card, kept openly interpretive.

> [!reading]- Siblings, Not Nesting Dolls (parallel, not nested)
> Two ideas that keep getting mistaken for one. **As-above-so-below** is correspondence of *structure* across scales, the same shape from galaxy to atom. **[[Source vs Reflection]]** is fidelity of a *mediation*, whether a reflection stays true to its source. They overlap where a faithful reflection just *is* a true as-above-so-below. They split where a distorted reflection is a broken correspondence that the Pattern-as-structure doesn't, by itself, name. They rhyme because they share theosophical ancestry, not necessarily because anyone cross-wired them on purpose. Hold them as siblings standing side by side, not one tucked inside the other.

> [!map]- Don't Cross the Streams (the non-negotiable guardrails)
> Three distinctions that have to stay clean, because the same imagery (descent, ascent, the spark) can carry opposite meanings depending on which tradition you're standing in:
>
> - **Hermetic ≠ Gnostic.** Hermeticism is **world-affirming**: the cosmos is a coherent law, legible and climbable. [[Gnosticism - The World as Trap]] is **world-rejecting**: the cosmos is a false copy to flee. Same descent-and-return imagery, *opposite telos.* Always label the stream.
> - **Hermetic ≠ Lurianic.** The **Hermetic Qabalah** (Golden Dawn, tarot, Qliphoth-as-Tree-of-Death) is *not* the **Jewish-Lurianic Kabbalah** ([[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]], [[Keilim - The Breaking of the Vessels]]). Different *"Tree,"* different lineage.
> - **Engagement ≠ architecture.** That Roy *transcribed* the seven principles proves he read and collected the Kybalion, a reading-list, not a proven compositional grammar. The leap from *"he wrote it down"* to *"the songs run on it"* is interpretation, and it's labeled as such.
>
> The wider substrate the Kybalion sits inside is what makes this guide's concepts rhyme so naturally: Walter Russell (octaves, the cosmic pendulum), Alice Bailey (the *Labours of Hercules* material in Roy's hand, see [[The Labors of Hercules]]), Plato's *Timaeus* World-Soul, Jung. The Kybalion is board-grounded; the off-board Golden-Dawn correspondences stay interpretive. See [[Inherited Substrate]].

## Source notes

- **Grounded: Roy's own materials and public posts.** *"AS ABOVE SO BELOW"* on the 2021 *Down to You* end-card. *Hermetica* and *The Kybalion* on the public Instagram Book Club ([[Public Posts]]). The Hermes Trismegistus quote-card (*"The center is everywhere"*). The full seven-principle transcription on the *"I'm 33"* board, with its Correspondence, Mentalism, and Vibration cells. The 2026 *"Did Hermes paint his face?"* card. Roy naming Hermes/Mercury as the higher self in a Mirror Sessions interview: *"the spirit of Mercury, the winged messenger god… it's our higher self… pay attention to what grabbed your attention"* (he flags it himself, *"this is gonna get nerdy"*). And his public 2020 statement of the Vibration doctrine. Together these establish Hermes Trismegistus and Hermeticism as a **named, documented influence**, with a public lineage floor of 2020.
- **Released songs (grounded lyrics, interpretive mappings).** *"My head's an antenna"* and *"too happy to choose"* ([[Life Is Good]]); *"they infiltrate my brain"* ([[Online]]); *"Jericho fell like a wave"* ([[Loser]]); the reversible heaven↔earth refrain ([[Down to You]]); *"everything's upside down"* ([[Welcome to the Circus]]). The lyrics are grounded. Reading them *as* specific Hermetic principles is interpretation, built on the board as a key.
- **Framework (syncretic, esoteric, never claimed as authorial intent).** The Tree-of-Life and tarot scaffolding, Lucius-as-Hermes/Mercurius, the board's six-fold ten-stage ascent, the Gurdjieff/Russell octave reading. The system *permits* these resonances; it doesn't assert that Roy intends them.
- **How we'd be wrong.** Seven near-universal principles (duality, rhythm, cause and effect) fit almost *any* catalogue, and a lens that matches everything explains nothing. You can make everything either meaningful or derivative, and finding a place to apply a concept you already know across a career's worth of songs is not hard. There's real silliness and absurdity in here too, songs hovering outside the whole arc, not every line is a doorway. So a song-level Hermetic reading is only worth its weight if it would *fail* on some songs; where the mappings can't be told apart from chance, the frame is decorative, not operative. Roy hands us the ceiling himself, defusing his own method with a straight face: *"I would map a crow onto Mercury but that's just personal. I don't recommend you do it."* The author runs the correspondence-system **and** disclaims it in the same breath, which is exactly the honesty this page is trying to keep. If it ever sounds sure, it's doing it wrong. Note where the principles *don't* illuminate, not only where they do.
