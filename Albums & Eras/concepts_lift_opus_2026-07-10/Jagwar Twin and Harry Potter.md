# Jagwar Twin and Harry Potter

> **One-line reading.** There is exactly one documented Harry Potter reference in the whole Jagwar Twin catalogue, a 2025 song called "dumbledore." Everything past that single anchor is *convergence,* not influence: both bodies of work are reading from the same very old library of Western esoteric tradition, so the parallels come out real, dense, a little uncanny, and still neither side is borrowing from the other.

## At a glance

- **In one line:** one song ("dumbledore") is the only direct Potter reference on the record; every deeper parallel is two works inheriting the same tradition, never Potter feeding Jagwar Twin.
- **Where it shows up:** [[dumbledore]] (the song plus the "DARK ARTS VERSION") · the public Gatekeeper Saga on Discord · Lucius's "One minute your music is a twisted circus, the next it's Dumbledore" jab in [[The Interview]].
- **Connected ideas:** [[Sir Lucius]] · [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Realm]] · [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Circus]] · [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Nigredo]] · [[MAGIK]] · [[The Mask]].
- **Confidence:** one grounded anchor (the song and its release saga) braided with strong tradition-level readings and a clearly-labeled esoteric framework; the deep parallels are interpretation, flagged throughout. If any of it sounds certain, read it as a tone and not a verdict.

## What it is

Here is the whole situation in one breath. Roy is, by his own admission, a "potter head." Harry Potter sits on his public list of favorite *documentaries,* an endearing miscategorization he made in 2022, filed right alongside *The Wizard of Oz,* *Pinocchio,* *The Lord of the Rings,* and *The Fountain.* And in 2025 he put out a song named, flatly, **"dumbledore."** That is the whole documented footprint: one fandom, one favorites-list slip, one song. No Hogwarts, no Voldemort, no Horcruxes, no Rowling anywhere on the record.

One *other* Potter name does surface, and it surfaces as a punchline. While the label was sitting on the song, Roy lobbed **"Fuvk you malfoy"** (misspelling included) over the track, casting the gatekeepers as Lucius Malfoy. Hold that at exactly its weight. It is Roy jeering a suit in the saga's house-elf register, of a piece with the Dobby-sock beat below, and it is *not* a name-derivation for [[Sir Lucius]]. If anything it *strengthens* the firewall instead of cracking it: the one documented Malfoy in the entire catalogue is Roy mocking a gatekeeper, not sourcing his shadow's name. Malfoy stays the weakest, set-aside strand of the Lucius name, a shared scrap of Latin light, never an origin.

So why does this page exist at all? Because the moment you start reading Jagwar Twin against Harry Potter, the parallels pile up fast and start to feel like influence: alchemy, mirrors, shadows and doubles, hidden knowledge, word-magic, death and rebirth. The temptation is to draw a straight line from Rowling to Roy. **Don't.** Both works are running the same ancient operating system, Western esotericism, and that shared inheritance is doing all the work. This page's whole job is to keep you from mistaking a fellow traveler for a source.

The rule, said once and obeyed all the way down: **route every parallel through the shared *tradition,* never "Potter to Jagwar Twin" directly.** Sir Lucius and Lucius Malfoy share a Latin light-name. That is a resonance, a rhyme in an old dictionary. It is not a derivation. And here is the quiet trap of any big catalogue: finding a place to apply a concept you already know is never hard, and you can make almost anything either meaningful or derivative if you squint at it long enough. Keep the tradition in that last clause and the page stays honest. Lose it and you have turned a co-participant into an origin, the same error this guide avoids everywhere else.

## How it works

The one place Potter is genuinely *on the record* is the song, and the song is the only place the influence runs one direction. Everywhere else, the move is the same: name the motif, name where it lives in each work, and then, this is the load-bearing part, name the **tradition** that explains both. The explanation always lives in that last clause. That clause is the firewall.

Read the parallels this way and they stop being evidence of borrowing and become evidence of a shared library. Potter has Rowling-confirmed alchemy; Jagwar Twin has [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Nigredo]]. Both inherit the Great Work. Potter has the Mirror of Erised; Jagwar Twin has [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Source vs Reflection]] and [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Empty Mirror]]. Both inherit the speculum, the mirror-as-knowing. Potter has the disowned double that stalks its hero across the houses; Jagwar Twin has [[Sir Lucius]], the mind split from the heart, and [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Dweller on the Threshold]]. Both inherit the Jungian double. The pattern holds for word-magic, for awakening-as-remembrance, for death-and-rebirth. Six motifs, six shared traditions, zero arrows from Rowling to Roy.

The song "dumbledore" is where all of this gets *funny,* and the comedy is the insight. The name enacts its own degradation: the hook "I feel like Dumbledore / Dumble-Dumble-Dumbledore" decays in real time into "I feel like dumb, dumb, dumb… go dumb." A wisdom-name, the long-game sage, the keeper of hidden knowledge, flattened into a club chant. That is not a lapse, it is the thesis performed. The song doesn't *argue* with the wise-elder archetype, it memes it into mush, because that is precisely what the [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Algorithm|algorithm]] and the [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Circus|Circus]] do to anything profound. "Go dumb" is the wisdom-figure run through the spectacle-grinder, live, on the track: the spectacle used to dismantle the spectacle.

And underneath the joke, a real wound. The song builds genuine martyr-scaffolding (crucifixion imagery, thorns, climbing out of the grave, though that whole cluster leans on a lyric sheet streaming has never verified, so carry it loosely) and then bulldozes it with the party chant. That collision *is* the song: the sincere pain of carrying something true, slamming into an industry and an audience that just want a mindless banger. Don't resolve the oscillation. The oscillation is the point. (The deeper read of that tension is in the drawers below.)

## Where it appears

- **"dumbledore" (the song, 2025).** The one documented anchor, a release named for the Potter character, complete with a **"DARK ARTS VERSION."** Roy publicly framed it as *"a love song,"* a caption that rode the Acoustic version: the mentor/guide, *freeing* register, not a plot retelling. Credits are worth knowing: written by Roy English, Ryan Daly, and Dustin Steven Rutkowski; produced by Roy English, OmegaSign, and Ryan Daly; released via Big Loud Records (2025). The performer credit includes **Laboratorium Pieśni,** a Polish folk vocal ensemble, a genuinely strange and lovely detail for a song about a wizard. See [[dumbledore]].
- **The Gatekeeper Saga (public Discord, Nov 2024 to Feb 2025).** This is the grounded heart of the whole concept, and it is a sustained Harry Potter bit between Roy and Michael Morgan. "dumbledore" was the codename for a release Roy was fighting his label to put out; the gatekeepers demanded TikTok virality first. Roy's running commentary: *"I'm trying to release dumbledore but the gatekeepers are blocking me from getting it to the realm"*; *"they said the song needs to go viral on tiktok for them to release the song and im like wtf haha"*; and the Arthurian kicker, *"they couldnt even pull the sword out of the stone."* Then, plainly: *"idk about you but i feel like fucking dumbledore."* See [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Gatekeeper Saga]].
- **The blockchain end-run.** Roy's solution was to route around the label entirely: *"dropped it on the blockchain becuase the label has no rights there! Haha go cop it"*, posted via Michael with a Coop Records link, [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Realm]] in action. The label clearance was separately delayed by the January 2025 LA fires; in the meantime Michael told the Circle that *"the more you all comment and ask for it, the more ammo we have to prove to the label that it should be released."*
- **The resolution (the Dobby beat).** When it finally dropped, Michael announced it in pure Potter: *"Master gave dumbledore a sock, dumbledore is finally free"*, casting the label (again) as Lucius Malfoy and the freed song as Dobby the house-elf. Chef's kiss.
- **"dumbledore [DARK ARTS]" (visualizer, 2025).** An ornate priest-king robe with **no one inside it**, the hollow pole of [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Empty Mirror]] made literal, eyes-wide-shut, holding a dead-branch wand. The mask that finally replaced the face. See the drawers below.
- **[[The Interview]] (theatrical Roy v Lucius series).** [[Sir Lucius]] weaponizes the Potter pivot as a roast: *"One minute your music is a twisted circus, the next it's Dumbledore. You don't stand for anything. The darkness or the light?"*, using the turn toward the wise-elder song as evidence of Roy's incoherence. But watch the frame. The either/or is *Lucius's,* the forced choice, and Roy declines to take it: *"I guess I'm somewhere in the middle."* The demand is the trap, not the verdict; the polarity is meant to be held, not paid off.
- **The wider fan-world.** Roy self-IDs with the fandom, *"we just got to get it to the fellow potter heads and wizard nerds like us"*, and the Circle minted a bespoke **"Wizard" emoji** (March 2025) that Roy then used repeatedly, including to unveil the paid Inner Circle: *"Behold the Inner Circle 🧙."* Earlier still, in 2023, Michael captioned a tour clip *"You're a wizard Luke"* over a file literally named *Harry_Potter.mp4*, a Hagrid riff aimed at a fan, one entry in a long run of tour-memory videos. A wizard-nerd in-joke, nothing more.

> [!map]- The Same Very Old Library (the six shared traditions, mapped)
> Every parallel below is convergence routed through a tradition node. The tradition is the explanation; Potter and Jagwar Twin are two of its readers.
> - **Alchemy:** the Great Work, *nigredo → albedo → rubedo,* the red and the white, the Philosopher's Stone. Rowling has said she "learned a ridiculous amount about alchemy" to build the wizarding world; the red/white colour-coding even links Rubeus Hagrid (red) and Albus Dumbledore (white) as the two halves of Harry's ideal father-figure, and Nicolas Flamel is literal book-one canon. Jagwar Twin's side is [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Nigredo]], dissolution before rebirth. *Shared tradition, not Potter-sourced.*
> - **The double / the shadow:** Potter's disowned double and house-polarity (Gryffindor lion versus Slytherin serpent); Jagwar Twin's [[Sir Lucius]] (mind split from heart) and [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Dweller on the Threshold]]. *Shared tradition: the Jungian shadow.*
> - **The mirror / speculum:** the Mirror of Erised (desire versus truth); [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Source vs Reflection]], [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Empty Mirror]], the "magic mirror." *Shared tradition: the mirror as a way of knowing.*
> - **Anamnesis:** Potter turns on hidden knowledge and a long game; Jagwar Twin's [[The Circle]] runs on remembrance-not-acquisition. *Shared tradition: Gnostic/Platonic recollection.*
> - **Logos-magic / word-magic:** Latinate spells, true-names with power; the "magic of words," letters-as-creative-force ([[Letters of Creation|The Angels and the Angles]]). *Shared tradition: the word as creative power.*
> - **Death-rebirth:** Fawkes the phoenix and the sacrificial mentor; the Circle's recurrence and survival-through-rock-bottom. *Shared tradition: the death-rebirth mystery.*
>
> **One more, held at arm's length.** The song also runs a Christ-mythos and martyrology cluster, crucified, the crown of thorns, climbing out of the grave, the persecuted-truth-teller roll-call, and there is a genuine shared tradition behind it: the sacrificed light, the king-sacrifice the alchemy is pointing at all along. But this one does *not* get to sit in the map as a clean seventh row, because it leans almost entirely on a lyric sheet streaming has never verified. Convergence up top, this one provisional, until the words are confirmed. (See the Lyric-level caution in Source notes.)

> The deeper Potter-side architecture (the Black-family astronomy names, Sirius, Regulus, Bellatrix; the Deathly Hallows triangle-circle-line sigil; the sevenfold recurrence of seven books and seven Horcruxes) is real *in Potter* and Rowling-confirmed, but it is context about Potter, never evidence about Roy. Treat it as a neighbor's bookshelf, not a citation.

> [!reading]- What's in a Name? (Lucius: resonance, never derivation)
> [[Sir Lucius]] and Lucius Malfoy share the Latin light-field, *lux,* adjacent to Lucifer-as-light-bringer. In Potter it lends an aristocratic villain his irony; in Jagwar Twin it suits the brilliant intellect-shadow who brings a *dangerous* kind of light. But **Malfoy is explicitly the weakest strand of the entire name-field.** No derivation from Potter is documented, and the one documented Malfoy in the whole catalogue is a *jeer at the label,* not a source for anybody's name. The stronger lineage, Lucifer, *lux,* the morning-star problem down in the footnotes, and the Gospel-of-Luke-via-Steiner rabbit hole, runs entirely outside Potter and gets worked out where it actually lives: [[Sir Lucius — The Inside of the Inside]], the speculation wing that already links back here. Mention Malfoy on this page only to set him aside.

> [!reading]- Go Dumb or Go Home (the core move, and the survival read)
> The reading that holds the song together: **the name enacts its own degradation.** "Dumble-dore" sliding into "dumb… go dumb" is a wisdom-figure dumbing *itself* down in real time, the bit *is* that it disintegrates as you listen. Set that against the song's sincere scaffolding (the artist-as-martyr cluster: caught thorns, crucified, got out the grave) and you get performed cognitive dissonance: genuine pain colliding with an audience that wants a party track, and the collision left unresolved on purpose.
> The synthesis: **modern persecution is meme-ification, not the stake.** Hollywood doesn't burn its truth-tellers anymore, it flattens them into content. "Go dumb" is what witch-burning looks like now; the *delivery* is the thesis. This sits on Roy's own words elsewhere about cancel culture, that "if you're fighting something… you become that thing without knowing," the burner becoming the witch (the Inversion).
> And a gentler third reading, the one that touches the wound: "going dumb" as **dissociation and survival,** ecstatic ego-death, intoxication, numbing the mind so the body can still perform. "I'm already gone" carries all three at once, the meme-flattening, the party-nihilism, and the quiet exhaustion underneath. Be witty about the first two. Be careful with the third. (The persecution roll-call it all rests on is lyric-gated, see Source notes.)

> [!mask]- Nobody's Home (the empty robe, the wise fool, and whose voice is it?)
> **The DARK ARTS visualizer** reads as a single icon: a priest-king robe with *no body in it* ("no body, I'm already gone"), the sage turned dark-magician (the Lucius register), eyes-wide-shut as a blindfold-initiation, a dead branch where the wand should be. The mask that finally replaced the face, which ties straight to [[Welcome to the Circus]]'s "who are you without the mask?"
> **"Eyes wide shut," read straight.** The phrase sits *primarily* on the Gurdjieff frequency, man asleep, dreaming with the lights on, and Roy glossed it himself: a promo caption pins *"GO DUMB"* straight to Gurdjieff, quoting *"If you wish to get out of prison, the first thing you must do is realize that you are in prison. If you think you are free, you can't escape."* So the song's own punchline turns out to be a thesis in disguise: go dumb, never notice the cell, never leave it. Gurdjieff is an influence Roy has named publicly again and again; the Kubrick film is a real but *secondary* echo, fitting the Hollywood-occult setting, riding on top of the Gurdjieff sleep and not instead of it. So the empty robe is also a sleeping figure: a body that performs the rites with the lights on and nobody home.
> **The wise fool.** Truncating "Dumbledore" to "dumb" rhymes with the holy fool, and, in the deck the rest of the cosmology already leans on, the **Tarot's Fool (card 0):** highest wisdom appearing as idiocy to the profane world, the zero that contains everything, the fool who steps into the abyss unburdened, playing the idiot to survive the court. So the degradation cuts two ways at once: it could be *imposed* by the Circus, or *chosen* as the fool's disguise. Hold both.
> **Whose voice is it?** The unresolved authorship question: is "dumbledore" Roy's sincere wound, or [[Sir Lucius]] mocking the martyr-complex by turning a crucifixion into a club banger? The self-undercutting "go dumb" may be the tell. The Gatekeeper Saga grounds the *title* as sincerely Potter-coded, Roy genuinely "feels like dumbledore" fighting the gatekeepers, hidden-knowledge and long-game and the freeing, but the *song* holds both registers at once. Don't pick. And go one turn further, because it is worth holding: even "sincere Roy" may be a built pole, a mask like any other. Some of us think we are just as likely to find out the Roy character is a shadow too. That does not dissolve the question, it sharpens it.

> [!reading]- Narrated Entirely in House-Elf (the gatekeeper double-bind, and why the bit works)
> The saga's sharpest reading is its **double-bind:** to escape the [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Circus|Circus]], the Circle had to *run* the Circus, the comment campaigns, the streaming asks, the manufactured demand "to prove to the label it should be released." Freeing dumbledore meant playing the gatekeepers' own game well enough to beat it. That is the pathos under the joke.
> And the joke is genuinely good. The whole saga is a sustained Potter bit: Michael teeing it up two weeks early (*"U sure you're not feeling like dumbledore?"*), the realm that *"needs to be ready for dumbledore before it can drop,"* the gatekeepers who *"couldnt even pull the sword out of the stone,"* Roy and Michael bonding over their own target demographic (*"fellow potter heads and wizard nerds like us"*), and the perfect Dobby-sock landing. A real fight with a label, narrated entirely in house-elf.

## Source notes

- **Grounded (released music, public posts, public interviews).** "dumbledore" is a 2025 release named for the Potter character, with a DARK ARTS version; its credits (writers Roy English / Ryan Daly / Dustin Steven Rutkowski; Big Loud Records; Laboratorium Pieśni) are verified against streaming metadata. Roy's *"it's a love song"* framing is a public caption (riding the Acoustic version). The Gatekeeper Saga, the codename, the TikTok ultimatum, the sword-in-the-stone and "i feel like fucking dumbledore" lines, the Coop Records blockchain end-run, the LA-fires delay, the "Master gave dumbledore a sock" resolution, the "potter heads" self-ID, the custom Wizard emoji, the *"Fuvk you malfoy"* jeer over the track, and the promo caption pinning *"GO DUMB"* to Gurdjieff, is all public. Michael's 2023 *"You're a wizard Luke"* caption over *Harry_Potter.mp4* is a public tour-memory clip. Roy's 2022 favorites list naming "Harry Potter series" is public. Lucius's "twisted circus… the next it's Dumbledore" line, and Roy's *"I guess I'm somewhere in the middle"* answer, are from the released theatrical Roy v Lucius series. That Rowling used alchemy, that the red/white alchemical colour-naming links Rubeus Hagrid (red) and Albus Dumbledore (white) per Pottermore (as summarized by *Time*), and that the Black-family astronomy names and Hallows sigil are canon, all verifiable, Potter-side only.
- **Strong readings.** The six tradition-routed parallels (alchemy, the double, the mirror, anamnesis, word-magic, and death-rebirth) as *convergence;* the "name enacts its own degradation" core move; the meme-ification-as-modern-persecution synthesis; "going dumb" as survival; the empty-robe and wise-fool reads; the Lucius/Malfoy etymology as resonance-not-derivation; and the double/shadow tradition read as a **polarity held in order** (mind serving heart, the tension maintained) rather than a merger to be achieved, grounded publicly by Roy's own *"somewhere in the middle."* Built on grounded anchors, but interpretive, and labeled as such.
- **Lyric-level caution.** The song's deepest martyr and persecution material, the crucified/thorns/grave cluster *and* the whole Christ-mythos seventh "tradition," leans on a lyric sheet (AZLyrics) that streaming has *not* verified; Spotify carries no lyrics for this track. The persecution roll-call in particular (Galileo, Joan of Arc, and a "Constantine" that forks between the Emperor and the DC occultist) rests on three proper nouns that have never been confirmed against an official source. If any are mis-transcribed, the reads that depend on them soften. Treat that material, and the Christ-passion tradition it would add, as provisional.
- **Open questions.** Is "dumbledore" a single, a track on [[MAGIK]], or its own EP? The listings disagree: some log a six-track *dumbledore* EP dated 2025-02-28, while Big Loud and the lyric source treat it as a single, so don't assert any track-sequence meaning yet. And the narrowed absence: there is **no** documented Roy reference to Hogwarts, Voldemort, the Horcruxes, or Rowling anywhere. Malfoy is the exception that proves the rule, it has surfaced, but only as a *jeer at the label,* which graduates it from "absent" to "documented joke," not to a name-source for Sir Lucius. That standing absence is exactly why everything past "dumbledore" stays convergence, and the moment a real one surfaces, that item graduates from resonance to documented.
- **A fabrication-hygiene note.** Lyrics about *"Horcruxes / why have you abandoned me"* circulate online and are sometimes pinned to this song. **They are not Roy's,** they belong to a different wizard-rock track. Do not attribute them to "dumbledore."
- This page draws only on released music, public interviews and posts, and official videos. None of it is the answer. It is one way of seeing it.
