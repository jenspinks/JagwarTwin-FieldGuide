# The Jaguar

> **One-line reading.** The jaguar is the project's emblem of *seeing*: the creature that looks into other beings and, in the same motion, looks back into itself. That one trick quietly turns the whole project into a mirror of consciousness, and it asks not only *what is the self* but *what kind of seeing ever reveals it.*

> [!infobox] The Jaguar
> - **Spelling:** *Jagwar* (with a **w**) · deliberately not *Jaguar*
> - **Nature:** a field / emblem, not a cast member
> - **Role:** the seeing the whole project takes place inside
> - **First grounded:** the name itself (2018 interviews); the myth in *The Circle* (2022)
> - **Two faces:** the seer **and** the original singer
> - **Key symbols:** [[The Mirror]] · the eye · the string / [[The Thread]] · light-and-dark (Gemini)
> - **Sigils:** the jaguar-skull · the panther avatar · 🐆
> - **Related:** [[Jagwar Twin]] · [[Roy]] · [[Mary]] · [[Sir Lucius]] · [[The Circle]]

The name [[Jagwar Twin]] is built on an image, and [[Roy]], whose solo project this has been since 2018, with a billion-plus streams behind him, has explained it the same way for the better part of a decade: the jaguar is *"the creature who looks into the souls of other people and then, in turn, can look back into itself,"* because, his gloss almost every single time, *"humans are just mirrors for each other."* So the jaguar isn't a mask in the story or a fourth figure on the stage. It's the *frame* the whole project happens inside: reflexive seeing, looking outward and back at once. (For the full who-and-what, the labels, the chart history, the streaming numbers, see [[Jagwar Twin]]; this page is about the seeing the name encodes.)

That's the rare bit of artist-name lore that turns out to be the master key rather than the trivia answer. Most musicians' origin stories are a shrug: a misheard lyric, a band-name generator, a t-shirt someone liked. Roy's is a small theory of consciousness with a cat in it.

> 🚪 *The jaguar is not the only big cat in this story. Roy has shown off a hand-underlined copy of the Alice Bailey book and taught its frame on a public Live, and in its lion labor the hero can't kill the beast with weapons at all: he has to drop them and wrestle it in the dark, and what dies is the ego's authority, not its energy. That whole strange trial is waiting in [[The Labors of Hercules]].*

## At a glance

- **Role:** the project's emblem of seeing; the field, not a character.
- **Start with:** [[Jagwar Twin]] (the container the name describes), then [[The Circle]] (the myth where the creature actually appears).
- **Core conflict:** there are two ways to see, and the whole moral universe forks on which one you mean: [[Sir Lucius]] surveils, [[Mary]] witnesses.
- **Key symbols:** [[The Mirror]] · the eye · the string / [[The Thread]] · light-and-dark / [[Source vs Reflection]].
- **Confidence:** grounded fact (the etymology, told in interview after interview for the better part of a decade) braided with strong interpretation (the jaguar-as-frame, the seeing fork).

## Why this figure matters

Plenty of artists are named after an animal. Almost none have spent six years explaining, in interview after interview, *why*, and meaning the same thing every time. The jaguar isn't a logo Roy decorates the project with. It's the lens he keeps insisting you look *through.*

And what it reveals is the project's actual question. Not just *what is the self*, though the music's whole architecture is built on that, but *what kind of seeing reveals it.* The name carries both halves at once: **Jagwar**, the seer and the mirror; **Twin**, the paired self, observer and observed in one breath. A self that is both the thing looking and the thing looked at. Once you have that, the rest of the world snaps into place: the surveillance that watches everything and witnesses no one; the witnessing that recognizes and restores; the green-apple knight who wears *the God who sees me* at his throat, the exact faculty both modes are fighting over, and uses that same throat to insist *you don't know me.* All of it is the jaguar's question, asked in different keys.

## What it is: a cat with a theory of consciousness

Roy has told this etymology publicly for years, in his own voice, again and again, not a one-off he half-remembers but a standing doctrine he keeps polishing. The earliest and fullest is the **Ones to Watch** Q&A (2018): the jaguar looks into others and back into itself; *"humans are just mirrors for each other, so when you really look at somebody… you really understand yourself."* **Atwood** (2018): *"I felt like I was given new eyes and I started to see myself, others, and the world differently."* **New England Sounds** (2019) reverses the sequence and keeps the meaning. And **Mic Magazine** (~2024) adds the redemptive turn the earlier tellings lack: the jaguar *"looks into the darkness of itself and others but then is able to pull people out of that into the light."*

Then there's the tell that it has stopped being trivia. The **Highwire** profile (2021) hands the doctrine right back to him in its own words, *"a creature who looks within others as within itself. A mirror of consciousness."* That last line is the profile's prose, a journalist's paraphrase, not a Roy verbatim, which is precisely what makes it useful: when the write-ups start reciting your etymology *at* you, the origin story has quietly become the thesis.

Roy roots it specifically in **Mayan mythology**, *"I'm really big into Mayan mythology and all sorts of mythology from every different culture… so I was like, the jaguar, that's what it needs to be,"* and frames it as coming out of a real spiritual turn in his life before the debut album, not a branding exercise.

A few things keep this from flattening into one tidy idea:

- **The spelling is on purpose.** It's *Jagwar*, with a **w**, not *Jaguar*. The same misspelled creature is the namesake of the [[The Circle|founding myth]]. The typo *is* the point: this is a specific made thing, not the animal in the zoo.
- **Seeing has no fixed starting point.** Across tellings the order flips. 2018 has the jaguar look into others *first*, then back into itself; the 2019 New England Sounds telling reverses it (*"looks first into itself really deeply, but that self-realization allows it to look and see into others"*). That isn't a contradiction someone forgot to fix. The mirror-loop has no first step. The *reciprocity* is the doctrine.
- **The "Twin" half is grounded too**, and it means **duality**: *"the dual nature — the light and the dark, the yin and the yang — that everybody has."* Then, with perfect deadpan, the whole mirror-of-consciousness cosmology gets co-signed by his star sign: *"It also plays into the fact that I'm a Gemini, so I have the twin thing."* The 2024 telling tidies it into *"the light and dark or polarity of all things… the balancing of a force,"* but the Gemini is right there in the original receipts, and it's funnier and more human for it.

## Why it matters: a mirror of consciousness

Here's the lift the etymology makes when you let it. If the jaguar is the *seer* and the twin is the *seen*, then the project's recurring obsession isn't "identity" in the influencer sense. It's a much older question: a self that is both observer and observed at the same time. Two points, not one, because a single point can't know itself. It has a position but no way to see itself from anywhere. You need the second point for there to be any seeing at all, so the split isn't the fall. It's the requirement. The jaguar names the seeing; the twin names the doubling; together they make the work a **mirror of consciousness**, which is exactly the [[Source vs Reflection]] master-frame, stated as a creature instead of a concept. A reflection that returns you to the source instead of trapping you in the image.

Roy has never let the mirror idea retire. It keeps surfacing in his public posts, restated as plain aphorism: *"Relationships are fundamentally about two people reflecting to each other what they need to know to become more of who they are"* (2026); *"To Love is to meet the hidden part of yourself in someone else"* (2026); *"Compassion is a mirror"* (2026); *"A mirror can be your best friend or your worst enemy but will show you yourself"* (2025). The press has even started handing the doctrine back to him: a 2024 reviewer wrote that *"Jagwar Twin holds up a mirror to reflect humanity."*

And you don't have to take the etymology's word for it, you can count. Across 45 core songs the two most-loaded words are *"I"* (492) and *"you"* (478), neck and neck, together about a twelfth of everything sung. The reciprocal mirror isn't only in the interviews; it's welded into the grammar. Mirror made grammar. (The full tally lives on [[Pronoun Audit (I-You)]] and [[The Speaker Problem]], which are its proper home, not this page.)

## The Cat Sings, Too

It would be easy to read the seeing-jaguar as a creature of pure *vision*, and, one notch darker, of pure *surveillance.* Don't. The etymology centers seeing, but the jaguar's defining act *in the released music* isn't looking at all. It's singing.

In [[The Circle]], the closing track of **33** (2022) and the foundational myth of the whole project, the primordial Jagwar is a **maker** and a **teacher of the song**:

> *"Long ago, there was a Jagwar with a world tied on a string / And she made other Jagwars, then taught them to sing."*

The myth has a middle, and the middle is the part that earns the ending, but you have to run it in the order the song actually runs it. The loss comes first, and, tellingly, the loss isn't theft, it's *persuasion:* across *"many cycles,"* *"the new kings told Jagwars they never could sing."* Nobody steals the song. The Jagwars are simply talked out of believing they ever had it. And the maker's answer comes next, and the whole reading hangs on the word that carries it, *"So":* *"So the maker of the Jagwars hid the song inside their wings, on the inside of the inside, then left them to sing."* The hiding isn't a design flag planted before the trouble. It's a rescue, the maker's protective response to the counterfeit kings, tucking the song so deep inside the singers that it can be carried but not argued away, and then withdrawing, gone, leaving them to sing with the thing sealed in. Only after that do *"the empires built cages and sold everything."* And recovery, when it finally comes, is not a war won or a kingdom seized. It's a remembering: a wise one who *"found a small cave"* and *"remembered she once held the string."* Forgetting by being convinced, recovery by recollection. Rediscovery isn't invention, it's recognition. The whole arc of the project is in that shape.

So the emblem has two faces, not one: it *sees-into-beings* **and** it *sounds-the-world.* One capacity, two expressions. Read that way, the much-quoted manifesto line *"there's only one song"* turns, in this register, into the jaguar's song, the thing it taught, hid inside the wings, and that the wise one finally remembered. The seeing-field is also the original singer, and any reading that shrinks the jaguar down to a watching eye has quietly dropped half the creature on the floor.

## Two ways of seeing (and only one of them loves you back)

If the jaguar is the field of seeing, the project's actual drama is about *how* you see inside it. There are two modes, and they are not equal.

[[Sir Lucius]] is **surveillance:** counterfeit seeing. Extraction, prediction, ranking, the System's own way of looking, mastered by its single best student. Careful here, because this is the tempting mistake and the work refuses it: Lucius is not the machine. The machine is [[The System]], older than him, grinding away for twenty years before the face paint. Lucius is the one who *studied* it and learned to see exactly like it, and means to replace it with his own show. *"The algorithm, baby"* isn't the machine confessing to being the machine; it's the student handing back a perfect exam. What he does is reflection with the heart cut out: the distorted mirror, the encounter that looks like seeing and isn't. You can watch it in the theatrical [[The Interview|Roy v Lucius]] series, where he's inconsistent by design, cold and prophetic at the podium, almost human across the table from Roy, a bumbling showman with Leif. He reflects whoever is watching him. That's not sloppy writing, that's the diagnosis: a mirror without a heart takes the shape of the last face it saw.

And here's the joke everyone reaches for first, correctly: Lucius wears **[[El Roi]]**, *"the God who sees me,"* tattooed on his **throat**, the exact organ he uses to insist *you don't know me.* But it's more than a joke at his expense, and stopping at the joke keeps only the manipulation and loses the love. El Roi is the *contested faculty* itself, the very thing the two modes of seeing are fighting over. The eye that grew there grew to guard an unseen child, the buried [[Ballerina Boy]], the internal seer a kid builds when nobody is looking, and it ended up able only to watch, never to recognize. Read it that way and something else falls out of the placement: every word Lucius speaks passes *through* the name of the God-who-sees. The shadow speaks through the very site of being seen, which turns *you don't know me* into a challenge issued in the witness's own name. Iconography written at the literal site of utterance. In Genesis the same name lands the other way: Hagar names the God who finds her and says *"I have now seen the one who sees me."* Same faculty, opposite outcome.

[[Mary]] is **witnessing:** true seeing, El Roi *fulfilled.* And she isn't a separate power bolted on across the aisle. She is the *same* faculty, finished: seeing plus heart equals witnessing, and the same eye minus the heart is surveillance. Where Lucius ranks and predicts, she restores and dignifies; she is *seen-and-loved* against his *watched-and-ranked.* This is the whole framework of [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]], and it's why the jaguar's seeing can't be neutral. The field is morally loaded the moment somebody looks through it.

And there's a tell in the *register,* right out in the open on the feed. The same account will post a heart-aphorism and a merch-drop back to back, *"Compassion is a mirror"* one day and the *biggest merch drop ever* the next, *Jagwar Twin sells out stadiums*, Chaos Club masks for the faithful. The lazy read is that the mirror-of-consciousness keeps lapsing into hype-account. But look at how tight the alternation is: the feed isn't slipping into surveillance, it's *staging both tongues at once*, the two sides of the thesis performed in real time, post for post. Different paint jobs, same soul. Counterfeit seeing reduces the mirror to a follower count; the feed shows you the counterfeit and the real thing sharing a grid, and dares you to tell which is which.

## The lens, not a face in the frame

The jaguar is better treated as a **field or emblem than a cast member.** The character pages are [[Jagwar Twin]] (the container), [[Roy]], [[Sir Lucius]], and [[Mary]], and the jaguar is the *seeing those four take place inside,* not a fifth figure who acts in the same story. It's the lens, not a face in the frame.

This matters because the work invites a tidy over-reading and then quietly declines it. It's tempting to force a one-beast-per-figure scheme, a neat symbolic zoo with the jaguar slotted in as the last animal. The work doesn't support that geometry. The jaguar is the *condition* of the others, not their peer. It's the seeing, not one of the seen.

## The question we're not answering (on purpose)

Here is the thing the project leaves genuinely, deliberately unresolved, and it's worth keeping open rather than tidying away, because the not-knowing is itself well-built.

How does the jaguar relate to [[Mary]], the project's feminine source-figure and rightful seer? Two readings sit on the table, and they are not evenly weighted:

- **Reading A, a genderless capacity.** The jaguar is the *seeing-field* itself, distinct in kind from Mary, who sees *rightly inside* it. On this reading the two modes within the field are Lucius (surveillance) and Mary (witnessing), and the jaguar is the medium, not a person. This is the weaker reading.
- **Reading B, a deliberate collapse.** The seeing and the seer are one function with two faces. And the myth's own evidence leans this way: in [[The Circle]], the Jagwar is personified as a *feminine* maker who *"made other Jagwars, then taught them to sing,"* and the recovery comes through a feminine *"wise one"* who *"remembered she once held the string."* Both *"she."* The myth's seer is a maker-mother, which pulls hard toward identifying the seeing with the source.

For the myth register, B is the substantially grounded read and A is the thinner one. But Roy has not equated the two by name in public, so locking it would be overconfidence dressed as insight. The right move is to hold both and let the lean be a lean, not a verdict, until he says it out loud. Certainty is just a tone, not a truth. The evidence leans one way, the proof isn't in, and the page says so instead of pretending otherwise.

## The cat has a sense of humor

A mirror of consciousness sounds solemn, and the page above has been earning that solemnity. So it's worth saying plainly: the project is *funny*, on purpose, and the comedy lives right up against the cosmology instead of off in a corner. The seer-creature is also a deadpan, and three beats prove it.

**The non-sequitur.** Asked, in effect, to explain itself, the project once offered, under its own name, *"I like the sexual nature of ice cream."* That's it. No setup, no payoff, no mirror-of-consciousness. A theory of seeing that can also just say a profoundly stupid thing and let it sit there. The line matters *because* it refuses to mean anything: the same voice that built a small philosophy of the self will, without warning, decline to be a philosopher at all.

**The cutlery apocalypse.** The 2026 *Great Time to Be Human* visualizer (from *Lucius Lullaby*) stages the project's grandeur and then arms it out of the kitchen drawer, and the performer wielding the silverware is **Lucius himself**, which sharpens the whole thing: the surveillance-pole, geared up from the cutlery. He shoulders a *"PROPERTY OF B.M.R.C."* rocket launcher and waves goodbye to camera on the *"Shanghai summertime"* chorus; a **spoon** gets brandished as a scepter; a sacred **gong** is struck, with a kitchen utensil, on the line *"what a great time to be human."* It's the surveillance-vs-witnessing cosmology rendered as a sacrament you perform with the silverware. The apocalypse is real and the props are from the dish rack, and both things are true at once.

**The Goosebumps deadpan.** In conversation with **Bohnes**, the bit lands on a perfect straight-man / cosmic-man split. Bohnes: *"House of mirrors. That's probably the name of, like, a Goosebumps book."* Roy, without missing a beat: *"Not to be confused with the Hall of Mirrors — which is the Jagwar Twin metaverse."* The joke is the register clash: a children's-horror paperback and a metaverse correction delivered in the same flat tone, as if the second were the obvious clarification.

None of this dilutes the seeing. It's the *same* faculty: a creature that can look into the darkness of itself and others, and also notice that a spoon is funny. There is silliness and absurdity here, and not everything has to mean something. The humor is load-bearing anyway. A mirror that can only be solemn isn't a very honest mirror.

> [!receipts]- Which Gloss Is the Real Gloss?
> The soul-mirror telling is the grounded one, told in Roy's own voice again and again. But two *other* glosses circulate, and it's worth keeping them sorted from the core meaning so the page doesn't over-claim.
>
> - **The "sees into all living beings / travels between worlds" attribute.** This appears in bio and encyclopedic framing, e.g. the Wikipedia gloss that jaguars *"possessed mythical powers to see into all living beings."* It is **not** in any of Roy's verbatim quotes. The mirror-of-consciousness meaning is grounded; this inter-world / all-beings attribute is editorial unless and until Roy says it himself. Treat it as an open thread, not a fact.
> - **The "speed of the jaguar" gloss.** A label-bio framing reads the name via *"the speed of the jaguar… moving through technological and cultural dynamics,"* a velocity / tech-culture reading entirely absent from the soul-mirror tellings. Its existence is the useful part: it proves no single gloss is the whole story, and warns against treating any one telling as definitive.
>
> The practical rule: the *reciprocal-mirror* meaning promotes freely to grounded; the *inter-world* and *velocity* meanings stay flagged as interpretation or editorial.

> [!reading]- The Faceless About-Face
> A quieter pattern in the seeing-field, tracked across the years, points the same way the witnessing-pole does. Early on, the faceless are the *recipients* of care: in 2018 Roy dedicates songs *to* them, *"this one's for the faceless,"* and they receive. By 2025 to 26 the direction has flipped: now the faceless one is the *source* of the blessing, the Ballerina Boy outro, the *Weirdness* material, the *#1 Champion* register all have a faceless figure who *gives* recognition rather than waiting to be given it. Object-of-care becomes source-of-care. It's the same arc as the seer who is first looked-at and then learns to look: the one who is seen eventually becomes the one who sees, which is the same direction the witnessing-pole ([[Mary]]'s register) points. Held as a function and a direction, not a biography of any single traveling figure.

> [!reading]- The We in the Mirror
> Roy ties the jaguar/mirror to a **collective** register from the project's very start, not just an individual one. *"Jagwar Twin is a collective consciousness and I wanted people to feel that from the first listen"* (Atwood, 2018); *"we are all part of the same mind and body of a collective human consciousness."* The individual mirror scales up to a shared one.
>
> He pairs this publicly with a Rudolf Steiner line (2025): *"A healthy social life is found only when, in the mirror of each soul, the whole community finds its reflection…"* (the passage continues *"and when in the whole community the virtue of each one is living."*). The mirror that starts as one person looking at another becomes a mirror the whole community is reflected in. The *"humans are mirrors for each other"* doctrine isn't only intimate; at scale it's a social ideal.

> [!reading]- It's All in Your Head (Literally)
> A further extension Roy keeps gesturing at: the mirror runs all the way out to the world itself. He quotes Alan Watts, *"All that you see out in front of you is how you feel inside your head"* (2025), and posts *"People are made up in your head"* (2025). This pushes the jaguar's *"humans are mirrors"* past relationships and into a world-as-mirror-of-the-self register: what you see is partly what you are. (Grounded as Roy's quotes; the explicit jaguar-link is interpretation.)

> [!reading]- Credited Everywhere, Pictured Nowhere
> When *"Roy English"* first became publicly visible in 2015, he was credited everywhere and pictured nowhere. The Gulf Times called him a *"mystery man"*; Alesso noted *"I had actually seen Roy singing his own songs."* He was, precisely, *seen-but-unseen* at the very moment of being named, El Roi (*the God who sees me*) made biographical. The seer/seen inversion the project later mythologizes was already running in the real-world facts of the debut. (Strong reading, built on public press receipts.)

> [!props]- Spot the Cat
> Mostly the jaguar lives in the *name*, a verbal etymology you have to be told. But every so often it steps out of the word and into a picture. A **jaguar-skull** recurs as a sigil on the 2026 *Bounce* manifesto card (next to a sunburst and the line *"life is short"*), the one place the creature shows up as a dated visual-canon mark instead of a spoken gloss. The Nov 2025 *"not your homie"* merch ran a jaguar / globe / skull motif across the hoodies. Off the project entirely, Roy's personal Instagram (@royenglish) wears a **panther** avatar, a big-cat self-image threaded through his private presence, not just the band name; his X profile opens with 🐆 and *"There's two sides to every story,"* the duality thesis restated in one emoji and one line. And when he signs, the cat picks a side: the *"El Roy 🪄🐆"* captions set the jaguar glyph against Lucius's green apple 🍏, which quietly plants the emblem on the heart-pole self rather than the counterfeit one.
>
> One more, held loosely, because the drawn jaguar is a *multiple* and not a single mark: one of them is a **white, feminine jaguar carrying an ankh**, the sign of life, which refuses any tidy death-reading of the emblem outright. Where she belongs in the story is genuinely open, and this guide is not going to pretend otherwise.

> [!lore]- Ra's Cat, and the Serpent It Kills
> A second face for the emblem, and the rule comes first: it *complements* the seeing/mirror etymology, it never replaces it. Roy's own *"I'm 33"* board carries the Book of the Dead's **Spell 17**, the **Great Cat of Ra** who slays the chaos-serpent **Apophis** at the foot of the sacred tree. Set that beside the mirror-jaguar and the cat suddenly holds two jobs: it looks into souls, and it holds the line of solar order against the thing that would swallow the sun. The board image is grounded, it's public and you can go look at it; reading the JT jaguar *as* Ra's cat is the interpretation, offered as enrichment, never as a swap. ([[The I'm 33 Board]] has the fuller picture.)
>
> [~] And the serpent won't sit still either, which is very on-brand for this project. On the same board the snake seems to wear two faces: the chaos-thing the cat cuts, and, elsewhere, the ascending serpent (Nachash) climbing toward knowledge. So even the emblem's enemy may be doubled, tempter and awakener at once. Hold that one lightly.

> [!reading]- The Wrong Rider Gets Bucked
> In a 2026 post Roy treats the Jagwar as an agent in its own right: *"Jagwar will always throw off the wrong rider 🐎."* The mystical seer-creature, delivered as something close to a horse-girl flex, and the grandeur survives the emoji intact. Read straight, it's a real claim: the seer / mirror cannot be ridden by the false self. The true self stays in the saddle; the counterfeit gets bucked. It's right relation compressed into one line of stable-yard mysticism, the polarity held, the right rider up, the balancing of a force, not a merger where one self swallows the other but an ordering where the correct one holds the reins.

## Relationships

- **[[Mary]]:** the feminine source-figure and witnessing-pole (El Roi fulfilled). Her relation to the jaguar is *the* open question of the page, and the *Circle* myth's feminine maker-Jagwar is what makes it lean the way it does. Held open, but not held evenly.
- **[[Sir Lucius]]:** the surveillance-pole; counterfeit seeing inside the field. Wears *El Roi* on the throat and still can't witness. Grounded tattoo, strong reading.
- **[[Source vs Reflection]]:** the mirror master-frame the jaguar states as an image.
- **[[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]:** the doctrine of the two modes of seeing *inside* the field.
- **[[The Circle]]:** the founding myth where the jaguar is personified as a feminine maker who teaches the song. The other emblem on the [[Jagwar Twin]] page.

> [!note]- A Rabbit in Jaguar's Clothing
> A glorious footnote, flagged precisely so it stays a footnote. In 2023 Roy collaborated with **angelbaby**, a CAA-signed, Grimes-AI-voiced, septum-pierced, *leopard-patterned rapping metaverse rabbit* (Fast Company, deadpan: *"CAA is betting this rapping metaverse rabbit is the future of music"*). The avatar fuses a big-cat coat with a rabbit, Jagwar Twin's leopard/jaguar motif crossed with the clown-rabbit that elsewhere drops into the passenger seat. It's irresistible and it is **not canon**: angelbaby is Hume AI's design, not Roy's. Resonance only. The jaguar reincarnated as a Web3 bunny is a real thing that happened, and it lives in the appendix on purpose.

## Source notes: what's grounded, what's a guess

- **Grounded fact.** The etymology, *the jaguar looks into others and back into itself; "humans are mirrors for each other"; a mirror of consciousness*, is Roy's own, told across roughly five public interviews (2018 to 2024), with the caveat that the *Highwire* (2021) *"mirror of consciousness"* wording is the profile's paraphrase, not a Roy verbatim; the on-record tellings in his own voice are Ones to Watch, New England Sounds, Mic, plus Atwood's adjacent *"new eyes."* The deliberate *Jagwar* spelling, the *Twin*-as-duality / Gemini reading, and the Mayan rooting are all from those same on-record statements. The *Circle* lyric (the feminine maker-Jagwar who teaches the song; the wise one who held the string) is grounded in released music. The mirror-doctrine recurs across years in Roy's own voice, including before the *Hall of Mirrors* era, so mirror-thread readings rest on solid ground, not on the later site.
- **Strong reading.** That the jaguar is the *frame the whole project happens inside* rather than a character; that its seeing forks into surveillance and witnessing; that it is *Source vs Reflection* stated as a creature; that the field *sings* as well as sees; that El Roi is the contested faculty the two modes fight over; that the 2015 "mystery man" debut enacts the seer/seen inversion, these are interpretations built directly on the grounded material, and the page presents them as readings, not facts.
- **Framework / held open.** The Shekhinah/Sophia register on the *Circle* maker; the jaguar↔Mary identity question; the *Ra's-cat* solar reading; the *"sees into all living beings / travels between worlds"* and *"speed of the jaguar"* glosses, these stay flagged. The jaguar↔Mary question in particular is kept open *on purpose:* the myth's evidence leans toward collapse, the proof isn't in, and the page declines to lock it.
- **A note on craft.** The *Circle* lyric quoted above, the maker who *"hid the song inside their wings, on the inside of the inside,"* the wise one who *"remembered she once held the string,"* is grounded released music. But an AI synthesis once circulated that put *extra* words in the *Circle*'s mouth, narrating around those lines in a host-voice that sounds authorial and isn't. Those framing additions are not Roy.
