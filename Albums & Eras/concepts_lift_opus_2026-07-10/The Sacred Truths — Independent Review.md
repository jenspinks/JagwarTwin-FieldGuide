# The Sacred Truths — Independent Review

> [!review] Review status
> **Verdict:** Ship with calibration  
> **Scope:** Compared against the live page, the Opus lift, Roy’s repeated teachings and aphorisms, and the Heart and Mind, Duality, Circle, Realm, and Sacred/manifesto pages.

## What the rewrite improves

The rewrite gathers Roy’s recurring teachings into a coherent constellation: inner divinity, mirror law, heart guidance, polarity, remembrance, service, intuition, and the need to recognize rather than destroy shadow. This is useful because the ideas recur across interviews, posts, songs, and lore.

Its strongest methodological choice is to treat them as a constellation rather than a catechism. The project’s vitality depends on tensions among the teachings, not on forcing them into a perfectly closed doctrine.

## What is grounded

Roy repeatedly states many of the core teachings directly. Each proposition should be grounded through its own source rather than through the title “Sacred Truths” alone.

## Strong readings worth preserving

Keep the idea that the truths are recognized from within rather than imposed from outside. Keep their recurrence across different registers as evidence of project continuity.

The page may synthesize relations among the teachings, but those relations should be identified as the guide’s reading where Roy has not explicitly joined them.

## Required calibration

Do not present a closed creed, official theology, or exhaustive list. Avoid implying that contradictions have been resolved when the project leaves them productive.

Separate aphorism, character speech, lyric, interview teaching, repost, and project guidance. They do not all carry the same authority.

Avoid capitalizing or canonizing every recurring phrase into doctrine.

## Missing nuance

A “sacred truth” in this project may be provisional, paradoxical, humorous, or embodied rather than propositional. The character who speaks it may also complicate its status.

The page should preserve the difference between a teaching Roy endorses and a claim the work stages for examination.

## Cross-page implications

- **Heart and Mind:** one strongly grounded teaching among several.
- **Duality:** polarity does not settle every ethical relation.
- **The Realm:** service language remains multivalent.
- **The Speaker Problem:** character speech is not automatically doctrine.
- **Manifesto Cards:** statements form a field, not necessarily commandments.

## Exact revision instruction

Keep the constellation structure and direct quotations. Add source-type labels and an explicit statement that the page is a working index of recurring teachings, not an official creed.

Recommended thesis:

> **One-line reading.** The Sacred Truths are not a closed Jagwar Twin theology but a recurring constellation of teachings Roy returns to across forms, sometimes reinforcing one another and sometimes remaining in productive tension.

## Final assessment

The rewrite should ship after calibration. Its main safeguard is openness.
