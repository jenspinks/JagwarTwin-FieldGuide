# Inherited Substrate — Independent Review

> [!review] Review status
> **Verdict:** Ship  
> **Scope:** Compared against the live page, the Opus lift, Roy’s “modern mythology” language, and the vault’s comparative use of Tarot, Kabbalah, Theosophy, Hermeticism, alchemy, Jung, scripture, and popular fiction.

## What the rewrite improves

This page supplies the methodological distinction the entire vault needs: a symbol can belong to a shared inherited field without proving direct borrowing from one source. The “same well, different buckets” model is clear, flexible, and protects both rigor and imagination.

Roy’s own descriptions of traditions as different ways of expressing related concepts, and his modern-mythology approach, strongly support this model.

## What is grounded

Roy directly engages multiple traditions and repeatedly speaks in comparative rather than exclusivist terms. The project demonstrably draws from a broad symbolic vocabulary rather than one closed doctrine.

## Strong readings worth preserving

Keep the distinctions among direct citation, probable influence, shared substrate, structural resonance, and independent recurrence. These tiers should govern all comparative pages.

The page should also preserve negative evidence: a resemblance that fails elsewhere should reduce confidence rather than be ignored.

## Required calibration

Do not let “shared substrate” become a license to connect anything to anything. A useful resonance still needs contextual fit, repeated features, or explanatory value.

Direct influence should be stated only where source, quotation, visual borrowing, credit, or Roy’s own comment supports it.

## Missing nuance

Traditions are not interchangeable simply because they share symbols. Historical context, doctrine, function, and cultural specificity remain important.

The project may also transform inherited material enough that the new function matters more than origin.

## Cross-page implications

This page should be linked from every major framework page, especially Tarot, Kabbalah, Theosophy, Hermeticism, Gnosticism, alchemy, Jacob and Esau, and Harry Potter.

## Exact revision instruction

Adopt the rewrite substantially as written. Strengthen Roy’s modern-mythology statements near the opening and keep the evidence tiers operational.

Recommended thesis:

> **One-line reading.** Jagwar Twin works inside an inherited symbolic field, but resemblance does not always mean borrowing: the vault must distinguish direct source, shared tradition, structural echo, and imaginative resonance.

## Final assessment

This page is ready to ship and should function as a methodological charter.
