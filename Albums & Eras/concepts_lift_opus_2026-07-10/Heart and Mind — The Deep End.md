# Heart and Mind — The Deep End

> [!descend] You found the stairs
> This is the companion to [[Heart and Mind]]: the esoteric apparatus behind the doctrine, gathered in one place so the doorway page can stay light. It is dense by design, and all of it is framework: lenses from traditions the work demonstrably draws on, attributed to those traditions and never to a stated method. If any of it starts sounding like the answer, go back up a floor and listen to [[SOL]] instead.

## The Sun at the center

The seats are on the main page: Netzach and Hod flanking Tiferet on [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]]. What the Deep End adds is why the center matters. Tiferet is the Sun's station, and the Sun is the only body in the sky that makes its own light; everything else shines by reflection and takes its orbit from it. That is why the wordless [[SOL]] reads at home there.

In the old correspondence, the heart sits at the center of the human being the way the Sun sits at the center of the solar system, with the rest of the powers ordered around it. Roy's public social engagement indicates a pull toward exactly this correspondence, but the articulations of it in circulation are other people's wording, so this guide holds it as an indication of where his attention goes, nothing harder. Enlarged this way, "my mind must serve my heart" becomes a cosmology: order everything around the heart-center, then spend the whole work clearing away whatever displaced it.

One brake stays on. Roy reads as moving toward the heart-center, not as seated on it. Collapsing Roy into the Sun is the oldest inflation-trap in this kind of reading, and the honest tense here, as everywhere on the main page, is approaching.

## The break, the repair, and what stands in for it

In Lurianic Kabbalah the created order shatters, the vessels break ([[Keilim - The Breaking of the Vessels|the Breaking of the Vessels]]), and the faculties fall into isolation, each shard hoarding a little light. Read through that frame, the split gets a backstory, and "my mind must serve my heart" becomes tikkun: repair as an ethic, the scattered arrangement put back into right order rather than avenged.

There may even be an in-catalogue version of the story. Roy's own Hall of Mirrors artifact *David Star - Where The World Was Crossed* (solved code TWISTED LADDER), tells of kings and queens of time scattered as sparks under the shells, a twisted ladder, the sparks climbing back to become constellations. That this bedtime cosmogony and this doctrine are the same story is the guide's linking read, not a claim from the work.

And the tradition also names what stands in when repair does not happen. In the Theosophical frame, the Dweller on the Threshold is the self-generated shadow that accumulates at the gate of every real crossing; as a lens here, it reads [[Sir Lucius]]'s persistence. Repair delayed becomes a resident. His page holds the character; [[The Dweller on the Threshold]] holds the gate.

## Beth, crowned king over Wisdom

The *bounce* (Live Underground) title card carries a passage from the *Sefer Yetzirah*: "HE MADE THE LETTER BETH KING OVER WISDOM… AND WITH THEM HE FORMED THE MOON."

Four steps, because they carry four different weights. One: the passage is printed on the card, on the work itself; that part is simply there to see. Two: Beth as *house* is the letter's inherited meaning in the tradition. Three: house as *heart* is this guide's project-level reading (see [[The House and the Vessel]]). Four: put together, Beth crowned king over Wisdom reads as the doctrine in scripture form, the heart-letter set over the mind. Steps three and four are the guide's, and the chain is only as strong as its softest link.

So call it what it is: one of the page's richest framework correspondences, not a proof. The same verse forms the Moon, the receptive, vessel-side of the house in this cosmology, reflected light reaching back toward its source (see [[The Moon]]).

## The third voice, in full

On a public IG Live teaching Alice Bailey's Labours of Hercules, Roy sketches a three-part model of the faculties: "there's a third voice... the synthesis between the instinct and the intellect... the intuition," the god-voice. Instinct below, intellect above it, intuition outranking both. Notice that even Roy's larger geometry resolves by synthesis, not by erasure, which is why it belongs anywhere near this page.

Two guards, both load-bearing. First, it is the same three-part shape in a different register, not a second name for the cast: the instinct pole is not Roy-the-heart, and the intellect pole is not Lucius. Second, the teaching complicates the dyad rather than completing it; tie it to this one livestream, and do not let a hidden third become the quiet solution to every Roy-and-Lucius scene.

The labor Roy walks through will not even let the synthesis keep its prize. Hercules captures the doe he loves most, and the shadow of the gift is holding it too tight; the labor completes only in the release, the doe laid on the altar of Apollo, the sun-god. That the altar is the Sun, the same heart-center the first section keeps circling, is the guide's linking read, one more thread handed back to the reader rather than tied.
