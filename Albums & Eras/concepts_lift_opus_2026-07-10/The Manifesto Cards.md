# The Manifesto Cards

> **One-line reading.** The Manifesto Cards are the project's recurring title-card device: a flyer-like graphic that hides a tiny esoteric manifesto in its fine print. ## What it is

Since roughly 2019, a run of Jagwar Twin releases has carried the same graphic gag: a mock credit-card or concert title-card, blackletter type, *"presented by"* or *"brought to you by Jagwar Twin."* It looks like promotion. It is, in fact, a smuggling device. The small print almost always carries a compressed statement of belief, a manifesto set deliberately too small to read while you're thumbing past. You only find it if you stop and zoom in.

That's the whole design, and the concealment *is* the invitation. Truth needs stone buildings and hushed tones to be kept safe; nonsense gets to roam. So the card dresses its thesis as junk and lets it walk right out into the feed. It filters its own audience: the casual scroller gets an ad, the attentive viewer gets a thesis, and nobody is forced into the deep end. It is the project's entire method shrunk to flyer scale, a values statement wearing the costume of the very consumerism it's critiquing, using the spectacle to dismantle the spectacle.

And it runs the disguise as comedy. The register is vintage TV infomercial: a registered-trademark *®*, a breathless *CALL NOW*, a sponsor stamp, esoteric doctrine in the costume of late-night junk advertising. The 2020 *Happy Face* card even tells you to buy from a misspelling: *"brought to you **buy** jagwar twin."* The sponsor-frame literally instructs you to purchase. That's not a typo anybody missed.

## How it works

The cards are a fixed kit of parts, mixed and matched per release:

- a **blackletter title** plus an era-tag (e.g. *"LIVE UNDERGROUND IN LOS ANGELES"*);
- a **"presented by / brought to you by Jagwar Twin"** frame (sometimes punned to *"brought to you **buy**"*);
- a **seeker-question** pointed at the viewer (*"what is it that you seek?"*, *"are you content?"*, *"who are you without the mask?"*);
- a **sigil-triad**, a small cluster of glyphs and slogans;
- a flanking **parenthetical echo** in the typography, *(TICK)…(TOCK)* on *Happy Face*, *(bounce)…(bounce)*, *(welcome)…(welcome)*, *(wake up)…(wake up)*, the card winking at its own rhythm;
- and the payload: a **micro-type manifesto-paragraph** that only resolves when you stop and look closely.

What the device *does* across the work is more interesting than the kit. Read in sequence, the cards turn out to have been carrying the project's core doctrines for years, frequently well before the songs and characters that later made those doctrines famous. The card is where the mythology shows up early and quietly, in print nobody was reading.

- **The kingdom within.** *"you're the Kingdom, you're the King"* (*I Like to Party*, 2021) is the same move as *Welcome to the Circus*'s *"you are the maze"* (2026): the self as sovereign, the self as the structure you're navigating. It rhymes with the Chaplin line sampled in [[Superhuman (Interlude)]], *"the kingdom of God is within man."*
- **The Devil / Lucius, before the name.** *"Devil waking up to claim the kingdom"* sits on a 2021 card, roughly two years before [[Sir Lucius]] arrives by name in the 2023 dream-origin. Consistent with the project's habit: a figure accumulates in public long before it's credited.
- **As above, so below.** The Hermetic axiom (see [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Pattern]]) is a *Down to You* card sigil in 2021, the five-year-old root of *Welcome to the Circus*'s 2026 *"Did Hermes paint his face?"* The Hermetic thread isn't new to the *Lucius Lullaby* era; it was printed in the fine print all along.
- **Change the concept of self.** The *Down to You* manifesto, *"to change the world one must first change the concept of one's self,"* is the mirror doctrine of [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Source vs Reflection]] in plain language.
- **Burn it down, then rebirth.** *Happy Face*'s *"burning the old world down… out of the fire something new is being birthed. This is the death of my generation"* is the alchemical blackening (see [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Nigredo]]), later *staged* as the fire-close of the *Bad Feeling* video.

> 🚪 *The fine print knew the story years before the songs did. Line the videos up, 2019 to 2026, and the eras seem to dissolve into [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Visual Canon Throughline|one long work, slowly taking its disguise off]].*

The cards also connect the work *outward* into its alternate-reality games. The same *are-you-content* gag that opens on the *Happy Face* card also ran on a 2020 Jagwar Twin microsite and, later, surfaced as a working code inside the [[The Hall of Mirrors|Hall of Mirrors]] ARG. The gag had a back-end (see *Call Now, Operators Are Standing By*, below).

## "Are you content?"

The sharpest single line on any card is *Happy Face*'s **"are you content?"**, and it's a three-way pun doing the work of the whole project in three words.

*Content* (adjective): happy, satisfied, the literal Happy-Face question. *Content* (noun): media to be consumed, *are you THE content?*, the attention-economy product, pairing exactly with that card's *"brought to you buy."* And *content* (adjective again): complacent, contained, settled inside the system, the precise opposite of the other cards' *"what is it that you seek?"*

One infomercial throwaway holds the entire consumerism-versus-seeker argument. It points straight at [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Circus#What it produces (the Spectacle)|the Spectacle]], [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Consumption]], and [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Algorithm]]. And this is the part that's almost too neat: the pun isn't an interpreter's invention. It's grounded in Roy's own Instagram diary from around 2020, written after watching *"a documentary on A.I. merging with humans"*: *"I am holding a device currently transmitting 1's and 0's that you are reading and something labeled 'content'. Are you content?"* ([[Public Posts]]) The card is Roy's own joke, formalized.

## Where it appears

Short excerpts, in order:

- **[[Long Time Coming]] (remixes), ~2019:** the device's first appearance. Sigil: a hamsa (the eye-in-hand, eyes on the fingers). No seeker-line, no *"by"*-frame yet, the payload is the remix economy itself.
- **[[Happy Face]], 2020:** the card displayed on a CRT television showing an inverted, upside-down face, framed as a *"2020 JAGWAR TWIN HAPPY FACE® RELEASE."* Frame: *"BROUGHT TO YOU BUY JAGWAR TWIN"* (the *BUY* is on the card). Seeker-line: *"ARE YOU CONTENT? CALL NOW."* Payload: *"…the power to change the world. First we must each know and change ourselves. We are burning the old world down to the ground… out of the fire something new is being birthed. This is the death of my generation."*
- **[[I Like to Party]], 2021:** frame *"PRESENTED BY JAGWAR TWIN"*; on-card barker copy *"Attention! / LET'S SEE YOU CLAP THEM HANDS!"*; sigil-triad 🙏 praying hands · ✝ cross · the JT glyph. Payload: *"Devil waking up to claim the kingdom. People waking up to claim what's in them. Heaven is within your heart… you're the King."* The cover art plates a cake as Saturn, a ringed planet ringed *again* by skulls and cherubs, with the tagline *"A NEW DIRECTION AFFECTING THE STATE OF YOUR MIND."*
- **[[Down to You]], 2021:** frame *"PRESENTED BY JAGWAR TWIN"*; seeker-line *"WHAT IS IT THAT YOU SEEK?"*; sigil-triad *"NONE"* · the JT glyph · *"AS ABOVE SO BELOW."* Payload: *"To change the world one must first change the concept of the world. To do that one must change the concept of one's self. The concept one holds of the self makes one see the world as they do."* (It's also a Joni Mitchell title, *Court and Spark*, 1974, and its seeker-line later doubled as a working [[The Hall of Mirrors|Hall of Mirrors]] room code, below.)
- **[[Bounce]] (Live Underground), 2026:** the opening title sequence of the live track, not a separate poster. Era-tag *"LIVE UNDERGROUND IN LOS ANGELES"*; frame *"(bounce) BROUGHT TO YOU BY JAGWAR TWIN (bounce)"*; sigils: sunburst · *"life is short"* (also the song's own lyric) · a jaguar/skull doodle. Payload: the *Sefer Yetzirah* Beth passage verbatim, *"HE MADE THE LETTER BETH KING OVER WISDOM. HE BOUND A CROWN TO IT… AND WITH THEM HE FORMED THE MOON IN THE UNIVERSE, SUNDAY IN THE YEAR, AND THE RIGHT EYE IN THE SOUL, MALE AND FEMALE."* The first actual Hebrew letter on any card, a new move (see *Tear the Roof Off the House*, below).
- **[[Welcome to the Circus]] (Live Underground), 2026:** frame *"(welcome) BROUGHT TO YOU BY JAGWAR TWIN (welcome)"*; left sigil a concentric-labyrinth seal reading *"you are the maze"*; right sigil the cat/twin glyph; embedded seeker-line *"who are you without the mask?"* The payload quotes the song's chorus, then appends two **new** lines: *"who are you without the mask? Did Hermes paint his face? I don't know, maybe."* A separate end-card reads *"Produced In Cooperation With B.MR.C"* (see [[BMRC]]).
- **[[Lucius Lullaby]] (Live Underground), 2026:** the title-track card, and the **decider** for the letter-cipher question below: its epigraph-slot holds **no Hebrew letter** but a scripture-and-lullaby manifesto. Frame *"(wake up) BROUGHT TO YOU BY JAGWAR TWIN (wake up)"*; the *"nothing's what it seems"* motto; a scales-and-cup sigil (left) and the cat-skull (right). Payload: *"Now the serpent was more subtle than any beast of the field. No man can serve two masters. You gotta serve somebody. Nothing's what it seems, and it seems to be nothing. Look into this magic mirror, tell me what you see. Life is but a dream. Wake up."* This braids Genesis 3 (the serpent / the Nachash), Matthew 6:24 (two masters), Dylan's *"serve somebody"* (an allusion Roy names himself, not a writing credit), the Row-Row-Row nursery rhyme (*"life is but a dream"*), the mirror-doctrine, and the no-thing, all on the sleep-versus-**wake** axis.

> [!lore]- The Director's Cut (where the manifestos really live)
> Two of the cards turn out to be *abbreviations* of fuller texts that lived on now-dead Jagwar Twin microsites, recovered from web archives, both predating the [[The Hall of Mirrors|Hall of Mirrors]] ARG.
>
> **deathofmygeneration.fun** (the *Happy Face* "experiment," Dec 2020) carries the manifesto in a longer form than the card: *"We each have the power of the universe within us, and the power to change the world within us. But first we must each know and change ourselves. We are burning the old ways down to the ground, and that is a painful process, but out of the fire something new is being birthed. This is the death of my generation…"* It also carries the consume-satire copy, *"BUY! BUY! BUY!"* and *"WHAT TO WEAR! WHAT TO LIKE! HOW TO BE!"*, a facial-recognition *smile-gate* (*"Are you content?"*), and a phone gag, *"(313)-251-4932 / ARE YOU CONTENT? CALL NOW!"*
>
> **thechaosorder.academy** (the *Down to You* site, 2021) is the native home of that card's manifesto. The site's own meta-description is the card's seeker-line, *"What is it that you seek?"* It carries the concept-of-self manifesto in near-identical form (one clause differs, the site reads *"…change the concept of one's self which makes one see the world as they do"*) and adds two lines the card omits: *"No one else can be IT, you must be IT for your own unique journey"* and *"This is the path you chose but there are others you could have taken."* The *"you must be IT"* phrasing firms the kingdom-within doctrine to a dated 2021 anchor and ties it to the Alan Watts *"you are it"* lineage that later resurfaces as *"you are the maze."*
>
> In Roy's 2022 culture3 interview he describes the *Happy Face* interactive directly: *"I wanted to experiment with how a fan could really immerse themselves psychologically and physiologically in a song,"* the smile-gate set against satirical consume-commands (Hollywood *"telling you what to wear and what to like and how to be"*). Of the whole build he says it *"creates a very interesting effect on the mind. It really gets to the heart of the question the song asks."* He credits a free weekly guided-meditation space as the seed of the *Happy Face* project.

> [!door]- Call Now, Operators Are Standing By (the seeker-lines had back-ends)
> The phone number on the 2020 *Happy Face* site, *(313)-251-4932 / "ARE YOU CONTENT? CALL NOW"*, was independently cracked years later as a [[The Hall of Mirrors|Hall of Mirrors]] room code: the digits-only string `3132514932` unlocks the ARG's *"ARE YOU CONTENT?"* poster room. So the *are-you-content* device runs across three layers of the project, the 2020 cards, the 2020 microsite, and the 2022 web3 ARG, with a working unlock buried in a years-old phone gag.
>
> And it wasn't the only seeker-line wired to a back-end. *Down to You*'s *"what is it that you seek?"* is itself a solved Hall of Mirrors code: it unlocks artifact [[Artifacts - Gallery 4 (113-149)#142 · DOWN TO YOU|#142 DOWN TO YOU]] (minted CTHDRL.ETH), a wireframe figure lit from within at the center of a spiral galaxy. Both early-wave seeker-questions, *are you content?* and *what is it that you seek?*, turn out to have had a live door behind them the whole time. The bit was load-bearing from the start.

> [!reading]- Tear the Roof Off the House (the load-bearing 2026 card)
> The *Bounce* card is the one place the device prints an actual Hebrew letter, and three independent facts land on a single symbol. **[[Beth]] (ב) literally means "house."** The card epigraphs the *Sefer Yetzirah* passage on Beth. And *Bounce*'s own chorus is *"Let's tear the roof off of my house, my house."* The letter's meaning *is* the lyric's image, strong evidence the choice is deliberate, not decorative. Read together, the song reactivates Beth: the house turns from container into opening, closed to open, shelter to communion, a house strong enough to hold the heart and open enough to transmit.
>
> Push one step further and the card's first line states the project's foundational tenet. Through Beth = house = heart, *"HE MADE THE LETTER BETH KING OVER WISDOM"* reads as *the heart enthroned as king over the mind*, the [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Heart and Mind|heart-over-mind]] doctrine printed as scripture. The same verse continues *"…He formed the Moon,"* the body this guide's Tree map gives to Roy, the heart's side of the ledger (see [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]]). This is a strong reading rather than a documented gloss, it routes through Beth = house = heart, but the intent reads as deliberate. For the letter itself and its place in the wider alphabet-mysticism, see [[Beth]] and [[Letters of Creation]].

> [!mask]- The Patron Saint of Small Print (Hermes in the letter-slot)
> On the *Welcome to the Circus* card, the small-print slot that held *Bounce*'s Hebrew letter now holds a Hermes riddle: *"Did Hermes paint his face? I don't know, maybe."* Hermes, the god who *invented letters and writing*, occupies the exact position where the previous card printed a letter. The move recasts the face-painting ringmaster ([[Sir Lucius]]) as Hermes / Thoth: trickster, inventor of writing, and psychopomp, the guide through the underworld, which lands pointedly given the *"Live **Underground**"* setting. He's the namesake of Hermeticism *and* hermeneutics (decoding itself), and alchemy's Mercurius, the duplex trickster, a tidy fit for a figure who *"tells the truth, but the truth deceives."* The shrugging *"I don't know, maybe"* declines to confirm the card's own riddle, which is the joke and the honesty at once.

> [!lore]- Speak of the Devil (He Was Already on the Card)
> Two doctrines printed on the cards deserve their lineage spelled out. **You-are-it / kingdom-within:** *"you're the Kingdom, you're the King"* (2021) = *"you are the maze"* (2026) = *"you must be IT"* (thechaosorder.academy, 2021) = Alan Watts's *"you are it"* = Chaplin's *"the kingdom of God is within man"* (sampled in [[Superhuman (Interlude)]]) / Luke 17:21. The self-is-sovereign axiom was on a public card in 2021. **The Devil:** *"Devil waking up to claim the kingdom"* (2021) puts the Devil/Lucius figure on a public card by function-name roughly two years before [[Sir Lucius]]'s 2023 named dream-origin, consistent with the project's principle that origins *accumulate*, they don't *begin*. The card is where the figure first becomes visible.

> [!props]- Let Them Eat Saturn ("NONE," the cake, and the No-Thing)
> The *Down to You* card answers *"WHAT IS IT THAT YOU SEEK?"* with the sigil *"NONE,"* the no-thing of [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Empty Mirror]], alongside the project's later *"no thing ever creates"* line. And the *I Like to Party* cover repays a close look: a cake plated as **Saturn / Cronus the devourer**, the god who eats his children, *plus cake* = [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Consumption]], ringed by skulls = *memento mori.* A birthday platter that eats you. Esoteric doctrine smuggled inside a dessert, which is the device's whole personality.

> [!map]- The Reading List (Sources, Named and Resonant)
> The cards are a clean index of the project's stated and resonant sources. **Hermeticism / the Emerald Tablet:** *"as above, so below"* (2021), *"Did Hermes paint his face?"* (2026). **Kabbalah / Sefer Yetzirah:** the Beth passage on *Bounce* (likely via Aryeh Kaplan's translation); Beth = "house," the second letter (creation begins after Aleph's unity). **Alchemy:** Nigredo, the blackening (*Happy Face*); Mercurius the trickster-spirit (Hermes/Lucius). **Christian scripture:** Luke 17:21, the kingdom within. **Chaplin:** *The Great Dictator* speech, sampled in [[Superhuman (Interlude)]]. **Jung:** the persona = mask. **Alan Watts:** *"you are it."* **Joni Mitchell:** the *Down to You* title (*Court and Spark*, 1974), one thread of the catalogue's wider Joni lineage. **Neville Goddard:** a Roy-named influence, not a guess. Roy names Goddard out loud in his ~2021 IG diary (*"listened to a lecture by Neville Goddard… What we tell ourselves really does become the reality we inhabit"*) and shared Neville lectures on his public Discord, contemporaneous with the 2021 *Down to You* card whose manifesto sits squarely in his imagination-as-creation lineage (see [[Roy on Influences]]). **Roman myth:** Saturn / Cronus the devourer. **TV-infomercial advertising:** the *®*, *CALL NOW*, *PRESENTED BY* register, parodied throughout.
>
> Only a sliver stays open. The *Down to You* concept-of-self manifesto sits so close to **Neville Goddard**'s *"change your conception of yourself and you change your world"* that it reads like his doctrine spoken back almost word for word. Before calling the card a near-verbatim *quotation* rather than a faithful echo of the teaching, the exact *"change the concept of one's self"* wording wants a check against a Goddard primary. The influence is grounded; that one phrasing match is the only thing left to verify.

> [!reading]- It Was One Work All Along (and how the cipher question got settled)
> The strongest synthesis, guarded as synthesis and not fact, is that the Manifesto Cards are one of three recurring visual-canon devices (with the Realized Circus and the silent-Lucius end-tag) that argue the Jagwar Twin project is one continuous, pre-architected work: the 2026 *Lucius Lullaby* material is the *unveiling* of a mythology already fully present by 2020–2021, not its birth. See [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Visual Canon Throughline]]. The faint echo had been building to a crescendo for years, hints of it everywhere, sitting right there on Spotify the whole time.
>
> A real caution rides alongside it, and this is where the letter-cipher question got settled. The cards' content genuinely *varies*, and not every release carries one, which is itself the argument against any rigid hidden cipher. The early wave (2019–2021) used **no** Hebrew letters; the 2026 wave introduced exactly one (Beth on *Bounce*), while the *Welcome to the Circus* card put a Hermes riddle in the letter-slot instead of a letter, and the *Lucius Lullaby* title-track card (2026-07-10) carried no Hebrew letter at all, only a scripture-and-lullaby manifesto (the serpent, *"serve somebody,"* *"life is but a dream, wake up"*). That card was the decider: the strict letter-by-letter code across the *Live Underground* cards is **retired**, and what stands is the softer, confirmed pattern, *an esoteric epigraph per song, mixed traditions* (Hebrew Beth on *Bounce*, Greek Hermes on *Welcome to the Circus*, scripture-Dylan-nursery here). A recurring statement of values, not a locked code, and [[Beth]] stays the one real Hebrew letter the series ever printed. Certainty was only ever a tone, not a truth, and the cards decline to be a cipher.
