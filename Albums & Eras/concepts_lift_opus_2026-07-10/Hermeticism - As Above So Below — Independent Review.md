# Hermeticism - As Above So Below — Independent Review

> [!review] Review status
> **Verdict:** Ship with calibration  
> **Scope:** Compared against the live page, the Opus lift, Roy’s correspondence/polarity language, recurring scale-patterns, and the Pattern, Theosophy, Kabbalah, and Gnosticism pages.

## What the rewrite improves

The rewrite correctly recognizes that correspondence is not merely an imported theory here. Roy repeatedly uses “as above, so below,” polarity, rhythm, mind, vibration, and recurring pattern language. The page therefore has a strong foundation for treating Hermetic thought as one of the project’s active vocabularies.

Its best contribution is the scale-reading: inner and outer, person and system, song and mythology, body and cosmos repeatedly echo one another. The rewrite gives that recurrence a clear conceptual home.

## What is grounded

Roy’s explicit use of correspondence language and related principles grounds the core. Recurring mirrors between inner life and outer systems, body and cosmos, character and institution, also directly support the pattern.

## Strong readings worth preserving

The Kybalion-as-operating-system language is vivid and useful when clearly identified as framework. The page should retain polarity, rhythm, vibration, and mentalism as interpretive lenses where they fit specific material.

The idea that a pattern repeated at several scales becomes legible without proving deliberate encoding is especially strong.

## Required calibration

Distinguish classical Hermetic sources from the modern *Kybalion*, New Thought, occult synthesis, Kabbalah, Theosophy, and general esoteric correspondence. They overlap historically but are not one tradition.

Do not treat every parallel as proof of intentional Hermetic construction. Recurrence may arise from shared tradition, artistic intuition, common symbolism, or retrospective pattern recognition.

## Missing nuance

“As above, so below” is not merely sameness. Correspondence can involve analogy across difference. The lower is not a photocopy of the higher.

The page should preserve failures of correspondence, where mirrors distort or scales do not align.

## Cross-page implications

- **The Pattern:** recurrence requires evidence tiers.
- **Inherited Substrate:** shared wells do not prove direct borrowing.
- **Theosophy/Kabbalah:** related vocabularies must remain distinct.
- **Source vs Reflection:** correspondence can transmit without identity.

## Exact revision instruction

Keep the operating-system metaphor and scale mappings. Add historical distinctions and mark claims of deliberate construction as interpretation.

Recommended thesis:

> **One-line reading.** Hermetic correspondence is one of the clearest languages Roy uses for Jagwar Twin: patterns repeat across inner and outer worlds, but resemblance across scales does not make the scales identical or prove a single hidden blueprint.

## Final assessment

The rewrite should ship after calibration. The framework is well grounded; the historical taxonomy and intent claims need precision.
