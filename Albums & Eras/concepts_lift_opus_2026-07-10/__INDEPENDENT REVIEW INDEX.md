# Independent Concepts Review Index

This index reviews the Opus concept lifts without replacing or altering the proposed drafts. Every proposed concept now has its own companion independent review in this folder.

## Review standard

The review does not reject imaginative or esoteric readings merely because they are not fully demonstrable. Speculation, symbolic resonance, comparative mythology, vibe-reading, and exploratory synthesis are welcome. The governing requirement is calibration:

- **Grounded:** stated or directly shown by the work, Roy, or a reliable public source.
- **Strong reading:** supported by several internal features, but still an interpretation.
- **Resonance / framework:** a useful external lens or symbolic correspondence.
- **Speculation:** an imaginative possibility worth preserving without presenting it as the work's declaration.
- **Open question:** evidence supports more than one live reading.

The test is not “Can this be proven?” The test is “Does the prose tell the reader what kind of claim this is?” Eloquent readings should remain when they illuminate the work, but the guide must not speak for the work more confidently than the work speaks for itself.

## Status key

- **Ship:** safe to adopt substantially as written.
- **Ship with calibration:** preserve the reading, but soften or relabel claim strength.
- **Targeted revision:** one or more conceptual or sourcing corrections are required.
- **Substantial revision:** the organizing synthesis needs rebuilding, though good material may remain.
- **Hold:** unresolved evidence could materially change the page.
