# Witnessing vs Surveillance

> **One-line reading.** There are two ways of being seen. One recognizes and restores you. One watches, ranks, and shapes you. The Jagwar Twin world treats the gap between them as a spiritual fault line.

## At a glance

- **In one line:** surveillance gathers data; witnessing transforms the witnessed. Same gaze, opposite verdict.
- **Where it shows up:** [[Online]] (surveillance named outright), [[The Watchers]] (the purest surveillance song), [[Not Your Homie]] (both poles fused in one throat).
- **Connected ideas:** [[Machine vs Human]] · [[Source vs Reflection]] · [[The Algorithm]] · [[The Aquarium]] · [[Mary]] · [[Sir Lucius]].
- **Confidence:** the witnessing/surveillance split is a strong interpretive reading built on grounded anchors (*El Roi*, the songs, the *Online* ARG); the deeper Tree-of-Life and letter-mysticism scaffolding is framework-tier and labeled as such throughout.

## What it is

Both witnessing and surveillance fix a gaze on a self. The entire difference is in what the gaze *does* to the person caught in it.

**Witnessing** is relational presence. It recognizes, dignifies, integrates. You are *transformed* by being seen, the way Hagar is changed in the wilderness the moment she learns she was never invisible to God. The gaze hands something back. This is the register of *El Roi*, "the God who sees me."

**Surveillance** is extraction. It predicts, categorizes, monetizes, and quietly stabilizes your behavior so it stays legible and sellable. You are *shaped*, not known. The gaze takes something out of the room with it. This is the register of the feed, the camera, the algorithm.

The cleanest test fits on a bumper sticker: *surveillance gathers data; witnessing transforms the witnessed.* Or sharper still, **the algorithm does not know you; it knows how to shape you.** That is why surveillance reads as spiritually *wrong* in this cosmology rather than merely creepy. It isn't the opposite of being seen. It's a forgery of it, the soul's deepest need (to be seen and loved) counterfeited and sold back as being watched and ranked. It is the distorted reflection, the encounter that looks like seeing and isn't. And it works precisely because it lands so close to the real thing: the most dangerous predator is the one that can mirror your wound back to you so accurately you mistake the reflection for understanding, *being seen* turned into the trap itself. As the project's broader machine/human thread puts it: the human wants to be seen by God; the machine sells being-watched by the feed.

## How it works

This distinction is one of the load-bearing beams of the whole world, and it does most of its work by sorting the cast into two poles.

**The surveillance pole** is the machine pole, [[Sir Lucius]] and his apparatus. Here live [[The Audience]], [[The Circus#What it produces (the Spectacle)|the Spectacle]], [[The Algorithm]], and [[The Aquarium]] (the self watched in a tank that can't see the glass), plus parasociality and the feed. Lucius is the watcher who *cannot witness*. A shadow can't generate its own light, so he learned to reflect only against himself, a sealed loop that produces no new information, forever watching everyone and recognizing no one. He needs a mirror and an audience to cohere at all (no audience, no Lucius), and he sees without ever believing he can be seen.

**The witnessing pole** is the human pole, *El Roi* and [[Mary]]. Mary is the figure who sees without consuming: the faceless feminine who blesses ("born to shine") with her own face out of sight, so there's nothing to rank her by and nothing to eat. On [[No. 1 Champion (la Conquistadora)]] she's named and faceless in one breath, *"Saw a halo but I didn't see a head."* She is, precisely, the one thing Lucius's surveillance system can't absorb, because she returns no surface to flatter. (For the full mechanism, why the consuming eye simply ends when it meets a presence it can't consume, see [[Mary]].)

The crucial move is that these aren't two characters so much as two *uses of the eye*, and the catalogue keeps splitting a single gaze into both. The all-seeing field can recognize you into yourself (witnessing) or shape you into a copy (surveillance): same eye, forked function. That fork is why this concept is a **sibling of [[Machine vs Human]]** and a **species of the master-frame [[Source vs Reflection]]**: does a reflection return you to the source, or trap you in the image? Witnessing returns you. Surveillance traps you.

It has siblings down the registers, too. [[Communion vs Spectacle]] is the same split at the social scale, *a unison is not a communion.* [[Flattened Identity]] is the gaze felt as compression: being watched-and-ranked reduces a multidimensional self to a usable surface (person → profile → content → audience → product → star), different paint jobs on the same soul. And [[The Speaker Problem]] is the grammatical flip side, because being-seen mirrors who-speaks: the catalogue is, structurally, an *I↔you machine*, mirror made grammar. That is not just a figure of speech. Across roughly forty-five core songs the *I*'s (492) come out almost exactly balanced by the *you*'s (478), and the two together run about twelve percent of every word sung (see [[Pronoun Audit (I-You)|the pronoun audit]]). The mirror is measurable.

This is syncretic framework held loosely, never a claim about authorial intent. Certainty is just a tone, not a truth.

## Where it appears

**[[Online]] (2022, on *33*)** names surveillance and predictive profiling outright, and does it grinning. *"There's an army up against me and they got my face… we're stuck just like a bug inside your database"*; *"They know my every step / they infiltrate my brain / but it's okay."* The horror comes wrapped in an upbeat consumer earworm, which is the joke and the thesis at once (see below).

**[[The Watchers]] (2024)** is the catalogue's purest surveillance song. It interpolates Rockwell's 1984 *"Somebody's Watching Me"* (credited), *"(I always feel like somebody's watching me) / And I have no privacy,"* and twists the dread one notch further into clone-fear: *"I'm afraid I'm a clone,"* over a refrain that keeps gasping *"Wake up."* "I'm afraid I'm a clone" is the exact *output* of surveillance, the self shaped into a copy instead of witnessed into itself, and it's sung by the figure whose name (*El Roi*) promises the opposite gaze.

**[[Not Your Homie]] (2025, *Lucius Lullaby*)** fuses both poles in one body: *El Roi* inked on the throat, *"you don't know me"* in the mouth. The name of true seeing worn on the very organ that insists no one sees him.

**[[Welcome to the Circus]] (2026)** stages the audience as the show's product: *"take your seat, scream and shout,"* *"they love it when you lose control,"* *"did you like the show?"* Lucius later confesses authorship of the whole apparatus on camera, *"the algorithm, baby"* ([[The Interview]], EP3).

**[[Pay Attention (feat. little luna)]] (2022, on *33*)** personifies Attention itself as a speaking power that hands out desire and harvests awareness, *"eyes all around my head,"* *"behind the Empires that rise / what they televise is just my disguise."* Its call-and-response, *"What's my name? / Attention!"*, makes the act of answering *into* the act of paying. The song collects its own toll.

**The browser experience around [[Online]]** (the "Darkness_site" gate) turns the doctrine into a rite you perform on yourself. A centered poem, *"YOU SEE THE PATTERN, DON'T YOU? / THIS WAS YOUR DREAM / TURN OFF THE LIGHTS / WHO DO YOU SEE? / THE WHOLE WORLD IS WATCHING YOU BEHIND YOUR SCREEN,"* gives way to a gate that asks you to *enable your camera* (submit to being surveilled), then *cover the lens with your finger* ("turn off the lights") to pass through to the song's mint. Both modes in one gesture: be watched, then blind the watcher to proceed. The hand over the lens is the refusal that reclaims the self from the feed, because you don't dismantle this machine with fire or war, you dismantle it by withdrawing your belief. Half solemn, half prank.

A companion page, *pay-attention.jagwartwin.com*, states the premise in Roy's own deadpan first person: *"my failure at being a social media influencer has me here at 33 still trying to make it. To solve this problem I'm uploading my consciousness to the internet so a digital version of me can be a 24/7 influencer. Enter now and mint body parts…"* By Roy's own account, the whole surveillance-and-upload cosmology began as a satirical joke with the dev team, which is exactly why it lands. It's a Trojan horse, steak to hide the meds, a fairy tale to hide the philosophy.

> [!reading]- A Spoonful of Sugar (Sedates the Surveillance)
> Roy's own framing of *Online* is *"so fun and so ridiculous"* (Ones to Watch, 2022), and that is load-bearing, because the song is funny *on purpose.* The jingle, the "Feeling good!" shout, the *"Buy! Buy! Buy!"* → *"Bye! Bye! Bye!"* pun: the form wraps *"they infiltrate my brain"* in an earworm so pleasant you never notice it's about being colonized. **The song *is* the mechanism it describes.** It's the music like hiding your dog's medicine in steak, only here the steak is hiding a surveillance report. Satire as sedation.
>
> The verbal density does triple duty. *"They know what you like in bed before you come in two"* is a deliberate homophone, *come into the world / come in two / the sexual sense* fired at once, staging predictive intimacy as a literal pun: the machine knows you before you know yourself. And *"Maybe Jupiter is stupider than people say"* stacks every spiritual authority (shamans, Brahmans, mamas, Lamas) into a claim that *"we come from space,"* then deflates the whole cosmos with a punchline, the metaphysical reach always landing back inside the surveillance grid. Roy's stated image for the song's core, which also surfaces as a line in the public *Online* artifact: *"Internet is like a web and we're the Spider's food."*

> [!reading]- Somebody's Borrowing Me (The Watchers / Rockwell)
> *The Watchers* singing through Rockwell's *"Somebody's Watching Me"* is not a casual sample, it's the deepest reason the song works. Rockwell (Kennedy Gordy) wrote a paranoid prayer-song from inside his father Berry Gordy's Motown shadow, with Michael Jackson as the uncredited ghost on the hook: a buried name (Kennedy Gordy → Rockwell) mirroring Roy English → Jagwar Twin, and a contested authorship to match. Two charges sit latent in Rockwell's *story*, the watching-God of the song's prayer-origin (surveillance ↔ the divine gaze) and the nepo-son clone-fear, and it's Roy's *own* Watchers lines that reach back and set them off: *"now they want candy / but I'm not Mandy Moore"* (refusing to be the wholesome, manufactured pop-commodity) and *"dancing like Michael Jackson"* (naming the uncredited ghost who actually sang the hook). So the surveillance song doesn't just *describe* being copied. It *is* a copy, sung in a borrowed throat, by the project most haunted by the digital double.
>
> The keeper line for the whole song: **"The Watchers is the moment the Hall of Mirrors grows eyes,"** reflection escalating into surveillance. And that phrase is not a stretch but the Hebrew root unfolding (see the eye-and-mirror reading below). The theremin on the track, an instrument *played without touch, from a distance,* quietly enacts the content: control at a remove, no contact required.

> [!reading]- One Eye, Split by the Heart
> The witnessing pole's anchor is *El Roi*, Genesis 16:13, *"the God who sees me,"* the name Hagar gives God after being seen in the wilderness (the only person in scripture to name God). The mirror is already there in the scene: as she names him she says *I have now seen the one who sees me.* Roy signs himself **"El Roy"** as an authorial signature, on the *#1 Champion* visualizer caption, on the *Roy* persona's writings, in how fans and collaborators address him. The Hebrew אל ראי is inked on [[Sir Lucius]]'s throat, where to a non-Hebrew eye it reads like an abstract mark (≈ "X73X").
>
> The placement carries the whole irony: Lucius wears *the God who sees me* on the very organ that insists *you don't know me,* the machine built from surveillance carrying the name of true seeing at its throat. **The machine that watches everything cannot witness anyone.**
>
> But the irony runs deeper than a mismatch. It's an *origin story.* Lucius doesn't just happen to wear the seer's name; **the Protector grew the eye in the first place.** The child who wasn't seen, the one told *"you're not a real boy"* ([[Brandon|Ballerina Boy]]), built an internal watcher to do the seeing no one did for him, and that watcher, cut off from the heart, could only ever *watch.* When you don't have your own light, all you can do is reflect others. So *El Roi* isn't the good pole of this page and it isn't simply the bad one. It's the **contested faculty itself**, seeing, which becomes *witnessing* when the heart is in it (Mary, *El Roi* fulfilled) and *surveillance* when it isn't (Lucius). The two columns of the table above are, underneath, **one eye, split by the heart.**
>
> Which is also why Mary's witnessing pole quietly *rises.* Lay out the guide's basic character map, Roy at the heart, Lucius at the mind, and Mary is the one figure who won't sit on the line. If Lucius holds the fallen mode of the seeing-God's name, the *fulfilled* mode has to live somewhere. On [[No. 1 Champion (la Conquistadora)]] she is named and faceless in one breath, and the blessing-voice, *"you were born to shine,"* is recognition that *transforms* instead of ranks. She is where the fulfilled seeing is kept. (That is the guide reasoning from public material, Mary named-but-faceless in *#1 Champion*, the blessing that dignifies rather than ranks, not a key anyone handed us.)
>
> There's even a biographical rhyme at the project's origin. When the name "Roy English" first went public (Jan–Aug 2015), he was *credited everywhere yet pictured nowhere,* a Gulf Times piece called him a "mystery man" whose site held "nothing but the video for *Cool*, in which he does not appear," while Alesso volunteered that he'd "actually seen Roy singing his own songs." The artist literally seen-but-unseen at the moment of being named: *El Roi* made biographical, a fresh receipt at the project's starting line.
>
> Pushing the Hebrew further, the root **ר-א-י (r-'-y)** braids *re'i* (seeing / mirror) with the *eye,* so that "the Hall of Mirrors grows eyes" is the root itself unfolding (seeing **is** mirroring **is** the eye); *ari* = lion → light-bearer; *El × ro'i* = "God of my seeing," "the one in whose seeing I am reflected." That belongs to a letter-mysticism tradition, **not** to anything Roy has stated as his method. These are harmonics the system permits, not claims it asserts. If any of it ever sounds sure, that's the tell it's being read too hard.

> [!map]- The Missing Sixty-Fourth
> To upload a self, you have to anthropomorphize attention, and attention given a face *is the Eye.* The net is the new all-seeing field; the **Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra** is the old name for that field, sent out into the world. So the Eye forks into the two modes this page names: surveillance *shapes,* witnessing *transforms.* *Pay Attention*'s *"eyes all around my head"* is the surveillance face of it.
>
> The math even rhymes. The wedjat (Eye of Horus) fractions, ½, ¼, and on down to 1/64, famously sum to 63/64, always missing the final 1/64. That missing sixty-fourth is the **upload's paradox**: the part of a self that won't digitize, the residue no clone can copy. (The public "upload recipe" artifact assigns the senses those exact fractions, with *touch* as the smallest, hardest-to-digitize piece.)
>
> And the Eye has a *witnessing* face, too. The Hall of Mirrors "Queen Binah" artifact, in Roy's own hand, draws Binah as a whale with *"extra eyes,"* captioned *"GOD the Mother on Saturn / Great Sea."* The all-seeing Mother is the witnessing-pole of the Eye, set against Horus's surveilling *"eyes all around the head."* (That *Binah = Divine Mother + Saturn + Great Sea* identification is Roy's own; *Binah = the witnessing Eye* is the interpretation.)

## Source notes

- **Grounded.** *El Roi* = Genesis 16:13, "the God who sees me" (public scripture). The Hebrew אל ראי inked on Lucius's throat (visible in video stills). *El Roy* as Roy's authorial signature (public posts and captions). The lyric content of [[Online]], [[The Watchers]] (with the credited Rockwell interpolation), [[Not Your Homie]], [[Pay Attention (feat. little luna)]], and [[Welcome to the Circus]] (released music). Lucius's *"the algorithm, baby"* (the theatrical [[The Interview|Roy v Lucius]] series). The *Online* / *pay-attention* browser experiences and their verbatim text (public ARG). SARAVI's public introduction and stated purpose (culture3 interview). The LISTEN, Empty Mirror, and crossed-out ME tattoos with Roy's glosses. That *La Conquistadora* and *Queen of Heaven* are real Marian titles.
- **Strong readings.** The witnessing/surveillance distinction itself, and the functional test ("data vs transformation"); the two-cluster sorting (Lucius pole / Mary pole); the *El Roi*-on-the-throat irony; "The Watchers is the Hall of Mirrors growing eyes"; predictive profiling as counterfeit intimacy; the *Online*-as-sedation reading; *All My Friends*' city-sees-a-copy / friends-see-you; the third-eye watcher as the axis-in-one-face; that the "Mandy Moore" and "Michael Jackson" lines are Roy's own Watchers lyrics activating what sits latent in Rockwell's story. Built on grounded anchors, interpretive in the joining.
- **Framework (syncretic, never authorial intent).** The Tiferet/Yesod and Binah mappings; the *r-'-y* letter-mysticism cluster; the Eye-of-Horus / wedjat-fraction scaffolding; the Grigori, Debord, Plato, and Gurdjieff frames (the Tree itself lives on [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]], not elaborated here). The system permits these resonances; it does not assert that Roy intends them. The *Superhuman (Interlude)* proto-*El Roi* read is a deliberate back-read, and it leans on the confirmed "You, the people" portion of the sample rather than the later "kingdom of God" line.
- **How we'd be wrong.** If the plain "shaped vs known" doctrine accounts for every surveillance song as well as the esoteric apparatus does, then the apparatus is decorative, and we'd want to avoid back-dating the doctrine onto songs (beyond *Online* and *The Watchers*) that never actually carried their own surveillance anchors. Both falsifiers stay on the table. None of this is the answer; it's one way of seeing it. This page draws only on released music, public interviews and posts, and official videos.
