# Duality

> **One-line reading.** Duality is the root structure of the whole project. The arc is never the *elimination* of the split, only its correct ordering: an embrace, not a funeral.

![[roy-lucius-split.jpeg|400]]

*One face split down the middle: [[Roy]] on the left, [[Sir Lucius]] on the right.*

## At a glance

- **In one line:** every major symbol, character, and move in Jagwar Twin descends from one foundational split, heart and mind, dark and light, source and reflection. The work doesn't pick a winner. It puts them in the right order.
- **Where it shows up:** [[Welcome to the Circus]] (the Circle inverted) · [[Down to You]] (the heart/mind split, stated outright in 2022) · [[Bananas]] (the same split in kindergarten language).
- **Connected ideas:** [[Roy]] · [[Sir Lucius]] · [[The Mirror]] · [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Source vs Reflection]] · [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Inversion]] · [[Letters of Creation|The Angle and the Angel]].
- **Confidence:** grounded fact (the name itself, Roy's own statements from 2018 onward, released lyrics) braided with strong interpretation. The esoteric framework is sealed in collapsibles and labeled as such.

## What it is

Start with the name. *Jagwar Twin,* the Twin. That's not a flourish, it's the thesis printed on the marquee. The Twin is the dual nature, and Roy has said so, in plain words, in basically every press cycle he's ever done.

The clearest origin statement is from 2018, his very first interviews: *"the twin is the dual nature — the light and the dark, the yin and the yang — that everybody has. It also plays into the fact that I'm a Gemini, so I have the twin thing."* That last part is funny and load-bearing at once: the man's astrological sign is literally *the Twins,* so the project's name is, among other things, a horoscope. Eight years later he was still saying it: *"Gemini can also hold two truths at once… I can understand the lower and the higher, and the dark and the light, which is a lot of what Jagwar Twin is"* (2026).

So duality isn't a reading this guide imposed. It's the artist's own one-sentence summary of his entire project, said the same way for the better part of a decade. The trick this page pulls is to take that simple, public idea, *two sides to everything,* and follow it all the way down, because in Jagwar Twin it goes surprisingly far: into the characters, the spelling of words, the layout of his tattoos, and a creation myth that's secretly about how a self learns to see itself.

## How it works

Here's the move that turns a slogan into a structure: **the duality is never resolved by killing one side.** It's resolved by getting the hierarchy right. Roy has said the aim in plain interview words, no esoterica required: *"Jagwar Twin… was sort of me coming to this place where I wanted to explore polarity. I wanted to explore light and dark"* (2024). *Explore,* not *fix.* You don't fix a polarity any more than you fix a magnet. Hot isn't good and cold isn't bad, only the *extremes* are trouble, and both are just temperature doing its job. Harmony comes from adjusting the degree, not amputating one end.

Roy's stated resolution is four words long, *"my mind must serve my heart,"* and it's inked on his body and woven through the published LOOP-era lore (in the theatrical telling he repeats it as he steps through a gate). Notice what it is *not.* It isn't "my mind must die." The mind stays. [[Sir Lucius]], the mind split off and given a face, isn't beaten in a duel. The relationship is simply *reordered.* The split remains. What changes is which side serves which. He's said the same in interviews, just as plainly: *"The mind is a useful tool, of course, but it needs to be subservient to one's own heart and inner guidance system."*

**The "serve" hinge.** There's a single verb doing the heavy lifting across the whole arc, and it's *serve.* Both poles of the *Lucius Lullaby* story command service, they only differ in its object. Lucius's bargain runs on *"you gotta serve somebody"* (Roy's nod to Dylan, whose whole point is that you *will* serve, the only question is whom). Roy's resolution is *"my mind must serve my heart,"* and on his body, *"in service to all, in service to the one."* So the resolution isn't refusing the bargain. It's answering the bargain in its own grammar: the servant, the mind, reassigned from the external master (the metric, the money, the fear, Lucius) to the internal source (the heart). Service is constant. Only its object moves. That's the elegant, slightly devious heart of the whole thing: you don't escape servitude, you choose your sovereign.

**More light, more shadow.** Roy has also described the *mechanism* by which the two poles relate, and he's described it the same way across several years and settings, so it's a settled teaching rather than a one-off. The clean version (2023): *"we are becoming more aware of our shadows, collectively and individually, and as more light comes in, more darkness is seen."* The vivid version, to Bohnes: think of a pane of glass with specks of dust on it, turn up the light and the specks don't vanish, they get *amplified,* suddenly those little shadows look enormous. And the aphorism version, a 2026 X post ([[Public Posts]]): *"The brightest lights cast the deepest shadows."* On all three framings, shadow-work is a *consequence* of illumination, not a separate chore. You don't find the darkness in spite of the light. The light is what shows it to you.

**The root split, Roy and Lucius.** All of it descends to one master pair: [[Roy]] and [[Sir Lucius]], the heart and the mind given faces and co-credited, in 2026, as equal authors of [[Lucius Lullaby (album)|the album]]. And they *have* to be equal, because the Lucius side is no more negative than the Roy side. It's polarity, not a morality play. The light has to come through him as much as through Roy. If it came from the heart-pole alone it would be flat and invisible, a face with no shadow to prove it has depth.

| [[Roy]] | [[Sir Lucius]] |
|---|---|
| Heart | Mind |
| Vulnerable, authentic self | Intellectual, defensive persona |
| Vowel / angel | Consonant / angle |
| Faces the source | Works through the image |
| Honest reflection | The reflection that forgets |

Lucius redirected toward Roy's purposes becomes something different. The dark side isn't killed, it's re-subordinated. Roy's own formulation, plainly stated, is *"lucius is the mind. the mind is a tool, but that force can lead us astray if it is not subservient to the heart,"* and the work, crucially, is **dis-identification, not defeat**: *"these thoughts are not my own… what we fight we become."* You don't win by fighting the voice. You win by stopping mistaking it for your own. And we know, at least, that it was never about either side winning.

**The internal comes first.** One detail ties duality to the whole [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Source vs Reflection]] frame: Roy is explicit about which way the mirror points. *"I saw it first in the world around me, but then realized that that was a reflection of my internal world. So Jagwar Twin is really the exploration of, first, my internal world's light and dark, and the reflection of that in the external"* (2024). The outer dualities are the inner one, projected. That direction-of-travel is the engine under everything the project does with mirrors.

## Where it appears

The duality is everywhere, but a few anchors establish it cleanly across years and forms.

- **The name itself.** *Jagwar Twin.* The title is the thesis. Twin = the dual nature.
- **The interviews, 2018 → 2026.** It recurs, in Roy's own voice, with remarkable consistency. 2018, his very first press, is already the whole thesis in plain words (the *"light and the dark, the yin and the yang"* line up top): dark/light is *2018* vocabulary, in his earliest interviews, not a later projection. 2019 says it again: *"The twin is just the nature of everything we've been talking about. The dark and the light."* 2021 (Highwire): *"light and dark and yin and yang."* 2023/24 (Mic Magazine): *"The Twin part is the light and dark or polarity of all things… really the balancing of a force."* 2024 (Strife, on the name): *"I think it captures a bit of who I am at the core, playing in light and dark."* North Coast Music Beat: *"the twin reflects the dual nature of things… a continual ebb and flow of light and dark."* That's seven-plus distinct public statements. It is the most stable single idea in the entire corpus.
- **He uses the literal word.** To Jeff Ellis: *"everything we talk about is duality. Like, the internet is my friend, but it wants me dead, you know?"* To Bohnes: *"I feel like everything is always dual."* To Briana Cuoco: *"there can be no light without darkness and no darkness without light, and they're both so important."*
- **He enacts it as comedy, refusing to be flattened to one side.** With Briana Cuoco, every attempt to file him under one category (hiker? sober? Myers-Briggs? Enneagram?) gets the same deflection: *"I'm no thing."* When she catches herself, *"I'm so sorry, I keep doing that, I don't mean to put you —"* he finishes her sentence for her, deadpan: *"Pigeonhole me."* The man whose whole project is *two sides to everything* will not let you collapse him to one. And in the Bohnes session the duality of a *conversation* itself becomes an accidental koan, the two of them circling *"you're gonna talk about apples, I'm gonna talk about oranges"* until it spirals into *"I'm probably talking oranges because I'm an apple, so I can't even perceive that I'm an apple,"* the perfect comic image of a pole that can't see its own polarity. (Whose line is whose gets genuinely slippery in that stretch of the tape, so take it as the shape of the exchange, not a verdict pinned on either speaker.)
- **The artist bio and the feed.** Across streaming panels the bio reads *"There's two sides to every story,"* and Roy posted it himself in June 2026: *"🐆 ☉ There's two sides to every story ☉ 🛶 🥥."* The same feed gives *"The brightest lights cast the deepest shadows"* and a *"Janus and the March Hare"* aphorism (Janus, the two-faced god of the year's turning, duality as a calendar). And in mid-2026 he goes looking for a second tongue outright: *"I need to become bilingual."* He doesn't gloss the line, so this guide won't over-gloss it either, beyond the obvious, that a project built on two-sides-to-everything would want to be fluent in both at once. The Hebrew register does have one anchor Roy put on a screen himself: the live *"Bounce (Live Underground)"* video opens on a title card printing the *Sefer Yetzirah*'s Beth passage, *"HE MADE THE LETTER BETH KING OVER WISDOM… AND WITH THEM HE FORMED THE MOON…"* Beth is the second letter, the house, and read through *house = heart* the card states the whole pillar in one line: the heart set king over the mind. (One aside this guide can't quite un-notice, held loosely and as nobody's claim but our own: in Hebrew the letters of בבל, *Babel,* the loud confused tower, are the very same three consonants as לבב, *the heart,* only reordered. A tidy cross-card letter-cipher was once floated on that mirror and then let go, when the *other* manifesto cards turned up refusing to spell anything. So take the spelling-echo as a wink, not a key.)
- **The songs.** [[Down to You]] (33, 2022) puts the heart/mind split right in the lyric, *"Once you find it, can't unwind it with your head on / so listen to your heart,"* proving the duality theology surfaced explicitly in a 33-era song, years before it had characters. (It also rhymes with Joni Mitchell's own duality text, her *"Down To You,"* *"you're a kind person / you're a cold person too… you're a brute, you're an angel,"* and with the harder duality of her *"Don Juan's Reckless Daughter,"* *"the eagle and the serpent… at war in me… Man to woman / Scales to feathers,"* the same heart/mind split in plumage. Joni is already grounded on 33 via her writer credit on *Soul Is A Star,* the sample this guide identifies as *Woodstock,* so these read as candidate dialogues rather than proven citations.) [[Bananas]] (2026) strips the cosmic split to nursery language, *"I wanna be Good, but it's fun to be Bad / I wanna be nice, but I always get mad,"* the shadow before it learns to scheme. The same song stages the *other* half of the split as dissociation played for a laugh: *"I hear voices in my mind I don't remember — Did I do that?"*: the self splitting off from the part that acts, and the shrug (probably a wink at Steve Urkel's sitcom catchphrase) standing in for the reckoning that never comes. [[Welcome to the Circus]] is the Circle inverted: same shape, opposite outcome.
- **The body.** Every tattoo is inked on the right side, then mirrored onto the left, with a single *+* in the center spanning both, bilateral symmetry as a lived daily practice. The ROSE / ESOR mark sets the word *rose* against its own reversal around that central cross, a palindrome of the sacred symbol and its mirror image. (Roy's gloss on the rose: *"the rose is the heart revealed, the union of opposites, light and dark."*)
- **In character.** In [[The Interview]], from the theatrical Roy v Lucius series, Roy states the thesis, *"Our music is an exploration of light and dark, the beauty and the chaos of being a human — a mirror for those seeking to remember who they are,"* and Lucius, perfectly, fires back: *"Your truth is one-sided."* The mind accusing the heart of being half a story. He's not entirely wrong, which is the whole problem with him.

## Backwards On Purpose (the Rorrim Ehtni principle)

The most literal duality in the whole project is a name written backwards. *"In the Mirror,"* reversed into **Rorrim Ehtni,** so the mirror reads it true and the rest of us get gibberish. It isn't a tattoo, it's a byline, and it keeps coming back. It's the pen name credited on two 2008-09 tracks from Roy's first band, Eye Alaska. It reappears in mirror-writing at the foot of the *"I'm 33"* board, where the closing line only resolves once you flip it (*"Devil in the Mirror,"* reversed). And it is the one canonical reversal code in the [[The Mirror|Hall of Mirrors]]: **RORRIM EHTNI LIVED,** the string that unlocks the room called *Devil in the Mirror.* Same handful of words, walked from a band credit to a handwritten board to a solved puzzle-room, across the better part of two decades.

The principle is small and enormous at once: **what looks distorted from one angle is the true text from another.** The inverted reading is sometimes the *real* one. That's the engine behind [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Inversion]], the move that keeps a form perfectly intact and only reverses its *direction.* Lucius doesn't negate the prayer, the circle, the liturgy, he keeps every word and points it backwards (*"Fear = Prayer in Reverse,"* per Roy's own tattoo gloss). Which means inversion is detectable only from the inside, by one test: as you go deeper, do you feel more yourself, or less?

## The Question It Won't Answer

The project leaves one tension genuinely unresolved, and it's better to name it than to smooth it over: is the duality *generative* or *pathological*? Roy seems to *need* Lucius to function, the work may not exist without the split (recall that the heart-pole alone reads flat and invisible, which is the whole reason Lucius gets a microphone). So is Lucius being **redeemed** (his distorted light reclaimed and put back in service) or **retired** (the split finally healed, the construct dissolved)? The catalogue holds both open. There's a finer fork worth tracking underneath it: does any given song bet on the *un-reconciled* divided self (a "Cain moment," the mark, the wandering) or the *embraced* one (an "Esau moment," the weeping reunion)? And is there ever a wound that *stays,* a permanent limp, the mark of integration, versus one that simply heals?

And here's a second genuinely open fork, held very loosely and offered as a question, not a claim. The catalogue keeps drawing a feminine light with its face turned away: a halo with no head (*"saw a halo but I didn't see a head"*), a face kept out of sight, a *born to shine* that belongs to someone the songs won't quite let you look at. If light is a pole, and that pole keeps arriving faceless, is the Twin's polarity quietly missing a *named* light-source, a third thing the two-character frame can't hold? Maybe. The honest counterweight is sitting right there in the name: the project is called Jagwar *Twin.* Two, not three. So this stays a raised eyebrow, not a thesis, and if it ever starts to sound settled, that's the tell that it's being pushed too hard.

## Advanced reading

> [!map]- Turtles All the Way Down (the split at every scale)
> The same split recurs at every level of the project, which is what makes it the *root* structure rather than one theme among many:
>
> | Register | Pair | Notes |
> |---|---|---|
> | Biographical | Roy English / Jagwar Twin | the name change as intentional self-transformation |
> | Compositional | consonants / vowels | angles (structure) and angels (breath); see [[Letters of Creation|The Angle and the Angel]] |
> | Character | Roy / Lucius | heart and mind, co-credited as equal authors on the album |
> | Visual | mirrored tattoos | ROSE / ESOR, the Empty Mirror, the central *+*, bilateral symmetry as lived practice |
> | Structural | the Circle / the Circus | same shape, opposite orientation and outcome |
> | Symbolic | sun / moon | originating light versus reflected light |
>
> The discipline that keeps this honest: adopting Roy's own heart-vs-mind, authentic-vs-persona frame *as the analytic lens* risks circularity, describing Roy using Roy's own self-account. The split may be partly a deliberate two-character *artistic device* rather than literal interior architecture. And here's the turn of the screw worth leaving on the table: even the "clean" heart-pole may be a constructed thing. It's at least as likely that the Roy *character* is a mask too, an overcorrection rather than the unmediated authentic self, in which case *both* faces of the split face are performed. That doesn't break the duality thesis, it sharpens it. Hold the reading without over-literalizing it.

> [!lore]- In the Beginning, I Split (the "One become Two" poem)
> The "I'm 33" board, handwritten by Roy, carries a little creation poem that places the duality at the very origin and sings it in a cheeky nursery-rhyme sing-song (*"In the beginning / Back long ago / I was just me / but who was I to know?"*). The core lines: *"I spoke the words One become Two / Then I was in reverse… and I became You / Well, You and I could dance forever — A Darkness and a Light — But, when we crashed and came together, A Sun was born inside."*
>
> Read it slowly and it reframes the whole project. The self *divides in order to see itself,* and the second term is *"You."* This is why reflection exists at all. There's a plain geometry sitting under the sing-song: a single point can't know itself. It has a position but no structure, and you need at least two points before there's any geometry to speak of. So the split isn't a fall. It's a requirement, the cost to play. Two points make tension, and tension is where existence happens.
>
> It reframes Lucius, too: not an evil origin but *the risk built into reflection itself,* the reflection that forgets it was only ever the One looking back. And it hints at why the breaking had to hurt. In the Lurianic telling the first vessels shattered *because they were uniform,* all the same, unable to hold a light that wanted to be particular. The repair doesn't melt them back into sameness, it rebuilds each shard as its own specific thing. The individuation is the point. Knowledge is relational, so a single note can't hear itself, and the acorn has to forget it's a tree in order to become one. (That Lurianic layer isn't only board-poetry: Roy minted it as a Hall of Mirrors artifact, *"David Star — Where The World Was Crossed,"* unlocked by the solved code TWISTED LADDER.) The collision of opposites births a luminous center, the Sun inside, which is the alchemical *coniunctio* in playground rhyme: the union of opposites producing the Self.

> [!reading]- Two Moons and an Empty Sun (Source vs Reflection)
> The dark/light split feeds upward into the master frame, [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/Source vs Reflection]]: a mirror is faithful *while it knows it is a mirror,* and becomes Lucius the moment it forgets it is not the source. The refinement worth carrying: the reflection-pole has *two bodies.* There is the honest Moon, the reflected light that never pretends to be the sun (carried by [[Roy]] and [[Mary]]), and there is the usurping reflection, Lucius, the morning-star herald mistaken for the dawn (his clean map is Hod/Mercury). The Sun, SOL, the true light, is what both orbit and *neither one is.* This is the ethical test the mirror device always faces: faithful transmission versus self-substitution.

> [!map]- Order Out of Chaos (his making-engine, and the gendered pole)
> Roy gives the duality a making-engine, to Bohnes: *"chaos is creativity… everything is birthed out of chaos… the feminine principle of yin-yang… and then we order it. That's part of the mechanism of art — to make sense of that chaos and order it, and bring the light side of the yin-yang, bring the yang."* So in his own framing the dark/chaos pole is gendered *feminine* (the generative ground) and the ordering move *yang.* (Recorded as **his** framework, not this guide's.) This is where duality touches [[Mary]] and the hieros-gamos / union-of-opposites register.

> [!reading]- The Pronoun That Changes Hands
> The cleanest proof of "ordering, not elimination" is a single pronoun. In [[Weirdness]] the line first arrives as a Lucius-tagged projection, *"your weirdness is the sunshine,"* and by the final chorus Roy has taken it into his own mouth: *"my weirdness is my sunshine."* That *your → my* is the whole arc compressed into one possessive. Here's the honest wrinkle the page won't hide: the outro slides *back* to *"your weirdness is the sunshine."* So the pronoun doesn't land clean and stay landed, and that isn't a bug, it's the point. What the shift proves is ownership *claimed,* not the split dissolved: the tension is held, not healed. And note what it says about Lucius. If his weirdness is *sunshine,* then he is **distorted light, not the absence of light,** which is exactly why he can be re-subordinated rather than destroyed.

> [!reading]- Heart vs Mind, Feature-Length (Machine vs Human)
> The heart/mind split scales all the way up to [[Albums & Eras/concepts_lift_opus_2026-07-10/The Pattern]] and the Machine-vs-Human axis: Machine/Human is Heart/Mind written at civilizational size (the Chaplin *Great Dictator* speech surfacing in the Superhuman Interlude, *"you are not machines… you have the love of humanity in your hearts"*). Same dual structure, different magnification. The fractal holds from a single tattoo to the species.

> [!map]- The Reading List (the traditions Roy named)
> Duality is the place where Roy's stated influences cluster most densely. The most direct: **Hermeticism / the Kybalion,** whose Principle of Polarity he transcribed *verbatim* on the "I'm 33" board, *"Everything is Dual; everything has poles; everything has its pair of opposites; opposites are identical in nature, but different in degree; extremes meet… Love and Hate are two poles of the same thing."* That handwritten transcription is the closest thing to a named-tradition primary source for this whole concept. (Hermes Trismegistus is a named Roy influence, *"the center is everywhere."*) Around it: **Taoism** (yin/yang, which he pairs with light/dark constantly; the board also transcribes the *Tao Te Ching*); **astrology** (Gemini, the Twins, his own Sun); **Jungian** depth psychology (Persona/Shadow/Self, he posted the diagram, captioned *"Soul Calibrator,"* in 2020); **Walter Russell's** cosmic pendulum (contraction↔expansion, captioned *"The Law,"* posted 2020) underwriting the board's *"one must contract to expand"*; **alchemy** (the *coniunctio,* the union birthing the Sun); and **Dion Fortune,** a *named* Roy source on the shadow pole, who gives it nearly verbatim: *"The shadow is not evil. It is power unrecognized and therefore misused."* (She also wrote *The Mystical Qabalah,* which grounds the Kabbalistic furniture by name.)
>
> Fortune doesn't stand alone on the shadow/integration pole, she anchors a whole **Western ceremonial-magic and Theosophy cluster** Roy named publicly, mostly in a six-week burst around the late-2025 *Not Your Homie* / "darkside" launch: **Gurdjieff** (named three times; his *two-worlds* doctrine, Inner/Outer struggle resolving into the World of the Soul, maps straight onto correct-ordering, and his *"many I's,"* the context-dependent selves with no integrated center, is a clean gloss on Lucius); **Blavatsky** (named, Theosophy, and the source of a notably apt shadow-pole line Roy later posted, *"the mind is the slayer of the real… we must slay the slayer"*); **Israel Regardie** (named, *"if you face something that you fear and recognize it for what it is, you give it balance, you restore equilibrium,"* the doctrine of right relation in esoteric dress); and **Éliphas Lévi** (named, *"will is the key to magic; without it man is a toy of forces,"* which frames the Roy/Lucius split through magical *will* rather than psychology alone).
>
> The visual mark has a named tradition too: **Rosicrucianism / the Rose-Cross.** The ROSE / ESOR tattoo is the Rosy Cross built as a palindrome, and Roy glosses the symbol himself, *"the rose is the heart revealed, the union of opposites, light and dark,"* and posts the Rosicrucian formula plainly: *"the rose blooms in the center of the cross"* (2026). Truth hidden in the center of the self, the union of opposites at the crossing-point.
>
> On the scriptural side: **Jacob & Esau** (the divided blessing, the embrace) with **Cain & Abel** as its un-reconciled shadow; the **Lucifer / light-bearer** archetype as the mind-pole; **Isaiah 5:20** (*"call evil good, and good evil"*) as the diagnosis of inversion. And the name's *seer* side is grounded in **Mayan mythology by Roy's own admission,** from his very first interviews (2018): *"I'm really big into Mayan mythology… the jaguar is the creature who looks into the souls of other people and then, in turn, can look back into itself. Humans are just mirrors for each other."* The jaguar that goes into the dark and brings sight back out is the seer/mirror grounding the *Jagwar* of the name. That mirror-of-consciousness reading is also why Roy named the project's metaverse the **Hall of Mirrors,** correcting Bohnes's *"House of mirrors"* on the spot (Bohnes had quipped it sounded like *"the name of, like, a Goosebumps book"*).
