# The Wise One

> **One-line reading.** The Wise One walks into the old cave, finds the song everyone was told they couldn't sing, and remembers she once held the string. She is the beat where the whole Jagwar Twin cosmology stops falling and turns back toward light, and she does it by doing almost nothing.

> [!infobox] The Wise One
> - **Role:** the remembering figure who closes the creation myth
> - **Nature:** feminine; remembrance itself, made into a person
> - **First appeared:** *"The Circle,"* the closer of *33* (2022)
> - **Sole home:** [[The Circle]]
> - **Defining act:** she *"remembered she once held the string"*
> - **Key symbols:** [[The Thread|the thread]] · the cave · the song hidden inside the wings
> - **Related:** [[Mary]] · [[Jagwar Twin]] · [[Concepts/The Jaguar]] · [[Sir Lucius]]

## Start here

She is the shortest character in the catalogue. Two lines, total, and then the song is over.

But those two lines are where the whole Jagwar Twin genesis story turns around, which makes her a strange kind of protagonist: the one who shows up last, does almost nothing, and saves everything.

She doesn't fight. She doesn't conquer. She doesn't win the song back in some final battle. She walks into an old cave, and she remembers. That's the entire performance. And it's enough.

> *"Til a wise one found a small cave where the Jagwars used to sing / And the wise one remembered she once held the string."*

By the time she arrives, the myth has gone all the way down. The new kings have talked the Jagwars out of believing they can sing. The empires have *"built cages and sold everything."* The song is buried, forgotten, sealed somewhere no one thinks to look. Into all of that walks one figure with no weapon and no army, and she reverses the entire descent with a memory.

That is the thing to hold onto about her. In this world, what's broken is fixed by remembering, never by acquiring. She is the proof.

New here and want the song, not the syllabus? Go straight to [[The Circle]] and let it play to the end. The rest of this page will keep.

## Where she sits

Line her up against the rest of the myth and she is the answer-shape to every figure in it.

The new kings installed the loss. The Maker hid the song and withdrew. [[Sir Lucius]] keeps the circle's comforting name and quietly takes the remembering out. And the Wise One is the single beat where all of that gets undone, not by opposing it, but by recalling what was true before any of it started.

Pull her out and the myth has no ending. It just keeps falling: kings, cages, cycles, silence. She's the hinge the whole thing swings back on.

She is easy to mistake for [[Mary]], and the overlap is real, both sit in the catalogue's divine-feminine register. But hold the guard the work itself keeps. She is not simply Mary, and she is not a fourth named character standing beside Roy, Lucius, and Mary. Her defining act, remembrance, is her own. *What* she is, exactly, is the richest material on the page, and it's folded at the bottom for whoever wants to go there.

## Meet her here

You meet the Wise One in exactly one place, and it's worth going straight there.

[[The Circle]] is her only home: the closing track of *33* (2022), the song Roy calls *"Jagwar Twin's Genesis story"* and *"my favorite song that has ever come through me."* Note *come through me,* not *that I wrote.* Even the author files himself as a Jagwar remembering, not a king inventing. Play it to the end and you'll hear her two lines land exactly where the myth resolves.

There's a live form of her, too. When a crowd sings the song back at a show, that *is* the Jagwars singing again, the suppressed song recovered in real time by people who turn out to have known it all along. (More on that just below.)

And if you want to see what she *is* by watching her disappear, go to [[The Circle (Lucius Version)]]. Same sacred name, the myth's front door, and she is simply not in it.

## The two lines (the grounded floor)

Everything on this page is reading, except this. Before any interpretation starts, three things in those two lines are simply *true.*

**One:** she is feminine. *She.*

**Two:** her single act is remembrance. She *remembered.* She didn't acquire, conquer, or receive.

**Three:** the string she remembers holding is the *same string* the myth opened on: *"Long ago, there was a Jagwar with a world tied on a string."* Maker and Wise One hold the identical object.

The *she,* the remembrance, and the shared string are the bedrock. Build carefully on them and the rest of the page follows.

It matters, too, that she is Roy's solo invention. He is the *sole writer* of "The Circle" (Roy English and Matt Pauling produce), so the Wise One isn't a co-write or a borrowed myth-figure. She's his own.

## What she reverses: loss by persuasion

The thing that makes her job so specific is *how* the song was lost. It wasn't stolen. Nobody took it.

> *"The new kings told Jagwars they never could sing."*

The Jagwars were simply talked out of it. The loss is a belief, installed by persuasion and repeated across *"many cycles… with their kings."* And a belief can't be seized back by force, which is the whole reason the cure is a rememberer and not a warrior.

So she can't go *get* the song back, because it was never anywhere else. The Maker had *"hid the song inside their wings / On the inside of the inside, then left them to sing."* The thing everyone was hunting was sealed inside every Jagwar the entire time.

Recovery, then, isn't retrieval. It's remembering what was always there. She doesn't find a lost object in the cave. She finds her own forgotten memory of holding the string. That's the engine of the whole figure, and it's why she is *wise* rather than *strong.*

## The string is the thread

The string she remembers holding is, in the catalogue's vocabulary, [[The Thread]], and her line is where the Thread *originates.* *"A world tied on a string"* (the Maker) and *"she once held the string"* (the Wise One) are the same filament: the living connection to the source, feeling, creative agency, the song itself. Ariadne's thread, the line back out through the maze of cages and empires.

And there's an ethics folded into the verb. She remembers she once *held* the string. She recovers her own participation in the source. That is the exact opposite of *pulling* strings, the thing control does: working a figure from outside the living relation, a hand that moves the puppet without ever having made it.

So the catalogue's one-line test for any thread runs straight through her. Remembrance *holds* the string. Control *pulls* strings. Does the line lead you home to the source, or does it just make you moveable by a false one? She is the held-string answer.

## The circle grows

Her remembrance doesn't stop with her. The album's literal final words, repeated to fade, are the doctrine she sets loose:

> *"The circle will grow / From the inside of the inside… But the inside of the inside must be where you go."*

This is restoration that *spreads,* organically, from the deepest interior outward, by awakening rather than by force. And it has a live, audible form. When a crowd sings the song back at a show, that *is* the Jagwars singing again: the suppressed song recovered in real time, by people who turn out to have known it all along. It was *"inside their wings,"* inside them, the whole time. Her private cave-moment, scaled to a room. Anamnesis with a chorus.

And the doctrine is older than the song. Roy was already saying it in 2019, three years before "The Circle" existed: *"There is no beginning and no end, it's just a circle,"* back in the *Subject to Flooding* press cycle, attached to [[Move To You]]. Her remembrance-doctrine was being spoken aloud at the debut. The 2022 song doesn't invent it. It gives it a face.

## The Lucius Version: switched off

The cleanest way to see what she *is* is to watch her get deleted.

On *Lucius Lullaby* (2026), Track 1 is called **"The Circle (Lucius Version)."** Same sacred name, the genesis myth's front door, except she isn't in it. None of the myth is. The track wears the title and loops a single line from a music box: *"It's a beautiful life, it's a beautiful life, I'm dreaming."*

This is the savage joke of the whole album. [[Sir Lucius]] doesn't *argue* with the remembrance-figure. He never makes the case that the Circle is false. He just keeps the comforting name and swaps the awakening-song for a lullaby, and the figure whose entire being is *"remembered she once held the string"* gets written over by *"I'm dreaming."*

With perfect efficiency, she is put back to sleep.

He wins not by convincing anyone the Circle is wrong, but by convincing them they've already found it, then handing them a music box. Her *absence* is the whole finding: this is appropriation, not deletion. The awakening function stripped out, the warm label left on. (Why that lullaby is a nastier trick than a straight lie is in the deeper cuts.)

## Relationships

- **[[Mary]]:** the catalogue's divine-feminine figure, the same Sophia/Shekhinah register the Wise One sits in. The overlap is real and strong, and the source may even be one figure under two names. But the link is archetypal overlap only. The hard guard holds: this is *not* "the Wise One is Mary." Her signature act, remembrance, leans more Sophia/Shekhinah than conventional Marian, and the full identification waits on something the work doesn't yet state outright.
- **[[The Circle]]:** her sole home, the closing track of *33* and the foundational Jagwar myth. Everything she is happens in two lines of this song.
- **[[Concepts/The Loop and the Circle]]:** the keystone doctrine she resolves. No beginning and no end, the sought thing already within, recovery by remembrance, the circle that grows from the inside of the inside.
- **[[The Thread]]:** the *string* she remembers holding; the Thread originates in her line. Remembrance holds the string; control pulls strings.
- **The Maker Jagwar:** the feminine primordial Jaguar who ties the world on the string and teaches the Jagwars to sing. On the cosmic reading, the Wise One *was* the Maker: seeker and maker, two phases of one being.
- **[[Jagwar Twin]]:** the project named for the made-and-divided creatures of this myth. *Jagwar Twin* reads as self-identification with the Jagwars who were told they couldn't sing, which makes the Wise One *us,* remembering.
- **[[Concepts/The Jaguar]]:** the emblem of seeing *and* singing. The primordial Jaguar *"taught them to sing,"* and the Wise One recovers that field's lost voice. (Open question: whether the seeing-field and the source are distinct orders or one function in two faces. The myth tips toward the latter, but it isn't locked.)
- **The new kings:** the antagonists, the masculine system-of-control who *"told Jagwars they never could sing"* and *"built cages and sold everything."* The exact loss her remembrance reverses.
- **[[Sir Lucius]]:** the inverse pole. The Loop is return without transformation; the Circle is return with memory restored. In "The Circle (Lucius Version)," he hollows the remembrance-figure out entirely: *"I'm dreaming"* replaces *remembering,* and she simply vanishes.
- **[[Roy]]:** must hold the thread rather than cut it; the Eye-Alaska *shore = the wise* line links his self-naming to the Wise One at a distance, registers kept apart.

### The deeper cuts

Everything above stands on its own. What follows is optional: the framework held loosely, the readings that go further than the lyric strictly licenses, the source notes. Open what you want. Ignore all of it and nothing breaks.

> [!note]- The two faces (the core frame)
> The richest way to hold this figure is that she wears two faces at once, joined at a hinge.
>
> **Face one, cosmic / source.** *"She remembered she once held the string,"* the line says, but the *Maker* held the string at the myth's opening. If the Wise One once held it, then on this reading she *was* the Maker. Seeker and maker become two phases of one feminine being: she made the world, the world forgot her, and she returns at the end to remember herself. This is the divine-feminine face, the territory this guide maps to **Sophia / Shekhinah:** wisdom recovering the thread, the source-figure returning from exile. (The *"she"* is grounded. The Sophia/Shekhinah identification is interpretation, a family resemblance, not an equation.)
>
> **Face two, immanent / collective.** But the song was hidden inside *every* Jagwar's wings, inside everyone. So the Wise One is also the *template* of remembrance that any one of us can instantiate: not a specific being but *us, remembering.* This is the Christ-as-exemplar pattern, the ones who remembered the song and sang it back (a Jesus, a Bowie, a Prince), and it ties quietly to the album title, **33**, Christ's age. Roy grounds this collective face in his own public words: *"Every person has it inside of them. The trick is getting it inside out"* and *"If it's happening for me it's happening for everyone."*
>
> **Both at once,** through a single hinge: the spark inside each person, *remembered,* reunites with the source, so personal awakening and reunion with the cosmic Maker are *the same event.* You don't choose between the faces. The Wise One is precisely the place where "I woke up" and "the source returned" turn out to be one sentence. (The 33-as-Christ's-age tie is held lightly, a resonance, not a locked claim.)

> [!note]- A faculty, not a fourth character
> She reads best *not* as a separate roster figure but as a **state, a faculty: feminine-coded remembrance.**
>
> Two earlier readings only *look* like they fight. (a) She's the awakened self, the recovered self, the listener's own future state. (b) She occupies [[Mary]]'s archetypal territory, the feminine rememberer. They reconcile the moment you notice that *remembrance itself is feminine-coded* in this system: caves, mirrors, intuition, the held thread. The awakened self recovers the song *by* recovering the feminine faculty the kings suppressed. So the Wise One is *whoever re-members the feminine thread,* the listener, Roy, or the feminine principle itself, because at the instant of recovery they are the *same act.*
>
> Two hard guards. She is **not** simply Mary (archetypal overlap only; there's no naming evidence, and her defining act, remembrance, is more Sophia/Shekhinah than conventional Mary). And she is **not** a fourth named character standing beside Roy, Lucius, and Mary. That's the over-reading the project warns against elsewhere (cf. [[Concepts/The Jaguar]]).
>
> Zoom out and the whole myth is *bracketed by two feminine figures who hold the same object:* it opens on a feminine Maker with the world on a string and closes on a feminine Wise One who remembers holding it. Read against [[The Thread]] (thread = feeling, the song, the animating principle), the cosmogony resolves into one image: the feminine holds the thread, the *"new kings"* cut it, recovery is re-membering it. The kings are the masculine system-of-control: cages, commodification, "you can't sing," mind divorced from heart at *civilizational* scale, the same severance [[Sir Lucius]] performs at the personal one.
>
> So the divine feminine ([[Mary]] / Shekhinah, the Wise One, the primordial [[Concepts/The Jaguar|Jaguar]]) reads as **one function seen at three phases:** *origin* (the Maker who ties the world on the string and teaches the song), *exile* (the hidden source inside the darkened world, Shekhinah-in-exile), and *return* (the Wise One who re-members). Archetypal *continuity,* not a claim that the three are one named character.

> [!note]- The cave, the wings, and the withdrawal
> **The cave.** She walks into *"a small cave where the Jagwars used to sing,"* the site of the original, now-forgotten song. Read against Plato, it's an *inversion:* here the cave holds the *truth,* the original song, not shadows. She enters to recover an original relation, not to be deceived inside it. Eden remembered, rather than the fall into illusion. Whether the cave is best read as memory, sacred space, the unconscious, or the origin point stays open, but the direction is clear: she goes *in* to find what's *real.*
>
> **The withdrawal.** The Maker comes down, walks with the Jagwars, then *hides the song inside their wings and withdraws,* a contraction that leaves the divine concealed *within* creation, the shape Lurianic Kabbalah calls *tzimtzum.* The middle of the myth is caged fragmentation; the Wise One's remembrance is the *tikkun* beat, the gathering of the scattered light back into reintegration. The song concealed *inside* rather than acquired from outside is also the *kingdom within,* the essential thing uncovered, not obtained. Framework correspondence, not a stated source, but the founding myth's architecture sits unusually cleanly on it.

> [!note]- The one song, and the singing field
> Roy's Manifesto FAQ states a spine the Wise One runs straight through: *"The world needs music, but the world doesn't need more songs. There's only one song. I'm still learning how to write it."* Read through the Circle, that *one song* is the primordial song the Maker hid in the wings and the kings suppressed, and the catalogue is a run of attempts to re-transmit it. Her remembrance is *how* the one song is recovered: not writing a new song, re-membering the only one. (His public line *"Music is how the world remembers who it is"* is the same mechanism, in his own words.)
>
> The Jaguar belongs here too. The emblem is usually framed as *seeing,* the creature who looks into others and back into itself, but the primordial Jaguar's defining act in this myth is *teaching the song.* So the seeing-field also *sings,* and her remembrance recovers that field's lost voice. The *one song* is, in this register, the Jaguar's song.
>
> And one honest curio. The Maker descends to *"the empires of the Earth, Wind, and the Sea,"* three elements, no fire, which is essentially *Earth, Wind & Fire* (a named childhood influence of Roy's) with Fire swapped for Sea. Tempting. But it's negative evidence, reading meaning into what's *absent,* which is the cheapest kind, and the swap may be pure euphony. The archive flags it against itself: the over-read risk is the lesson, not the fire.

> [!note]- The remembrance pattern across the songs
> She anchors a documented pattern across three verified songs, each restating *recovery by remembrance* rather than acquisition. "The Circle" (*"the wise one remembered she once held the string"*), then [[Soul Is A Star]] (*"do you know who you are?"*), then [[tomorrow (ANGLE OF ETERNITY)]] (*"do you remember? / take my hand, I'll help you remember"*). Salvation recovered, not purchased: the Platonic and Gnostic *anamnesis* shape, sung three different ways.
>
> The collective face has an early seed, too. The [[Superhuman (Interlude)]] passage borrowed from Chaplin's *Great Dictator*, *"The kingdom of God is within man / Not in one man / Nor a group of men / But in all men / In you,"* is the exact theology of the Wise One's all-of-us template: the treasure is in everyone or it's nowhere. The album's *we, not me* was never only solidarity. It was already a theology of distribution.

> [!note]- Why "I'm dreaming" is worse than "I'm asleep"
> The substitution is darker than a straight inversion. *"I'm asleep"* would break the spell; you'd know something was wrong. *"I'm dreaming"* feels magical, meaningful, alive, so the swap goes unnoticed. Dreaming is sleep that mistakes itself for awakening. The awakening-song isn't contradicted. It's sung *back to sleep,* and made to feel like wisdom while it happens.
>
> The form enacts it too. A music box is a credited instrument here (Roy plays *"Music Box"*), and a music box is, mechanically, a string-instrument with the strings replaced by a fixed mechanism, a comb plucking a pinned cylinder. So the track literally renders [[The Thread]] (feeling, *held*) as clockwork (*cranked*). The thing meant to be held in a living hand becomes a sequence you wind up. *"Mind divorced from heart,"* enacted in the form itself, which is exactly Lucius's nature staged as music.
>
> And the one-line distinction she lives inside: the Loop is return without transformation; the Circle is return with memory restored. Lucius runs the same circuit she does, with the remembrance taken out. Same geometry, opposite charge.

> [!note]- Lower-confidence framework notes
> A faint, deliberately-held-apart resonance. Roy's 2010 band-name gloss (Eye Alaska) once defined *"the shore"* as *"the wise — people who have gone through things to make them wiser."* So his 2026 line *"I am the shore"* ([[Public Posts]]) faintly rhymes with becoming *the Wise One,* the one who remembered. Held loosely, registers kept apart: this is *not* "Roy is the Wise One," just a resonance between his self-description and a mythic figure in his own work.
>
> Lower still: a speculative mapping reads *"the wise one… held the string"* onto the twelfth Labour of Hercules (Pisces, the Red Cattle of Geryon), the retrieval of the dispersed self, the return with the whole herd. Recorded as a framework note only; it carries no weight in the grounded reading.

> [!note]- Source notes
> **Grounded fact.** The Wise One appears in "The Circle," the closer of *33* (2022), in two Spotify-verified lines: *"Til a wise one found a small cave where the Jagwars used to sing / And the wise one remembered she once held the string."* She is feminine (*"she"*); her single act is remembrance; the string she remembers is the same one the Maker holds at the song's opening. Roy English is the sole writer. Roy publicly calls "The Circle" *"Jagwar Twin's Genesis story"* (2024) and *"my favorite song that has ever come through me"* (2022); the Circle doctrine itself is documented as early as 2019 (*"there is no beginning and no end, it's just a circle"*). The collective-face aphorisms (*"every person has it inside of them"*; *"music is how the world remembers who it is"*) and the *"there's only one song"* line are Roy's own public words. "The Circle (Lucius Version)" carries only the looped *"I'm dreaming"* music-box refrain, and the Wise One is verifiably absent there.
>
> **Strong reading.** That the string is [[The Thread]] and originates in her line; that her remembrance is *anamnesis* (recovery, not acquisition); that she reads as Sophia/Shekhinah paired with a feminine Maker; the *two faces* frame (cosmic-source and immanent-collective, joined at the Gnostic hinge); the *held-string vs pulled-string* ethics; the crowd-singing-it-back as her act at scale; the Lucius Version as appropriation, not deletion. These are well-anchored interpretations, unusually so, since the song is Roy's solo write and names the project's own creatures, but they are interpretations, not statements the work makes outright.
>
> **Framework / esoteric.** The Wise One as a feminine *faculty* rather than a fourth character; the one-function-at-three-phases reading of the divine feminine; the inverted Plato's cave; the tzimtzum/tikkun shape; the cross-song remembrance chain and the Chaplin/Superhuman seed; the Joni *"Circle Game"* counter-figure; the Eye-Alaska *shore* resonance and the Hercules XII mapping. These are correspondences and structural readings, flagged as such, strongest where they sit directly on the grounded lyric, lightest at the edges.
>
