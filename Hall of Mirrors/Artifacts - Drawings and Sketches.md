# Drawings & Sketches

Most of the Hall of Mirrors is made of finished-looking things. This page is not.

These are the pages Roy drew by hand: cosmology diagrams, a homemade bestiary of planet-creatures, a self-portrait with a snake weeping out of one eye, and the number-pyramids of a mind trying to solve reality with a pencil. They look like they were torn out of a notebook because most of them were.

If the polished artifacts are the mythology performing, these are the mythology thinking out loud. You can watch an idea here before it becomes a song, a character, or a room: a rough shape in graphite, half-lit, arguing with itself in the margins.

## How to read them

Start with what is actually on the page. Then the title. Then the code. Then the links.

Two things repeat often enough to explain up front. Many of these sheets are pieces of Roy's larger thought-system, the [[Hall of Mirrors/The I'm 33 Board|"I'm 33" board]]: a wall-sized field where music, number, geometry, and theology all connect. Several drawings here are single cells of that board minted one at a time. And a good number of the codes are not mysteries at all: a letter-plus-number code (J403, F316, V222) is a spreadsheet cell coordinate, a column and a row on Roy's "I'm 33" spreadsheet map. The wild ones are the phrases.

## The planetary bestiary

A run of these drawings share one strange design: each is an imaginary creature, each creature is a planet, and each code is a coordinate on a single body (a hip, a shoulder, a cranium, a set of teeth). Read together they assemble one cosmic anatomy, a hand-drawn engine for [[Concepts/The Pattern]]. Mercury is a fox, Venus a dove, Mars a horse, Jupiter a dolphin, Saturn a whale, and none of that is this guide's guesswork: the planets are named in Roy's own captions (*"Jupiter the Blue Dolphin,"* *"Cinnamon the Horse (who lives on Mars),"* *"Venus the Dove,"* *"GOD the Mother on Saturn"*), and the fox holds the ☿ Mercury glyph in its paws. The codes all run on one grammar, spelled out on [[Hall of Mirrors/The I'm 33 Board|the board]]: the animals count down from the source (swan 1, owl 2, whale 3, on to rabbit 10), the body parts count up from the ground (feet 1 to crown 10), so each *"[part] of a [animal]"* code names a single station from both ends, and the two numbers always add to eleven. Below, the menagerie.

<a href="https://storage.googleapis.com/jagwar-twin-33.appspot.com/artifacts/0xd722c486ba8a39b19cd81b4f5b5cac3b977934026d04ec6515e0f8d5ea6af4d9..png"><img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/jagwar-twin-33.appspot.com/artifacts/0xd722c486ba8a39b19cd81b4f5b5cac3b977934026d04ec6515e0f8d5ea6af4d9..png" width="300"></a>

Setting them apart marks the linkage, not the quantities. Held beside a note on Roy's own board, *"there must be a Link,"* the figure reads as the tradition's answer to it: in the geometry he is copying, a link is a mean, the term that binds two extremes into one.

### #11 · Roman Calculus · `9360`
<a href="https://storage.googleapis.com/jagwar-twin-33.appspot.com/artifacts/0xe0e13d94ee1e14ef20ee89e165997c7e4ce15965c767c5b8079ac13ace454df4..png"><img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/jagwar-twin-33.appspot.com/artifacts/0xe0e13d94ee1e14ef20ee89e165997c7e4ce15965c767c5b8079ac13ace454df4..png" width="260"></a>

A Roman abacus: vertical columns crossed by two bars, beads riding in upper and lower registers. Arithmetic as a physical, positional act, a number you move with your hands. And the beads are not set at random: counted by abacus convention (an upper bead is five, each lower bead one), the four columns spell **9 · 3 · 6 · 0**, the artifact displaying its own unlock code on its face, with the final zero drawn the only way an abacus can draw it, as an empty column. The number itself is built, not picked: 9360 = 26 × 360, and both factors are authored elsewhere in the work. 26 is the alphabet, and the YHWH-gematria Roy works out on the KHAWAH page (room 30, whose code is 26); 360 is the full circle; and the ARG's earlier desktop keeps a Trash file literally named *9360*, illustrated with a bust of Julius Caesar, that opens a Caesar-shift cipher tool. Roman abacus, Roman Caesar: 9360 divides cleanly by both 26 and 360, so in either system the complete operation returns to its starting position, the whole alphabet turned through the whole circle and back where it began. That return-to-origin geometry reads as the maze's quiet restatement of the question held at [[Concepts/The Loop and the Circle]]: a perfect return, with everything depending on whether anything is remembered on the way around.

### #88 · Complete Upload Recipe · `INTERNAL GUIDE`
<a href="https://storage.googleapis.com/jagwar-twin-33.appspot.com/artifacts/0xeb355aac61d7bdff66349fed14458aff800433f0ff39aaf203c6c4a9a9558bb0..png"><img src="https://storage.googleapis.com/jagwar-twin-33.appspot.com/artifacts/0xeb355aac61d7bdff66349fed14458aff800433f0ff39aaf203c6c4a9a9558bb0..png" width="300"></a>

A typewritten *"Upload Recipe,"* the ingredient list for a self: sensory faculties, brain cells, electromagnetism, then the senses weighted as those same Eye-of-Horus fractions (½ smell down to 1/64 touch, summing to the incomplete 63/64), then anabolic and catabolic systems, plus and minus ego, a *"Ra-link,"* ways of growing, and finally Sacrifice, Love, Will, Fear, Compassion. The code *"INTERNAL GUIDE"* frames the whole thing as the operating manual for a digital double. Touch, the smallest 1/64, is the part that never fully digitizes, which makes this quietly the clone paradox: a complete being rendered in parts, always short by exactly one piece. (That *"Ra-link"* is the [[SARAVI]] component.)
And held against the rest of the gallery, the recipe stops being a curio and becomes the maze's own index. Its *"Surface perceptions"* line is the exact code that unlocks #89, the first of the two warnings the wiring diagram shows fencing the Undefined Map. *"Ra-link"* is #85's title, the first leg of the SARAVI loop. *"Ways of growing"* names a whole realm of the maze, the shelf where the Eye-of-Horus fractions (#74) and SENSE HIERARCHY (#95) themselves file. The stray cell-code *"C884"* is a working key too: the *Creation w_Aubs* room's on-chain label reads *"Unlocked with C884."* And the ingredients combine the way a recipe's should: *Fear* plus *Love* cook down to *"Fearlove,"* the code of #60 and the name of Roy's real 2011 solo EP. So *"INTERNAL GUIDE"* earns a second, literal sense: the sheet that tells you what a self is made of is, line for line, a guide to the interior of the maze.
