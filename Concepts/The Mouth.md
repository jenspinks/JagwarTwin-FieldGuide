# The Mouth

> [!info] Merged
> The mouth is one organ doing two opposite jobs, and its two halves now live where they belong. The mouth that **testifies**, the voice getting out, is folded into **[[The Voice]]** (see its *two mouths* reading). The mouth that **consumes**, the voice getting eaten, is **[[Consumption]]**. The dense apparatus (the throat in the body-map, the letter-mysticism, the King James mouth-texts, the Munch and Cassandra readings) remains at [[The Mouth — The Deep End]].
