# The Flip and the Distorting Mirror

> [!gist] Then "I" was in reverse, and "I" became "You"
> A Darkness and a Light.
>
> A clean mirror is the simplest thing in the world: you, looking at you. This project refuses to leave it that simple. Here the mirror is a verb with two moving parts. First it **flips**: you look in, the reflection inverts, separates, and walks off as a second person. One self becomes two. Then it **distorts**: because this isn't one tidy pane, it's a hall of them, a carnival funhouse, and the self comes back as masks, projections, and spectacle, a version of you that's almost right and exactly wrong. The work of the whole catalogue is walking that hall and finding the one true face again.
>
> The shortest proof is on the marquee. The project is named **Jagwar Twin**, and the Twin is the thing the mirror keeps making: different paint jobs on the same soul. The flip and the distortion are each grounded in Roy's released music, his posts, and his body; that they fuse into one machine is this guide's strong read, not a stated fact. And the hoped-for reversal, flipping back, getting back together, is sung in the future tense. Approached, never yet arrived.

**Where it enters**

* The name itself: **Jagwar Twin**, with the twin later billed as a second artist outright ("Jagwar Twin, sir lucius" on the [[Lucius Lullaby (album)|Lucius Lullaby]] credits). The double is a credited fact before it's ever a reading.
* [[Another Way II Heaven]] (*33*, 2022, written by Roy alone), a tiny creation myth set to music: *"I spoke the words 'One, become Two'… Then 'I' was in reverse… And 'I' became 'You.'"* The flip stated out loud, on the album.
* The distortion's built-in tell: the Happy Face emoji as actually deployed is 🙃, the upside-down smiley, and the [[Happy Face]] video's CRT shows an inverted face. A grin announcing that it's wearing its opposite.

That is the surface, and it stands on its own. The drawers go one reading deeper at a time.

> [!map]- Written on the man
> The reversal grammar isn't a frame we bring; Roy keeps stating it. His X bio reads *"There's two sides to every story,"* the flip as a standing slogan. A 2019 Facebook post, years before this era, says it plainest: *"Everything is a mirror,"* echoed in 2026 as *"Compassion is a mirror."* The feed runs reversal riddles (*"how do you reverse a mirror?"* answered with *"The answer is in all of your reflections"*).
>
> And it's inked. On the clavicle, under *Fear*: *"[FEAR] Functions like Prayer in Reverse,"* with his gloss that fear drives us from our destiny, so he acts out of love instead. That is the cleanest grounded statement of inversion anywhere: faith running backward, written on his body. Nearby, the word *rose* inked mirror-reversed into ROSE ESOR around a central **+**, and the backward code *Rorrim Ehtni Lived*, "Devil in the Mirror" spelled in reverse, a self-naming that predates the project by more than a decade. A strong read would go further and call the whole body a mirror diagram, right side reflected onto left around that central axis; hold that one as a read, not a receipt.

> [!reading]- One, become Two
> The clearest statement is a song, not marginalia. [[Another Way II Heaven]] hands you the mechanism in a few lines: the self speaks, splits, runs in reverse, and becomes a *You*, *"A Darkness and a Light,"* and *"When we crashed and came together / A Sun was born inside."* A creation story that is secretly a description of looking in a mirror.
>
> The same poem sits, word for word, on Roy's publicly posted [[Concepts/The I'm 33 Board|"I'm 33" board]], his own conceptual spreadsheet, so we get to watch him compose the identical myth twice: once as a song, once as a cell counting toward *"The Great Design."* And notice the tense of the ending: *"One day we'll get back together / And see The Great Design."* One day. The reunion is the hope the whole apparatus points at, and the song is honest enough to leave it in the future.

> [!mask]- The twin that walked off
> Read this way, the flip is [[Roy]] becoming [[Sir Lucius]]: the source self looks in the glass, inverts, and the reflection walks off as the twin. A strong read, but the funhouse logic fits him uncannily well. *"While Sir Lucius only tells the truth, the truth can be deceiving 🍏"* ([[Public Posts]]), which is exactly the funhouse trick: a distorted mirror doesn't lie, it shows you something true but warped, longer, meaner, more frightening than the real thing.
>
> And here's the tell that he really is a mirror and not a man: he can't hold a fixed shape. Cold and imperial at the premiere podium, almost human across the table from Roy in [[The Interview]], a bumbling near-gangster in the looser skits. It looks like inconsistent writing until you notice the mechanism: a funhouse surface reflects whoever leans in. His deeper move, the reflection that stops warping and starts claiming to *be* the source, waits in the Deep End.

> [!reading]- The funhouse, not the pane
> The second operation. A single mirror is faithful; a hall of mirrors is a funhouse, and the funhouse is the point. The self comes back refracted three ways: as **masks** (the 🙃 tell, the grin laughing at you while announcing it's upside-down), as **projections** (*"people are made up in your head"*: we build others out of our own contents and warp each other before a word is said), and as **spectacle**, the performed self and the distorting feed, the modern funhouse with a Like button (see [[The Circus]] and [[The Algorithm]]).
>
> Which is why the distortion can teach. A caricature exaggerates the truth; you confront the warped projections precisely in order to recover the real face. The [[Hall of Mirrors]] makes this literal, a funhouse you can actually walk. It's a maze you move through, not a mirror you glance into.

> [!reading]- Flipping back
> The hope the whole project is built on, and the easiest thing on this page to overstate, so hold it precisely. If inversion made the split, the flip should be reversible. But what reverses is the *mistake*, not the twin. The mirror doesn't get smashed; it goes back to knowing it's a mirror. The mind isn't killed; it's put back in service to the heart, the published gate-mantra of the LOOP era: *"my mind must serve my heart."*
>
> So integration here reads as a marriage, not a merger. The polarity is kept: Roy holds *"two sides to every story"* in his bio, and his board transcribes the Kybalion's Principle of Polarity a few cells from a note on how a battery works, current flowing only while the poles are held apart. Wire the poles together and you don't get peace, you get a dead cell and a little heat. *"One day we'll get back together"*: an embrace, not a funeral, and *together* still takes two. In the released work this remains a horizon. Sung toward, never shown done.

> [!question]- Left open on purpose
> **Does the flip actually reverse?** The Loop-to-Circle integration, mind re-subordinated to heart, is the project's stated hope, but the catalogue hasn't staged it; Lucius still reads like the final boss. How that resolves constrains everything else on this page. **Are the flip and the distortion always separable in practice**, or do they collapse into each other in specific songs and images? Worth auditing each example for which mechanism is actually operating, not for mood: a reversed prayer isn't automatically distorted, and a warped copy may be incomplete rather than flipped. **And the built-in falsifier:** a prominent reflection with no ethical charge, plain vanity or aesthetic doubling, would show the two-operator machine is narrower than the motif it claims to organize. We keep that possibility on the table on purpose.
