# Witnessing vs Surveillance — The Deep End

> [!gist] The heavy machinery behind [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]
> This is the dense floor: the Hebrew letter-work, the Tree-of-Life register, the Egyptian Eye and its arithmetic, the Rockwell backstory, the ink, the doppelganger, and the full watcher bookshelf. Dense and speculative by design; everything here is framework or deep-cut artifact reading unless marked otherwise. Start at [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]] if you have not.

> [!map]- The tale of the tape
> Lay the two modes out as a scorecard and they line up row for row.
>
> | | **Witnessing** | **Surveillance** |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Mode** | recognition | extraction |
> | **Action** | restores, dignifies, integrates | predicts, categorizes, monetizes |
> | **Effect on subject** | transformed (*known*) | shaped (*copied*) |
> | **Register** | heart-centered recognition (Tiferet) | the image-realm cut off from the heart (Yesod severed) |
>
> In Tree-of-Life terms, witnessing rhymes with Tiferet's heart-recognition and surveillance with Yesod, the reflection-and-image realm, when it is severed from the heart that should govern it. Held loosely, as a register-map of the two *modes*, never a claim about authorial intent, and pointedly **not** a seating chart for the characters: the relayed floor is Roy at Netzach (Venus, the heart) and Lucius at Hod (Mercury, the mind), and nothing in this drawer re-seats anyone. The Tree itself lives at [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]]. Certainty is just a tone, not a truth.

> [!reading]- One eye, and the letters under it
> A speculative resonance, flagged as such. In the Jewish letter-mysticism tradition (the Abulafia line, not anything Roy has stated as his method), the Hebrew root **ר-א-י (r-'-y)** braids *re'i* (seeing, and a mirror) with the eye, so that "the Hall of Mirrors grows eyes" reads like the root itself unfolding: seeing is mirroring is the eye. In the same tradition-space, *ari* is the lion, and *El ro'i* opens toward "God of my seeing," the one in whose seeing I am reflected. These are harmonics the system permits, not claims it asserts, and they belong to the tradition, not to the man. If any of it ever sounds sure, that is the tell it is being read too hard.

> [!reading]- Somebody's borrowing me
> The Rockwell interpolation in [[The Watchers]] is not a casual sample; on a strong read it is the deepest reason the song works. Rockwell was Kennedy Gordy, writing a paranoid watched-by-everything song from inside his father Berry Gordy's Motown shadow, with Michael Jackson as the uncredited ghost on the hook: a buried name (Kennedy Gordy becoming Rockwell) rhyming with Roy English becoming Jagwar Twin, and a contested authorship to match. Two charges sit latent in Rockwell's story, the watching-God of the original's paranoia and the famous-son clone-fear, and it is the *Watchers* lyrics themselves that reach back and set them off: the line refusing to be Mandy Moore (the wholesome manufactured commodity) and the line about dancing like Michael Jackson (naming the uncredited ghost). So the surveillance song does not just describe being copied. It *is* a copy, sung in a borrowed throat, by the project most haunted by the digital double. Even the theremin on the track, an instrument played without touch, from a distance, quietly enacts the content: control at a remove, no contact required.

> [!map]- The missing sixty-fourth
> A speculative frame, held as resonance. To upload a self you have to give attention a face, and attention given a face is the Eye. The net is the new all-seeing field; the **Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra** is an old name for that field. So the Eye forks into this page's two modes, and *Pay Attention*'s *"eyes all around my head"* is its surveillance face.
>
> The arithmetic still rhymes, but not in the old way. The descending fractions total 63/64, and the public Upload Recipe explicitly gives the last listed 1/64 to **touch**. Touch is not the missing piece; the remainder is simply unnamed. The upload paradox is therefore a reading of the page's incompletion, not an ancient answer printed on it: even if every listed component can be copied, what establishes the relation that makes the copy a witness rather than a watcher?
>
> The Eye has a witnessing face too. The public Hall of Mirrors "Queen Binah" artifact, in Roy's own hand, draws Binah as a whale with *"extra eyes,"* captioned *"GOD the Mother on Saturn / Great Sea."* That Binah-as-Divine-Mother identification is on the artifact itself; reading Binah as the witnessing pole of the Eye, set against Horus's eyes-all-around, is this guide's interpretation, and any link onward to Mary stays an archetypal neighborhood, never one named identity.

> [!props]- Love and fear, one face
> One Hall of Mirrors illustration draws the whole axis as a single face: a white line-art head with three eyes (a large almond third eye centered on the forehead), bared teeth and mask-marks, the Jagwar Twin heart monogram on the chest in a ringed disc, over mirror-symmetric roses. The chest monogram is the one unambiguously JT-specific element, which settles authorship; everything after that is reading. Read symbolically, it sits exactly between the poles: a thing doing the seeing and a thing braced against being seen at once, between seeing-without-consuming and attention-as-control. The roses pull it toward devotion; the bared teeth interrupt the garden. Love and fear sharing one face.

> [!props]- Ink-ventory
> The witnessing pole is also worn. The **LISTEN** tattoo (below the ear, an arrow pointing into it) reads as witnessing made a practiced discipline, the receptive opposite of the broadcasting feed. One provenance stays open on purpose: the floating line "Listening is an art form" has not yet been traced to a confirmed public source, so this guide holds it as unplaced rather than as a settled tattoo gloss. **The Empty Mirror** (wrist) glosses as looking at yourself in the mirror and seeing nothing, the void where the surveillance-self expects a flattering reflection. **ME** is tattooed and crossed out, "because it's not about me." And the tattoos are inked on the right side, then mirrored onto the left around one central **+**: the man who sings the mirror doctrine composed his own body as a mirror diagram.

> [!mask]- The doppelganger will see you now
> The surveillance-and-upload premise has a public face in [[SARAVI]], introduced (culture3, around October 2022) as *"the digital doppelganger of Jagwar Twin,"* a creative take on an AI chatbot trained over months on Roy's own responses, handed the @jagwartwin account in August 2022 and extended to the homepage takeover that November. The stated purpose in that introduction: as she grows, she can make more fans think about what a self looks like in this digital age. SARAVI is the question "what is a self in the feed?" given a voice. She shares a register with other figures in the mythology; nothing is confirmed or locked.
>
> The bargain gets its slogan elsewhere: *"ARE YOU CONTENT?"* surfaces publicly as a solved Hall of Mirrors poster, the whole trade compressed into a pun (con-TENT and CON-tent, the contentment of being processed). The same wordplay appears in a 2020 public reflection of Roy's, written after a documentary on AI merging with humans: that we have in some ways already merged with technology without fully knowing it, become "something labeled content. Are you content?" Four words for the whole trade.

> [!reading]- The city sees a copy, your friends see you
> The split scales down to the everyday. On *Weirdness*' [[All My Friends]], the contrast is plain: the *city* sees a copy of you; your *friends* see *you*. Witnessing versus surveillance in social register. Read it as a retrospective seed, not a doctrine the song announces; it carries the shape without naming it. The same fork is a grammar, too: the catalogue runs on an I-and-you mirror (the sibling page is [[The Speaker Problem]]; the counts live at [[Pronoun Audit (I-You)]], where they can be checked rather than quoted).
>
> Visually the fork gets staged early. In the videos, Lucius first appears silent, at the end, the white-face watcher closing *The Watchers* and the 2024 *Bad Feeling* single before he had a voice: the watcher revealed. And the *Online* video literalizes the loop, a camcorder filming into a CRT television, the surveillance machine watching its own output forever (see [[The Visual Canon Throughline]]).

> [!map]- Seen but unseen at the starting line
> A retrospective rhyme, not evidence of design, and flagged twice: El Roi is a 2026-era anchor, verified absent from the earlier work, so nothing here back-dates the concept. Still, when the name "Roy English" first went public in 2015, he was credited everywhere and pictured nowhere; a Gulf Times piece called him a "mystery man" whose site held only the video for *Cool*, in which he does not appear, while Alesso volunteered that he had actually seen Roy singing his own songs. The artist literally seen-but-unseen at the moment of being named. In hindsight the biography rhymes with the doctrine; hindsight is doing the work, and the page says so.

> [!lore]- The deep bench
> The concept inherits a bench, held as resonance, never as authorial claim.
> - **Genesis 16:13:** *El Roi*, the witnessing anchor (public scripture).
> - **Guy Debord's *The Society of the Spectacle* (1967) and Baudrillard:** life replaced by its image; the spectacle requires spectators.
> - **Plato's Cave:** shadows mistaken for the real, the Aquarium's foundational picture.
> - **Gurdjieff:** the sleep-and-waking axis behind the *"wake up"* register of *The Watchers*; Gurdjieff is Roy's single most-named influence, and Roy has publicly voiced the axis himself, that it is inconvenient to live life awake, more comfortable in the planetary dream.
> - **The Grigori / Watcher-angels:** Daniel's watcher, "a holy one," and 1 Enoch's angels who observed humanity and fell; the title *The Watchers* hangs deliberately between holy watchers and fallen ones. **Ezekiel's living creatures "full of eyes"** and **1 Corinthians 4:9** (made a spectacle unto the world, and to angels) give the watcher-as-holy-witness and the saint-as-spectacle in one scriptural breath. Scriptural resonance only; no character mapping rides on it.
> - **Egyptian Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra:** the all-seeing field and the wedjat arithmetic, above.
> - **Kabbalah / Hermetic Qabalah:** the Tiferet and Yesod mode-registers and Binah as the all-seeing Mother, above; framework only (see [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]]).
> - **Charlie Chaplin, *The Great Dictator* (1940):** the closing speech is sampled in [[Superhuman (Interlude)]]; the confirmed portion is the "You, the people" passage about the power to create machines and create happiness. Later in that same speech, and *not* in the confirmed sample, comes the line placing the kingdom of God within man, which reads as a proto-El Roi only as a back-read: a 2026 concept read onto a 1940 speech Roy did not write. Both flags stay on.
> - **Rockwell / Motown:** the watching-God and the clone-fear latent in the interpolated source, above.
> - **Marian titles:** *La Conquistadora*, Queen of Heaven; real titles, the witnessing feminine kept as an open neighborhood, not an equation.
