# The Colors

> **One-line reading.** Jagwar Twin is unusually disciplined about color. A small set of colors carries the same meaning almost everywhere it appears, so once you know the little map, the videos and artwork start reading like sentences.

> [!infobox] The Colors
> - **What it is** — a small, repeated color code across the art, videos, and wardrobe
> - **The source** — Roy's own ten-color ladder on [[The I'm 33 Board]]
> - **The one rule** — color means the most inside the ladder; elsewhere, treat it as mood
> - **Related** — [[The I'm 33 Board]] · [[Sir Lucius]] · [[Roy]] · [[BMRC]] · [[The Green Apple]] · [[The Moon]]

## At a glance

- **White** — the top of the ladder, spirit, the crown. Roy's team wears white hearts.
- **Black** — the bottom-and-shadow end. [[Sir Lucius]]'s team wears black hearts, and Lucius is dressed in black almost always.
- **Gold-yellow** — the heart and the sun, the center of the ladder.
- **Green** — the heart's living color (the [[The Green Apple|green apple]] is that color held in the wrong hand).
- **Red** — the ringmaster's coat, a role rather than a person: whoever runs the show wears it.
- **Teal** — the underground, the dream, the flooded place (the [[The Moon|moon]]-lit garage).

## The ladder underneath it all

Everything starts on [[The I'm 33 Board]], where Roy draws a ten-rung ladder of color from matter at the bottom to spirit at the top. The bottom rung is dark and earthy, the top rung is pure white, and the heart sits in gold-yellow in the middle. That ladder is the dictionary the rest of the project keeps quoting: white for the crown, gold for the heart, black for the shadow.

## Two teams, one line down the middle

The clearest use is the two hearts Roy posts: white hearts for Team Roy, black hearts for Team [[Sir Lucius]]. You can even see it worn on the body. In the interviews, both men wear nail polish that is black on one hand and white on the other. The split does not just divide the two of them, it runs through each of them.

## Red is a costume, not a person

There is only one red coat, and both Roy and Lucius have worn it (Roy wore it to sing a Lucius song). A color that either of them can put on is not either one's color, it is the color of the job: running the show. When a room turns red in the story, someone is taking the stage over.

## Gold and teal, the two lights

The live show is lit warm gold. The underground scenes, where Roy is brought down into the garage, are washed teal, the color of water and the [[The Moon|moon]]. Watch which light a scene is in and you usually know whether you are seeing the show or the dream.

## The rainbow, whole and broken

On Roy's side the rainbow is whole: a halo, an arc, the full spectrum of a person. On Lucius's side the same rainbow comes back shattered, a little stained-glass mosaic sewn onto a black sweater. That break is the whole idea behind the name [[BMRC|Black Magic Rainbow Circus]]: the whole self, taken apart and put on display.

## Source notes

- **Grounded.** The two-hearts posts, the wardrobe, the video lighting, and the ten-color ladder on [[The I'm 33 Board]] are from public posts, official videos, and a public artifact.
- **Framework, not artist-stated.** Naming the ladder the Golden Dawn "Queen scale" of the Tree of Life is a correspondence the board *fits*, not a label Roy uses. This page is a basic symbol map for readers, not a claimed master key.
- **The honest limit.** Color means the most inside the ladder and the team-hearts. In the busy flow of a lyric sheet or a crowded collage, a colored thing is often just mood. Do not force every color to mean something.
