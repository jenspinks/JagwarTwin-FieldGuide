# Water

> **One-line reading.** Water is the catalogue's element of emotion and love — Roy's own key says so — and like [[Fire|the fire]] it refuses to pick a side: the same water drowns, stagnates, or carries you home, depending entirely on whether it is still moving. As of July 8, 2026, the feed states the equation outright: *"Love is water."*

## At a glance

- **What it does** — dissolves the walls between people (the flood as cure), sits in the tank going nowhere (the aquarium as trap), or completes the circle (the cycle as return). Water is the [[Concepts/The Loop and the Circle|Loop/Circle]] test poured into liquid form.
- **Carried by** — [[Roy]]. In his own four-element key, *"water being emotion"* — the heart's medium, opposite the mind's [[Fire|fire]]. And, since the July 2026 post, by love itself.
- **Appears in** — the debut's very title, *[[Subject to Flooding]]* · the 2026 water-line posts (*"Enter the water," "Baptize America," "I am the shore," "The waters are rising," "The ocean does not need to perform," "Love is water"*) · [[Concepts/The Aquarium|The Aquarium]] · the teal "flooded place" light in [[The Colors]] · the board's *"bottomless well"* and the Hero's-Journey wheel's own **"Water"** station · [[SOL]]'s solar counterpart.
- **Confidence** — the element-assignment and the dated posts are grounded; the three-registers split is a strong reading; the traditions are framework, labeled; the film-resonance is resonance, labeled.

## What it does

Three registers, one element.

**The flood — water arriving all at once.** Roy's stated meaning for the debut title holds the doubleness on purpose: with the world fragile, *"we should all let go of ego… After all, we are all Subject to Flooding."* We can all drown, *and* the walls between us can all come down — vulnerability and communion as the same exposure. What the flood destroys is the separation the record diagnoses as the disease. *"Jericho fell like a wave."* The title contains its own remedy (see [[Subject to Flooding]] for the full argument).

**The tank — water held.** *"Everyone's a guru. We are now fully in the aquarium"* (April 2026). The fish's medium is invisible because it never had another one; the tank filters and recirculates, and the water goes nowhere. This is water in a Loop: motion without return. The staging agrees — the underground scenes wash teal, the color [[The Colors]] already glosses as *the flooded place*. And [[Concepts/Nigredo|the alchemical page]]'s keystone warning applies here in liquid: the Circus specializes in a dark — and a water — that never moves.

**The cycle — water moving.** The ocean is scattered into billions of drops by the sun; every drop falls, flows, and attempts the trip back to the source; some are dammed, bottled, tanked, frozen — and still the attempt never stops; what returns is lifted and scattered again. Return *with* transformation, since evaporation is a purification: the cycle is the [[Concepts/The Loop and the Circle|Circle]] running as weather. The Loop/Circle test transfers verbatim — held water repeats in place while concealing its repetition; the cycle repeats with the possibility of return.

## "Love is water"

The post is three words, complete: **"Love is water"** — @JagwarTwin on X, **July 8, 2026**, 18:32 UTC, no image, no link (post ID 2074924634466857428; verified via a same-minute Internet Archive capture). It caps a running series — *"Love requires tremendous courage"* (June 4), *"Love is the highest paying work there is"* (June 9) — and a year of water-language on the same feed: *"Enter the water"* (Feb 15, reply), *"The ocean does not need to perform"* (Mar 11), *"Baptize America"* (Apr 8), *"I am the shore. I am not the wave."* (Apr 10), *"The waters are rising but so am I"* (Apr 13).

Read against the album it follows, and this is a reading, not a claim: [[SOL]] leaves the record on *"Love. Love is a question"* — then strips even those words away. Three and a half months later the same voice posts a three-word predicate. The question, answered in the grammar it was asked in: not an explanation (that was [[Move To You]], 2018), not a paradox (that was SOL), but an element.

**The sentence has a thirteenth-century twin.** Not a resonance — a near-identical statement, in a tradition of the kind Roy reads. Rumi, in the *Divan-i Shams*, translated by R. A. Nicholson:

> *Prize not at all life that has passed without love;*
> **Love is the Water of Life:** *receive it in thy heart and soul.*

The Persian is ***āb-i ḥayāt ast 'ishq*** — literally *love is the water of life*. (Nicholson, *Selected Poems from the Dīvāni Shamsi Tabrīz*, Cambridge, 1898, Notes p. 216; the *āb-e ḥayāt* is an authentic Persian-Sufi motif, tied to Khidr and the fountain in the Land of Darkness.) Nothing suggests Roy is quoting it — no source connects him to Nicholson or the *Divan*. What it establishes is that *"Love is water"* is a **statable position** with seven centuries of standing, not an aphorism improvised for a feed.

The equation has roots in his own record. In a 2021 interview Roy cited **Masaru Emoto's** water-crystal experiments — water shown words of love and gratitude forming harmonious crystals. (Flag kept in view: Emoto's claims are scientifically unsupported; what the citation grounds is that Roy has been thinking love-*into*-water since at least 2021, not that the crystals are real.) Add *"A single drop of rain can be the start of a flood"* (2020) and the 2018 title-gloss, and the July post stops reading like a mood and starts reading like a thesis finally compressed.

## Roy's own element-key (grounded)

The reason this page gets to exist at all: Roy states the assignment himself — *"fire being consciousness, **water being emotion**, air being thought, Earth being the physical reality you can touch"* — and places water in the West in his stated elemental directions (see [[Fire]] for the full quote and sourcing). So *"Love is water"* is not an imported correspondence; it is an equation inside his own published system. Love = the emotion-element. The heart's medium, as fire is the mind's.

## The two birds and the two waters

The [[The Crow|crow]]/[[The Dove|dove]] split runs straight through this element. Corvus, the crow constellation, is fixed in the sky beside Crater, the cup — eternally within sight of the water it was sent to fetch and can never drink: the mind's unquenchable thirst. The Hall of Mirrors' white **dove on the chalice** is the exact counter-image: the heart reaching the very cup the crow is denied. And Genesis 8 splits the same way over the flood itself — the raven that goes to and fro and never lands, the dove that returns with the olive leaf. The two birds are the two possible relations to this element: thirst and drink, orbit and return.

> [!note]- Advanced reading — the traditions (framework, not authorial intent)
> - **Thales.** The first recorded thesis of Western philosophy: the *arche* — the source-principle — is water. (Via Aristotle's report, *Metaphysics* 983b; Thales left no writings, so cite the report, not a quote.) Empedocles then named **Love** the force that draws the elements back into one. *"Love is water"* welds the first physics to the first metaphysics in three words.
> - **The Emerald Tablet.** The One Thing *ascends from the earth to the heaven and again descends to the earth* (Newton's rendering). The Hermetic circulation reads as rain before it reads as anything else — as-above-so-below not as a maxim but as a *mechanism*, sky-water and earth-water exchanging places. The board's own Kybalion **Rhythm** cell opens on flow (see [[Concepts/The Sacred Truths/Hermeticism - As Above So Below]]).
> - **Alchemy.** *Solve et coagula* is a water cycle in Latin — dissolve (scatter) and coagulate (gather) — and the laboratory operation *circulatio*, distillation repeated in the pelican flask, is a literal bench-top rain-cycle: evaporation and condensation until pure. The **aqua permanens**, the "permanent water," is Mercurius in liquid phase, and Jung calls it the *medium of conjunction* — the solvent in which separated opposites reunite (*Mysterium Coniunctionis*, CW 14). Note who else Mercurius is: the *caput corvi*, the raven's head of the nigredo (*Psychology and Alchemy*, CW 12 ¶404). **The crow and the water are one spirit in two phases** — the dissolver and the carrier home. On this frame, the feed's two 2026 correspondences — *"I would map a crow onto Mercury"* (April 7) and *"Love is water"* (July 8) — are two halves of a single formula. See [[The Crow]].
> - **Kabbalah.** **Chesed — Love, Mercy — carries the element water**; Gevurah carries fire. This is not only a Golden-Dawn tabulation: it is attested independently in traditional Jewish Kabbalah — the Zohar (I:119b–120a) has *"water [chesed, kindness] blended with fire [gevura, din]"*, and the Arizal via Chaim Vital sets **water–Abraham–chesed–South** against **fire–Isaac–gevurah–North**. What *is* Hermetic is the systematization (a full element-column cross-indexed to tarot and astrology). So in the tradition Roy demonstrably shelves, *love is water* is close to a table row — though that the post intends the sephira is never claimed. (One wrinkle worth keeping, and it illustrates the [[Concepts/The Sacred Truths/Hermeticism - As Above So Below|don't-cross-the-streams]] rule: Roy's own stated directions put **fire** in the South; the Arizal puts **water** there. The systems disagree.)
> - **Campbell.** The *I'm 33* board's Hero's-Journey wheel includes a station Roy hand-labeled **"Water"** (between *Dragon* and *Treasure*), *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* sits on the public shelf, and Campbell's own definition of the returned hero's boon is hydrological — the release again of the flow of life into the body of the world (*Hero*, prologue). One story, a thousand faces; one water, a thousand rivers; or, in the Manifesto's words, *"There's only one song."*
> - **The drop and the ocean.** The authentic Rumi is Masnavi IV:2617–22 (Nicholson), and it states the return-to-source outright: the drop, afraid of wind and earth, *"has leaped into the sea, **which was its source**… its outward form has disappeared in the sea, but its essence is inviolate and permanent."* It closes on a reversal worth holding — *"A Sea has become the suitor for a drop"*: the source courts the fragment, not only the reverse. Attar's Valley of Nothingness gives the other pole — *"A drop sunk in the sea, and it is vain / To search the sea for that one drop again"* (trans. Darbandi & Davis) — and his thirty birds arrive to find the Simurgh is themselves. Beside them: Kabir's doha (*that the drop merges into the ocean, everyone knows; that the ocean merges into the drop, rarely does anyone understand*) and Ramakrishna's salt doll, which walked in to measure the sea and dissolved. **Citation guard:** the circulating *"you are the entire ocean in a drop"* Rumi attribution is apocryphal — it appears nowhere in the sourced corpus and *inverts* the direction of the authentic imagery, in which the drop surrenders into the sea. The feed already holds the fragment half in Roy's own words: *"We are drops of light"* (2023).
> - **Tao Te Ching 78 / Bruce Lee.** Nothing in the world is softer than water, and nothing better at wearing down the hard — the Taoist root of Lee's be-water teaching (origin: the 1971 *Longstreet* episode and Pierre Berton interview, scripted by Lee's own student from Lee's doctrine; *not* Enter the Dragon). Water wins by yielding. Love as the soft thing that outlasts the hard is the same claim in the emotional register.

> [!note]- Advanced reading — the soul's water cycle, stage by stage (framework, never authorial intent)
> The comparison this page rests on is not new, and in several traditions it is not even metaphor — it is the stated **mechanism** of the soul's journey. What follows is the map, verified source by source. Nothing here is claimed as Roy's intent; the vault column records where his own material happens to land on an ancient diagram. (Terminology note: there is **no established scholarly term** for a water-cycle model of the soul. Eliade's own *"aquatic symbolism"* is the defensible phrase; *hydrotheology* is a real 1734 term meaning something else entirely, and a false friend here.)
>
> **Phase change: identity persisting through transformation.** Water can become ocean, vapour, cloud, rain, river, groundwater, ice, mist or dew **without ceasing to be water.** That, and not purification, is what the cycle actually demonstrates, and it is a large part of why water has been so durable a symbol for a One that becomes many without being lost in the becoming. Phase change offers a physical image of **identity surviving radical change of state.**
>
> Two guards, because an earlier version of this page overstated the case. The atmospheric cycle is **not** a purification machine: evaporation does separate water from salts and other non-volatile matter, but volatile compounds rise along with it, aerosols and pollutants are scavenged into cloud and returned as rain, and *"every pass purifies"* is simply false. And transformation is not the same thing as change of state, in either direction: a traveller can be transformed without any change of material state, and water can change state without acquiring awareness, relationship or integration. So phase change does not define the Circle by itself. The deeper test stays where [[Concepts/The Loop and the Circle|the Loop page]] puts it, on what happens to the traveller: whether the return carries awareness, relationship, remembrance, and the real possibility of having changed. The aquarium is a trap not because it fails a physics test, but because nothing that goes around it is any different for the trip.
>
> **The neighbouring symbol, for orientation.** Water is especially good at modelling **unity persisting across differentiated forms**; blood is especially good at modelling **one life serving many members through a centre**. That asymmetry is useful for deciding which image a given question belongs to, and it is emphatically not a taxonomy: blood also carries covenant, kinship and sacrifice, and water also carries baptism, which is death-and-rebirth rather than emanation. Held as an orienting distinction only.

> **The oldest full version has all three of this page's registers.** The *pañcāgni-vidyā* of the **Chandogya Upanishad 5.3–5.10** (7th–6th c. BCE, pre-Buddhist; parallel at Brihadaranyaka 6.2) teaches rebirth *as* the water cycle. Müller's translation, 5.10.5–6: the returning self passes *"to the ether, from the ether to the air… he becomes smoke, having become smoke, he becomes mist… having become mist, he becomes a cloud, having become a cloud, **he rains down**. Then he is born as rice and corn."* Two roads are named — the *devayāna*, which does not return, and the *pitṛyāna*, which returns as rain. And then a third, at 5.10.8: *"On neither of these two ways those small creatures are continually returning of whom it may be said, **Live and die.** Theirs is a third place."* (Sanskrit: *jāyasva mriyasva* — "be born and die.") The reason given is the line worth carrying: **"Therefore that world never becomes full."** The source is never full *because* some fragments never complete the return — which is the Loop, given a name and an explanation, twenty-seven centuries early. Of those who land in food the text adds: *"from thence the escape is beset with most difficulties."*
>
> **The Greeks make the soul literally hydrological.** Heraclitus B36: *"For souls it is death to become water, for water death to become earth, but from earth water is born, and from water soul"* — with soul occupying fire's place in the circuit. Zeno takes it into Stoicism verbatim (Arius Didymus in Eusebius, *Praep. Ev.* XV.20): *"Zeno calls the soul an exhalation endowed with sensation, just as Heracleitus does… and souls likewise are exhaled from moisture."* Stage 1 is a definition for these writers, not an image. (Qualifier: on Chrysippus's account only *wise* souls persist to the conflagration; ordinary souls disperse earlier — so "reabsorption into the cosmic pneuma" is not uniform Stoic doctrine.) Porphyry then builds the descent on it in *De antro nympharum* 10: *"souls descending into generation **fly to moisture**… Heraclitus says, that moisture appears **delightful** and not deadly to souls"* — and at §5, *"souls proceeding into generation are the nymphs called naiades."* Note the valence: the fall is a *desire*, not a punishment. (That Heraclitus line is **DK B77**, transmitted only through this channel and therefore among the less-secure fragments.) Plotinus supplies the source-image, *Enneads* III.8.10: *"a spring that has no source outside itself; it **gives itself to all the rivers, yet is never exhausted** by what they take."* Set that against Roy, March 2026: *"Generosity is not sacrifice. It's proof that you believe supply is infinite."*
>
> **The initiates' version is a thirst.** The Orphic gold tablets (5th–4th c. BCE onward) put the dead soul's first crisis at a choice of water: avoid the spring by the white cypress; drink from **the lake of Mnemosyne**, Memory. The formula: *"I am the child of Earth and starry Heaven… but I am parched with thirst and I perish; give me quickly refreshing water flowing forth from the lake of Memory"* (Edmonds, Petelia tablet). The right water is the one that restores remembrance — which is [[The Circle]]'s mechanism exactly, recovery by remembering rather than acquiring. **Correction kept in view:** the tablets **never name Lethe**; only Mnemosyne is named, and the forbidden spring is unnamed. Say *"the unnamed spring, generally identified by scholars with Lethe."* (And note the rhyme with [[The Crow]]: Corvus, fixed beside the cup it may never drink, is the same crisis with the answer withheld.)
>
> **Christian and Jewish circulation.** Eckhart's system is, in McGinn's phrase, a *"metaphysics of flow"* — *"the dynamic reciprocity of the 'flowing forth' of all things from God's hidden* grunt *and the 'flowing-back' or 'breaking-through' of the universe into essential identity with this divine source"* — with Sermon 5b's *"God's ground is my ground, and my ground is God's ground."* Ruusbroec teaches the same eternal out-and-back (the scholarly label *regiratio* is Van Nieuwenhove's, not Ruusbroec's own — he wrote Middle Dutch). Teresa of Avila builds the whole ladder of prayer out of irrigation (*Life*, chs. 11–22): the **well** hauled by hand, the **noria**, the diverted **stream**, and finally **rain**, *"when our Lord Himself waters it, without labour on our part"* — a gradient of *decreasing effort*, where union is the stage you stop carrying water and it falls on you. And Lurianic Kabbalah runs the circuit both ways at once: ***mayin nukvin***, the "feminine waters" rising from below as human longing, drawing down ***mayin dukhrin***, the "masculine waters" descending in response. The Islamic register agrees by vocabulary — in the Quran *rahma*, mercy, **denotes** the rain itself (7:57, 25:48: the winds sent *"before His mercy"*). (Guard: the popular claim of an Arabic etymological link between *rahma* and "moist" is false — the root is r-ḥ-m, as in *raḥim*, womb. The link is theological, not etymological.)
>
> **Where the traditions diverge, which is much earlier than the end.** The table above is **this guide's comparative construction**, not a doctrine any tradition teaches. These sources can be laid alongside parts of a hydrological sequence; they do not jointly hold one water-cycle system that branches only at the finish. They disagree on whether the soul is a stable entity at all, on whether incarnation is a fall, a delight, an ignorance, an emanation or a divine play, on whether individuality is real, provisional or illusory, on what return even means (dissolution, communion, remembrance, judgment, rebirth, transformed embodiment), and on whether a cycle is morally neutral, salvific, tragic or meaningless. The agreements are analogical and partial, and saying so is the price of using the table at all.
>
> The **sharpest single divergence**, and the one nearest this project, concerns the re-scattering. **Vedanta** reads the rain-return as bondage: the point is to reach the sea and stay. **Ruusbroec, the Neoplatonists, and the bodhisattva vow** read the flowing-out as the divine life itself, so that return-and-pour-back-out is the shape of love rather than a failure of it. **Ecclesiastes 1:7** supplies a third position, the cycle as futility, since the dominant scholarly reading of that verse is weariness rather than renewal (it feeds straight into *"all things are full of weariness"*). Worth keeping: the same diagram supports despair. Roy's *"I am the shore. I am not the wave"* sits interestingly against all three, declining the transient form without claiming the sea. Whether that is an answer to this fork, or simply a different image reached independently, is left open.
>
> **What is uncommon in the Jagwar Twin version.** The familiar **drop-and-ocean** traditions (Rumi, Attar, Kabir, Ramakrishna) cast individual beings as drops within a divine or ultimate sea. That is a claim about *those* traditions, not a summary of every source above: water is elsewhere matter and generation (Porphyry), mercy (the Quran), the unconscious (Jung), grace (Teresa), an elemental attribution (Chesed), or a model of yielding (Tao 78). Heraclitus runs the other way outright, since in B36 it is **death** for a soul to become water. Set against the drop-and-ocean group specifically, *"Love is water"* shifts the emphasis from the **traveller** to the **medium that joins travellers**: love is the medium, souls are the drops. Re-read the stages with that substitution and each rung says something different — the sun that lifts you is love, the fall into matter is love taking a shape, the flow home is love seeking its own level, dissolution is love rejoining love with the name burned off. The precedents for *that* move are thinner and grander: Empedocles' **Philotēs**, Love as the cosmic force drawing the four roots into one against Neikos; rain-as-*rahma*; Chesed's element; Rumi's *āb-i ḥayāt ast 'ishq*; and the last line of the *Commedia*, *"l'amor che move il sole e l'altre stelle"* — *"The Love which moves the sun and the other stars"* (Longfellow). Read against the feed, two posts resolve into one sentence: *we are drops of light* **+** *love is water* = **we are drops, and what we are drops of is love.** Which is why the Kush painting Roy captioned *"Egg"* — a sun cracking open over open water — holds both halves of the cycle in a single frame.
>
> **Moderns, cited carefully.** Thich Nhat Hanh's *No Death, No Fear* (2002) teaches the wave that stops fearing: *"if the wave bends down and touches her true nature she will realize that she is water"* (p. 23), and *"a wave does not have to die in order to become water"* (p. 106). > [!note]- Advanced reading — refraction: one light made visible through many situated vessels (framework, and deliberately not a stage)
> A rainbow makes unity perceptible through a multitude of **situated** refractions. Sunlight carries a spectrum that is not visible as separate colours. Each droplet refracts and internally reflects the light it receives, dispersing wavelengths in different directions. An observer standing in one place receives a narrow band from droplets at one angular position, and other bands from droplets at others. The arc appears only through the relation among **source, countless vessels, and a particular eye**, which is why two people standing apart are not seeing the same bow: they are not seeing the same water.
>
> Stated that carefully, it is an unusually good physical figure for the [[Hall of Mirrors]] doctrine, one light disclosed through many partial perspectives, none of them complete. It sits beside [[The Colors]], where Roy's rainbow is whole (a halo, an arc, the full spectrum of a person) and Lucius's returns as a mosaic sewn onto black, the same spectrum fragmented into ornament rather than into disclosure. It also gives a physical shape to Roy's *"You can't see something for someone else"* (2026-03-27), though the fit is a resonance and nothing stronger.
>
> **Three precisions the reading must not lose.** A droplet is not assigned one intrinsic colour; the narrowness is a fact about **viewing geometry**, not about the drop, and the drop carries no unique message of its own. A single droplet *can* disperse light into a spectrum under controlled conditions; what it cannot produce for an ordinary distant observer is the large atmospheric **arc**, so "no single drop makes a rainbow" is true of the arc and false of the dispersion. And a droplet **receives the full incident spectrum**, which is cleaner than saying it contains the whole light: there is no miniature sun inside it.
>
> **Why this is not a numbered stage.** Refraction is an optical event that may accompany precipitation, not a step in the hydrological sequence. Numbering it would make the stage table look more scientifically exact while making it less so. Held here at `[~]`, and as an open question rather than a mechanism: nothing in the released work connects rainbow optics to the rainbow in **BMRC**'s name, which is a site-metadata capture and Lucius's own corporate signature, not a statement of doctrine.
