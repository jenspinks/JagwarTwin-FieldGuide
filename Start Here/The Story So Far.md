# The Story So Far

You probably arrived with one song. "Happy Face," the one from the radio, the one that will not leave your head. Good. That song is the door.

Jagwar Twin is one man, a singer named Roy. But listen closely and there seem to be two voices in his songs. One is warm. One is sharp, a grinning showman in white face paint who calls himself Sir Lucius. Here is the secret the whole catalogue is built on. Roy and Lucius are, in the story, the same person. Most of the songs are the two of them talking. That's the whole engine; everything else here is detail.

Hold that, and the radio hit changes. "Happy Face" stops being a smile and becomes an instruction: put the mask on, and if you're sad, don't let it show. Someone in the song is giving that order. Someone else is trying to survive it.

Hear it before you read another word. Play [[The Circle]], then [[Happy Face]], then [[Welcome to the Circus]], then [[Weirdness]]. Four songs, in that order, and the shape of the argument is in your ears before anyone has to explain it.

Now, the sharp voice. [[Sir Lucius]] did not start as a villain, and the story never files him under one. Long ago, it seems, something hurt Roy badly, and a part of his own mind stood up to guard the wound. He saved Roy first. Then he took the stage. He is brilliant, funny, armored to the teeth, protective and dangerous in the same breath. He never lies, which is exactly the problem. The songs call his condition "truth and light divorced from heart": everything he says is correct, and none of it is kind. A protector can become a cage without ever meaning to.

[[Roy]] is the heart of the pair, the one still trying to choose. His signs are warmer: the heart itself, a sword, a dove he named Cygnus. And beneath them both there is a third voice: [[Mary]], a gentle presence whose face you never quite see. Her whole message fits in five words, "you were born to shine," and it is likely the one note the protector cannot absorb.

All of this plays out inside a ring called [[The Circus]]: the attention machine, the show that never closes, the place where they love it when you lose control. It looks like the music industry. It also looks a lot like your feed. The songs seem to say the tent lives inside the head too, and then go looking for an exit anyway.

The story so far comes in three acts. [[Subject to Flooding]] is the flood and the wound. [[33]] is waking up inside the Circus. [[Lucius Lullaby (album)|Lucius Lullaby]] is the strangest turn yet: the protector gets the microphone, top billing and all, and the long way home begins.

The two voices leave fingerprints everywhere: album covers, videos, a bird in the corner of a frame, an apple on a table. You don't need a decoder ring. You need our simple map, nothing more.

## The signs to watch for

| Voice | Watch for |
| --- | --- |
| Sir Lucius | green apple, crow (Corvus), cigarettes, "El Roi", the intellect, "truth and light divorced from heart" |
| Roy | dove (Cygnus), sword, heart, "El Roy" |

Now look at the two names hiding in that table. El Roi. El Roy. Near twins. That one inch of spelling holds the whole war.

That's the story so far. The songs are the trail, and this site is just a better flashlight. Pick a door.
